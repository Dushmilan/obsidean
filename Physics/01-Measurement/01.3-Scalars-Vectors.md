# 1.3 Scalars & Vectors

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 1, Topic 1.3

---

## 1. Scalars

**Definition:** Quantity with **magnitude only** (no direction).

| Quantity | Symbol | Unit | Dimensions |
|----------|--------|------|------------|
| Mass | $m$ | kg | M |
| Time | $t$ | s | T |
| Temperature | $T$ | K | Θ |
| Energy | $E, U, K$ | J | ML²T⁻² |
| Power | $P$ | W | ML²T⁻³ |
| Charge | $q$ | C | AT |
| Distance | $s$ | m | L |
| Speed | $v$ | m/s | LT⁻¹ |
| Work | $W$ | J | ML²T⁻² |
| Pressure | $p$ | Pa | ML⁻¹T⁻² |

**Operations:** Ordinary algebra. Add/subtract magnitudes directly.

---

## 2. Vectors

**Definition:** Quantity with **magnitude AND direction**.

| Quantity | Symbol | Unit | Dimensions |
|----------|--------|------|------------|
| Displacement | $\vec{s}, \vec{r}$ | m | L |
| Velocity | $\vec{v}$ | m/s | LT⁻¹ |
| Acceleration | $\vec{a}$ | m/s² | LT⁻² |
| Force | $\vec{F}$ | N | MLT⁻² |
| Momentum | $\vec{p}$ | kg·m/s | MLT⁻¹ |
| Electric field | $\vec{E}$ | N/C | MLT⁻³A⁻¹ |
| Magnetic field | $\vec{B}$ | T | MT⁻²A⁻¹ |
| Torque | $\vec{\tau}$ | N·m | ML²T⁻² |
| Angular momentum | $\vec{L}$ | kg·m²/s | ML²T⁻¹ |
| Current density | $\vec{J}$ | A/m² | AL⁻² |

**Representation:** Arrow — length ∝ magnitude, arrowhead = direction.

**Notation:** Bold ($\mathbf{v}$), arrow ($\vec{v}$), hat for unit vector ($\hat{v}$).

---

## 3. Vector Addition

### Triangle Law
Place tail of $\vec{B}$ at head of $\vec{A}$. Resultant $\vec{R} = \vec{A} + \vec{B}$ from tail of $\vec{A}$ to head of $\vec{B}$.

### Parallelogram Law
$\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$ from same point. Complete parallelogram. Diagonal = resultant.

### Properties
- **Commutative:** $\vec{A} + \vec{B} = \vec{B} + \vec{A}$
- **Associative:** $(\vec{A} + \vec{B}) + \vec{C} = \vec{A} + (\vec{B} + \vec{C})$
- **Zero vector:** $\vec{A} + \vec{0} = \vec{A}$
- **Negative:** $\vec{A} + (-\vec{A}) = \vec{0}$

---

## 4. Vector Subtraction

$$\vec{A} - \vec{B} = \vec{A} + (-\vec{B})$$

Reverse direction of $\vec{B}$, then add.

---

## 5. Resolution into Components

### 2D (Cartesian)
$$\vec{A} = A_x \hat{i} + A_y \hat{j}$$
$$A_x = A \cos\theta, \quad A_y = A \sin\theta$$
$$A = \sqrt{A_x^2 + A_y^2}, \quad \theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{A_y}{A_x}\right)$$

### 3D
$$\vec{A} = A_x \hat{i} + A_y \hat{j} + A_z \hat{k}$$
$$A = \sqrt{A_x^2 + A_y^2 + A_z^2}$$

**Direction cosines:**
$$\cos\alpha = \frac{A_x}{A}, \quad \cos\beta = \frac{A_y}{A}, \quad \cos\gamma = \frac{A_z}{A}$$
$$\cos^2\alpha + \cos^2\beta + \cos^2\gamma = 1$$

---

## 6. Addition by Components

$$\vec{R} = \vec{A} + \vec{B} = (A_x + B_x)\hat{i} + (A_y + B_y)\hat{j} + (A_z + B_z)\hat{k}$$

**Steps:**
1. Resolve each vector into $x, y, z$ components
2. Sum $x$-components, $y$-components, $z$-components separately
3. Find magnitude and direction of resultant

---

## 7. Scalar (Dot) Product

$$\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = AB\cos\theta$$

**Component form:**
$$\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = A_x B_x + A_y B_y + A_z B_z$$

**Properties:**
- Commutative: $\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = \vec{B} \cdot \vec{A}$
- Distributive: $\vec{A} \cdot (\vec{B} + \vec{C}) = \vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} + \vec{A} \cdot \vec{C}$
- $\vec{A} \cdot \vec{A} = A^2$
- $\vec{A} \perp \vec{B} \iff \vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = 0$

**Applications:**
- Work: $W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{s} = Fs\cos\theta$
- Component along $\vec{B}$: $\frac{\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}}{B}$
- Angle: $\cos\theta = \frac{\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}}{AB}$
- Power: $P = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{v}$

---

## 8. Vector (Cross) Product

$$\vec{A} \times \vec{B} = AB\sin\theta\ \hat{n}$$

**Direction:** Right-hand rule (fingers $\vec{A} \to \vec{B}$, thumb = direction)

**Component form:**
$$\vec{A} \times \vec{B} = \begin{vmatrix}
\hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\
A_x & A_y & A_z \\
B_x & B_y & B_z
\end{vmatrix}
= (A_y B_z - A_z B_y)\hat{i} + (A_z B_x - A_x B_z)\hat{j} + (A_x B_y - A_y B_x)\hat{k}$$

**Properties:**
- **Anti-commutative:** $\vec{A} \times \vec{B} = -\vec{B} \times \vec{A}$
- Distributive: $\vec{A} \times (\vec{B} + \vec{C}) = \vec{A} \times \vec{B} + \vec{A} \times \vec{C}$
- $\vec{A} \times \vec{A} = \vec{0}$
- $\vec{A} \parallel \vec{B} \iff \vec{A} \times \vec{B} = \vec{0}$
- $|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}| =$ area of parallelogram

**Applications:**
- Torque: $\vec{\tau} = \vec{r} \times \vec{F}$
- Angular momentum: $\vec{L} = \vec{r} \times \vec{p}$
- Magnetic force: $\vec{F} = q\vec{v} \times \vec{B}$
- Area: $\frac{1}{2}|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}| =$ area of triangle

---

## 9. Unit Vectors

$$\hat{i} = (1,0,0), \quad \hat{j} = (0,1,0), \quad \hat{k} = (0,0,1)$$

Any vector: $\vec{A} = A_x\hat{i} + A_y\hat{j} + A_z\hat{k}$

Unit vector along $\vec{A}$: $\hat{A} = \frac{\vec{A}}{A}$

---

## 10. Relative Velocity

$$\vec{v}_{AB} = \vec{v}_A - \vec{v}_B$$

Velocity of A relative to B.

**Example:** River crossing
- $\vec{v}_b$ = boat velocity in still water
- $\vec{v}_r$ = river velocity
- $\vec{v}_{b/g} = \vec{v}_b + \vec{v}_r$ (velocity relative to ground)

---

## 11. Problem Patterns (A/L)

| Pattern | Strategy |
|---------|----------|
| Resultant of multiple forces | Resolve each into components, sum components |
| Angle between two vectors | $\cos\theta = \frac{\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B}}{AB}$ |
| Perpendicular vectors | Dot product = 0 |
| Parallel vectors | Cross product = 0, or ratios of components equal |
| Work done by force | $W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{s}$ |
| Torque/moment | $\vec{\tau} = \vec{r} \times \vec{F}$ |
| Magnetic force | $\vec{F} = q\vec{v} \times \vec{B}$ |
| Area of triangle/parallelogram | $\frac{1}{2}|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}|$, $|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}|$ |
| Relative velocity | $\vec{v}_{rel} = \vec{v}_1 - \vec{v}_2$ |
| Component of $\vec{A}$ along $\vec{B}$ | $\frac{\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}}{B}$ |

## 12. Worked Examples

### Example 1: Resultant of three forces
$\vec{F}_1 = 3\hat{i} + 4\hat{j}\ \text{N}$, $\vec{F}_2 = -2\hat{i} + 5\hat{j}\ \text{N}$, $\vec{F}_3 = 1\hat{i} - 6\hat{j}\ \text{N}$

$$\vec{R} = (3-2+1)\hat{i} + (4+5-6)\hat{j} = 2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j}\ \text{N}$$
$$R = \sqrt{4+9} = \sqrt{13}\ \text{N}, \quad \theta = \tan^{-1}(1.5) \approx 56.3^\circ$$

### Example 2: Angle between vectors
$\vec{A} = 2\hat{i} - 3\hat{j} + \hat{k}$, $\vec{B} = \hat{i} + 2\hat{j} - 2\hat{k}$

$$\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = 2(1) + (-3)(2) + 1(-2) = -6$$
$$A = \sqrt{4+9+1} = \sqrt{14}, \quad B = \sqrt{1+4+4} = 3$$
$$\cos\theta = \frac{-6}{3\sqrt{14}} = -\frac{2}{\sqrt{14}} \Rightarrow \theta \approx 122^\circ$$

### Example 3: Torque
$\vec{r} = 2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j}\ \text{m}$, $\vec{F} = 4\hat{i} - 5\hat{j}\ \text{N}$

$$\vec{\tau} = \vec{r} \times \vec{F} = \begin{vmatrix}
\hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\
2 & 3 & 0 \\
4 & -5 & 0
\end{vmatrix} = (0)\hat{i} - (0)\hat{j} + (2(-5) - 3(4))\hat{k} = -22\hat{k}\ \text{N·m}$$
$\tau = 22\ \text{N·m}$ (clockwise)

### Example 4: River crossing
River flows east at $3\ \text{m/s}$. Boat speed in still water $4\ \text{m/s}$. Cross directly north.

$$\vec{v}_b = 4\hat{j}, \quad \vec{v}_r = 3\hat{i}$$
$$\vec{v}_{b/g} = 3\hat{i} + 4\hat{j} \Rightarrow v = 5\ \text{m/s}, \theta = \tan^{-1}(4/3) \approx 53^\circ \text{ north of east}$$

To cross perpendicular to banks:
$$\vec{v}_b = v_y\hat{j} + v_x\hat{i}, \quad v_x + 3 = 0 \Rightarrow v_x = -3$$
$$v_y = \sqrt{4^2 - 3^2} = \sqrt{7}\ \text{m/s}$$
Time to cross width $w$: $t = \frac{w}{\sqrt{7}}$

### Example 5: Magnetic force
$q = 2\ \text{C}$, $\vec{v} = 3\hat{i} + 4\hat{j}\ \text{m/s}$, $\vec{B} = 5\hat{k}\ \text{T}$

$$\vec{F} = q\vec{v} \times \vec{B} = 2 \begin{vmatrix}
\hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\
3 & 4 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 5
\end{vmatrix} = 2(20\hat{i} - 15\hat{j}) = 40\hat{i} - 30\hat{j}\ \text{N}$$

---

## 13. Quick Reference

**Dot:** $\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B} = A_xB_x + A_yB_y + A_zB_z = AB\cos\theta$

**Cross:** $\vec{A}\times\vec{B} = (A_yB_z - A_zB_y)\hat{i} + (A_zB_x - A_xB_z)\hat{j} + (A_xB_y - A_yB_x)\hat{k}$

**Magnitude:** $|\vec{A}| = \sqrt{A_x^2 + A_y^2 + A_z^2}$

**Unit vector:** $\hat{A} = \frac{\vec{A}}{|\vec{A}|}$

**Direction cosines:** $\cos\alpha = \frac{A_x}{A}$, etc.
