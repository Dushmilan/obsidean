# Quantum Nature of Light

## Definition

**Photoelectric effect:** $hf = \phi + KE_{max}$ — a photon's energy goes into the work function $\phi$ plus the electron's kinetic energy.

**De Broglie:** $\lambda = \frac{h}{mv}$ — every particle has a wavelength.

Constants: $h = 6.63\times10^{-34}$ J·s, $1$ eV $= 1.6\times10^{-19}$ J.

**Threshold frequency:** $f_0 = \phi/h$ — below it, no electrons, no matter the intensity.

## The Intuition

Light arrives in discrete packets (photons). One photon, one electron. Increase intensity → more photons → more current, but each electron still gets the same max KE. Only raising frequency gives individual electrons more energy — Einstein won the Nobel Prize for this, not relativity.

## The Physics

| Quantity | Equation | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Photoelectric | $hf = \phi + KE_{max}$ | energy bookkeeping |
| Threshold frequency | $f_0 = \frac{\phi}{h}$ | cutoff |
| De Broglie | $\lambda = \frac{h}{mv}$ | matter waves |
| Photon energy | $E = hf$ | quanta |

## Derivation

The photoelectric equation is energy conservation per photon. The cutoff exists because a photon below $\phi$ simply can't free an electron — classical wave theory had no explanation, which is what forced the quantum picture. De Broglie proposed $\lambda = h/p$ by symmetry with photons. [Full derivations: [[04.6-Electromagnetic-Waves]]]

## Method

1. Compute photon energy $hf$ (convert to eV if needed).
2. Subtract the work function: $KE_{max} = hf - \phi$.
3. Check against the threshold $f_0 = \phi/h$ — below it, no emission.

## Worked Examples

**Setup:** Light of $1.2\times10^{15}$ Hz on a metal with $\phi = 3.0$ eV. Find $KE_{max}$.

**Solution:** $E = \frac{6.63\times10^{-34}\times1.2\times10^{15}}{1.6\times10^{-19}} = 4.97$ eV. $KE_{max} = 4.97 - 3.0 = 1.97$ eV.

**Key insight:** Intensity changes current, frequency changes electron energy.

---

**Setup:** Find the de Broglie wavelength of an electron ($m = 9.1\times10^{-31}$ kg) at $10^6$ m/s.

**Solution:** $\lambda = \frac{6.63\times10^{-34}}{9.1\times10^{-31}\times10^6} = 7.3\times10^{-10}$ m.

**Key insight:** ~atomic scale — matter waves matter at quantum sizes, not everyday ones.

## Common Traps

- $KE_{max}$ is a *maximum* — most electrons lose energy on the way out
- Intensity vs frequency — don't swap their roles
- Work function is a property of the metal, not the light
- eV vs J — convert once, consistently
