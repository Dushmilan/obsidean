# Sound

## Definition

Sound is a **longitudinal** pressure wave in a medium. Speed in air:

$$v = 331 + 0.6\theta \ (\theta \text{ in }^\circ\text{C})$$

Intensity: $I = \frac{P}{4\pi r^2}$. Sound level: $\beta = 10\log\frac{I}{I_0}$, $I_0 = 10^{-12}$ W/m². Doppler shift: $f' = f\frac{v \pm v_o}{v \mp v_s}$.

## The Intuition

A Slinky: push one end forward and back repeatedly — a compression pulse travels down the coil while each coil only shifts locally. Sound does the same with air molecules.

## The Physics

| Quantity | Equation | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Speed in air | $v = 331 + 0.6\theta$ | °C |
| Intensity | $I = \frac{P}{4\pi r^2}$ | point source |
| Sound level | $\beta = 10\log\frac{I}{I_0}$ | dB |
| Doppler | $f' = f\frac{v \pm v_o}{v \mp v_s}$ | signs depend on motion |

## Derivation

Intensity follows from spreading power over a sphere. The decibel scale is logarithmic because the ear's response is roughly logarithmic — a $10\times$ intensity is +10 dB. Doppler: the wavelength is compressed/stretched by the relative motion. [Full derivations: [[Maths/Pure/01-Algebra/03-Logarithms/03-Logarithms]]]

## Method

1. Doppler: fix the sign convention — approach → higher $f$ (numerator + for observer moving toward, denominator − for source moving toward).
2. dB problems: $10\log$ the intensity ratio.
3. $I \propto 1/r^2$ — doubling distance quarters intensity.

## Worked Examples

**Setup:** A 440 Hz source moves toward an observer at 30 m/s ($v_{sound} = 340$ m/s). Observed frequency?

**Solution:** $f' = 440\times\frac{340}{340-30} = 440\times\frac{340}{310} \approx 483$ Hz.

**Key insight:** Approach raises the pitch; recession lowers it — the ambulance effect.

---

**Setup:** Two sounds have intensities $10^{-6}$ and $10^{-9}$ W/m². Level difference?

**Solution:** $\Delta\beta = 10\log\frac{10^{-6}}{10^{-9}} = 10\log10^3 = 30$ dB.

**Key insight:** A 1000× intensity ratio is only 30 dB — the scale compresses huge ranges.

## Common Traps

- Doppler sign conventions — the +/− must make the approaching case *higher*
- $10\log$ for sound level (power), but $20\log$ for amplitude ratios
- Sound needs a medium — no sound in a vacuum
- $I_0 = 10^{-12}$ W/m² is the hearing threshold reference
