# 5.7 Electromagnetic Waves

Maxwell's equations predicted something extraordinary: changing electric fields create magnetic fields, and changing magnetic fields create electric fields. This mutual generation sustains a wave that propagates through empty space at the speed of light. Light itself is an electromagnetic wave.

**The Intuition:** Imagine two people on opposite ends of a rope. One creates a wave by moving the rope up and down (electric field oscillation). The rope's response creates a side-to-side motion (magnetic field oscillation). These two oscillations sustain each other and travel down the rope. In EM waves, the "rope" is the electromagnetic field itself — no medium needed.

**The Math:**

Speed of EM waves from fundamental constants:

$c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0 \epsilon_0}}$

Wave relation connecting frequency, wavelength, and speed:

$c = f\lambda$

Electric and magnetic field amplitudes are linked:

$B_0 = \frac{E_0}{c}$

Intensity — power per unit area:

$I = \frac{E_0^2}{2\mu_0 c}$

**What does this mean for Physics?**

EM waves unify [[05-Electricity-Magnetism/05.1-Electric-Fields|electric]] and [[05-Electricity-Magnetism/05.4-Magnetic-Fields|magnetic]] phenomena into a single wave phenomenon. They explain [[04-Waves-Optics/04.6-Electromagnetic-Waves|light, radio, X-rays, microwaves]] — all the same physics at different frequencies. This prediction was the crowning achievement of Maxwell's theory and opened the door to modern physics.

---

**Setup:** Verify the speed of light from $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7}$ T·m/A and $\epsilon_0 = 8.85 \times 10^{-12}$ F/m.

**Solution:**

$c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi \times 10^{-7} \times 8.85 \times 10^{-12}}} = 3.0 \times 10^8\ \text{m/s}$

**Key insight:** The speed of light isn't an arbitrary constant — it's determined by two measurable properties of empty space ($\mu_0$ and $\epsilon_0$). This means light is an electromagnetic phenomenon, not something separate.
