# Digital Systems

## Definition

Digital systems convert between analog and digital, and process bits:

- **ADC resolution:** $\text{resolution} = \frac{V_{ref}}{2^n}$ — one bit = halved quantization error.
- **Quantization error:** $\pm \frac12$ LSB.

| ADC type | Speed | Complexity |
|----------|-------|------------|
| Flash | fast | high ($2^n - 1$ comparators) |
| Dual-slope | slow | low, noise-immune |

## The Intuition

An ADC slices a continuous voltage into $2^n$ steps. Each added bit doubles the levels and halves the error — but costs hardware. An 8-bit ADC gives 256 levels; 16-bit gives 65,536. The tradeoff drives every measurement-system design.

## The Physics

| Quantity | Equation | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Resolution | $\frac{V_{ref}}{2^n}$ | voltage per LSB |
| Quantization error | $\pm \frac12$ LSB | inherent |
| Max value | $2^n - 1$ | codes |
| Sampling | $f_s \ge 2f_{max}$ | Nyquist |

## Derivation

Resolution is the reference spread over $2^n$ codes. The $\pm\frac12$ LSB quantization error is the worst-case round-off when a continuous value lands between two codes. Nyquist's theorem: sample at least twice the highest frequency to avoid aliasing. [Full derivations: [[07.4-Logic-Gates]]]

## Method

1. ADC: $n$ bits → $2^n$ levels; resolution $= V_{ref}/2^n$.
2. Quantization error $= \pm$ half a level.
3. Choose bit count so the error meets the tolerance.

## Worked Examples

**Setup:** A 10-bit ADC with $V_{ref} = 5$ V. Resolution and error?

**Solution:** Resolution $= \frac{5}{2^{10}} = 4.88$ mV. Error $= \pm 2.44$ mV.

**Key insight:** Adding one bit halves the error but doubles the levels — cost vs precision.

---

**Setup:** A signal contains frequencies up to 20 kHz. Minimum sampling rate?

**Solution:** $f_s = 2\times20 = 40$ kHz (Nyquist) — audio CDs sample at 44.1 kHz.

**Key insight:** Below Nyquist, high frequencies alias into wrong signals.

## Common Traps

- $2^n$ levels but $2^n - 1$ maximum code
- Quantization error is $\pm\frac12$ LSB, not 1 LSB
- Sampling below Nyquist → aliasing
- Resolution ≠ accuracy (ADC resolution is only the step size)
