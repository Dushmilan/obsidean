# 1.3 Vector Identities Derivations

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 1, Topic 1.3

---

## 1. Basic Definitions

**Vector in 3D Cartesian:**
$$\vec{A} = A_x\hat{i} + A_y\hat{j} + A_z\hat{k}$$
$$|\vec{A}| = \sqrt{A_x^2 + A_y^2 + A_z^2}$$

**Unit vector along $\vec{A}$:**
$$\hat{A} = \frac{\vec{A}}{|\vec{A}|} = \frac{A_x\hat{i} + A_y\hat{j} + A_z\hat{k}}{\sqrt{A_x^2 + A_y^2 + A_z^2}}$$

---

## 2. Dot Product Identities

### 2.1 Definition
$$\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = |\vec{A}||\vec{B}|\cos\theta = A_x B_x + A_y B_y + A_z B_z$$

### 2.2 Commutative
$$\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = \vec{B} \cdot \vec{A}$$
*Proof:* $A_x B_x + A_y B_y + A_z B_z = B_x A_x + B_y A_y + B_z A_z$ ✓

### 2.3 Distributive
$$\vec{A} \cdot (\vec{B} + \vec{C}) = \vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} + \vec{A} \cdot \vec{C}$$
*Proof:* LHS = $A_x(B_x+C_x) + A_y(B_y+C_y) + A_z(B_z+C_z) = \dots$ ✓

### 2.4 Self dot product
$$\vec{A} \cdot \vec{A} = |\vec{A}|^2 = A_x^2 + A_y^2 + A_z^2$$

### 2.5 Orthogonal condition
$$\vec{A} \perp \vec{B} \iff \vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = 0$$

### 2.6 Component along direction
Component of $\vec{A}$ along $\vec{B}$: $A_{\parallel B} = \frac{\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}}{|\vec{B}|}$

### 2.7 Projection vector
$$\vec{A}_{\parallel B} = \left(\frac{\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}}{|\vec{B}|^2}\right)\vec{B}$$

## 3. Cross Product Identities

### 3.1 Definition
$$\vec{A} \times \vec{B} = |\vec{A}||\vec{B}|\sin\theta\ \hat{n}$$
where $\hat{n}$ is unit vector perpendicular to both by right-hand rule.

**Component form:**
$$\vec{A} \times \vec{B} = \begin{vmatrix}
\hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\
A_x & A_y & A_z \\
B_x & B_y & B_z
\end{vmatrix}
= (A_y B_z - A_z B_y)\hat{i} + (A_z B_x - A_x B_z)\hat{j} + (A_x B_y - A_y B_x)\hat{k}$$

### 3.2 Anti-commutative
$$\vec{A} \times \vec{B} = -(\vec{B} \times \vec{A})$$
*Proof:* Swapping rows in determinant changes sign.

### 3.3 Distributive
$$\vec{A} \times (\vec{B} + \vec{C}) = \vec{A} \times \vec{B} + \vec{A} \times \vec{C}$$
*Proof:* Expand components using definition.

### 3.4 Self cross product
$$\vec{A} \times \vec{A} = \vec{0}$$

### 3.5 Parallel condition
$$\vec{A} \parallel \vec{B} \iff \vec{A} \times \vec{B} = \vec{0}$$

### 3.6 Magnitude
$$|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}| = |\vec{A}||\vec{B}|\sin\theta$$
*Proof:* $|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}|^2 = (\vec{A} \times \vec{B}) \cdot (\vec{A} \times \vec{B}) = |\vec{A}|^2|\vec{B}|^2 - (\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B})^2 = |\vec{A}|^2|\vec{B}|^2(1-\cos^2\theta) = |\vec{A}|^2|\vec{B}|^2\sin^2\theta$

### 3.7 Area interpretation
- Area of parallelogram = $|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}|$
- Area of triangle = $\frac{1}{2}|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}|$

## 4. Triple Products

### 4.1 Scalar Triple Product
$$[\vec{A}\ \vec{B}\ \vec{C}] = \vec{A} \cdot (\vec{B} \times \vec{C}) = \begin{vmatrix}
A_x & A_y & A_z \\
B_x & B_y & B_z \\
C_x & C_y & C_z
\end{vmatrix}$$

**Properties:**
- **Cyclic permutation:** $\vec{A} \cdot (\vec{B} \times \vec{C}) = \vec{B} \cdot (\vec{C} \times \vec{A}) = \vec{C} \cdot (\vec{A} \times \vec{B})$
- **Anti-cyclic:** $\vec{A} \cdot (\vec{B} \times \vec{C}) = -\vec{A} \cdot (\vec{C} \times \vec{B})$
- **Zero if coplanar:** $\vec{A} \cdot (\vec{B} \times \vec{C}) = 0 \iff$ vectors coplanar
- **Volume:** $V_{\text{parallelepiped}} = |\vec{A} \cdot (\vec{B} \times \vec{C})|$

### 4.2 Vector Triple Product (BAC-CAB Rule)
$$\vec{A} \times (\vec{B} \times \vec{C}) = \vec{B}(\vec{A} \cdot \vec{C}) - \vec{C}(\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B})$$

**Proof:**
Let $\vec{A} = A_x\hat{i} + A_y\hat{j} + A_z\hat{k}$, etc.
$\vec{B} \times \vec{C} = (B_y C_z - B_z C_y)\hat{i} + (B_z C_x - B_x C_z)\hat{j} + (B_x C_y - B_y C_x)\hat{k}$

$\vec{A} \times (\vec{B} \times \vec{C})$ — expand using determinant or component-wise.

$\hat{i}$ component:
$A_y(B_x C_y - B_y C_x) - A_z(B_z C_x - B_x C_z)$
$= A_y B_x C_y - A_y B_y C_x - A_z B_z C_x + A_z B_x C_z$
$= B_x(A_y C_y + A_z C_z) - C_x(A_y B_y + A_z B_z)$
$= B_x(\vec{A} \cdot \vec{C} - A_x C_x) - C_x(\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} - A_x B_x)$
$= B_x(\vec{A} \cdot \vec{C}) - C_x(\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}) + A_x(B_x C_x - B_x C_x)$
$= B_x(\vec{A} \cdot \vec{C}) - C_x(\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B})$

Similarly for $\hat{j}$ and $\hat{k}$ components. ✓

## 5. Important Vector Identities

### 5.1 Lagrange's Identity
$$|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}|^2 = |\vec{A}|^2|\vec{B}|^2 - (\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B})^2$$

*Proof:* Use $|\vec{A} \times \vec{B}| = AB\sin\theta$, $\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B} = AB\cos\theta$, and $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$.

### 5.2 $(\vec{A} \times \vec{B}) \cdot (\vec{C} \times \vec{D}) = (\vec{A} \cdot \vec{C})(\vec{B} \cdot \vec{D}) - (\vec{A} \cdot \vec{D})(\vec{B} \cdot \vec{C})$

### 5.3 Vector Triple Product Expansion
$$\vec{A} \times (\vec{B} \times \vec{C}) = \vec{B}(\vec{A} \cdot \vec{C}) - \vec{C}(\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B})$$

$$(\vec{A} \times \vec{B}) \times \vec{C} = \vec{B}(\vec{A} \cdot \vec{C}) - \vec{A}(\vec{B} \cdot \vec{C})$$

### 5.4 Scalar Triple Product Symmetry
$$\vec{A} \cdot (\vec{B} \times \vec{C}) = \vec{B} \cdot (\vec{C} \times \vec{A}) = \vec{C} \cdot (\vec{A} \times \vec{B})$$

## 6. Coordinate Transformations

### 6.1 Cartesian to Spherical
$$\begin{aligned}
x &= r\sin\theta\cos\phi \\
y &= r\sin\theta\sin\phi \\
z &= r\cos\theta \\
r &= \sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2} \\
\theta &= \cos^{-1}(z/r) \\
\phi &= \tan^{-1}(y/x)
\end{aligned}$$

### 6.2 Spherical Unit Vectors
$$\hat{r} = \sin\theta\cos\phi\,\hat{i} + \sin\theta\sin\phi\,\hat{j} + \cos\theta\,\hat{k}$$
$$\hat{\theta} = \cos\theta\cos\phi\,\hat{i} + \cos\theta\sin\phi\,\hat{j} - \sin\theta\,\hat{k}$$
$$\hat{\phi} = -\sin\phi\,\hat{i} + \cos\phi\,\hat{j}$$

## 7. Applications in Physics

### 7.1 Work Done
$$W = \int \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{s} = \int F_s\ ds$$
If $\vec{F}$ constant: $W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{s}$

### 7.2 Torque
$$\vec{\tau} = \vec{r} \times \vec{F}$$
Magnitude: $\tau = rF\sin\theta$

### 7.3 Angular Momentum
$$\vec{L} = \vec{r} \times \vec{p} = m(\vec{r} \times \vec{v})$$

### 7.4 Magnetic Force
$$\vec{F} = q\vec{v} \times \vec{B}$$

### 7.5 Centripetal Acceleration
$$\vec{a} = \vec{\omega} \times (\vec{\omega} \times \vec{r}) = \vec{\omega}(\vec{\omega} \cdot \vec{r}) - \vec{r}(\omega^2)$$
For circular motion $\vec{\omega} \perp \vec{r}$: $a = \omega^2 r$

## 8. Common A/L Problems

| Problem Type | Approach |
|--------------|----------|
| Find angle between vectors | $\cos\theta = \frac{\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B}}{AB}$ |
| Find perpendicular vector | $\vec{A} \times \vec{B}$ |
| Check coplanarity | $\vec{A} \cdot (\vec{B} \times \vec{C}) = 0$ |
| Find area of triangle | $\frac{1}{2}|\vec{AB} \times \vec{AC}|$ |
| Find volume of tetrahedron | $\frac{1}{6}|\vec{AB} \cdot (\vec{AC} \times \vec{AD})|$ |
| Resolve into components | $A_x = \vec{A} \cdot \hat{i}$, etc. |
| Find component along $\vec{B}$ | $\frac{\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}}{B}$ |
