What is AI? It's not just the science fiction robots — it's the field of designing rational agents that perceive their environment and act to maximize their expected utility. CS 188 focuses on the techniques behind those agents.

**The Intuition:** Think of AI like a thermostat. It perceives the room temperature (percept), decides whether to turn heating on or off (action), and its goal is to keep the room comfortable (utility). A "rational" thermostat isn't just reacting — it's making the best decision given what it knows. Scale that up to self-driving cars, chess engines, or recommendation systems, and you've got the full spectrum of AI.

**The Math:**

A rational agent selects actions that maximize expected utility given its percepts and environment. The course covers four core components:

1. **Search & Planning** — finding sequences of actions that reach goals
2. **Probability & Inference** — reasoning under uncertainty
3. **Supervised Learning** — learning from labeled data
4. **Reinforcement Learning** — learning through trial and error with rewards

**AI Impact in the real world:** Text assistants, image generation, economy, politics, law, labor, sciences, education, military — AI touches every domain.

**This course is about:**
- General AI techniques for a variety of problem types
- Learning to recognize when and how a new problem can be solved with an existing technique
