# Two Pointers

## Definition
Using two indices (pointers) to traverse a data structure, typically from opposite ends or at different speeds.

## When to Use
- **Sorted input** — find pairs, triplets, or ranges
- **In-place modification** — partition, deduplicate
- **Slow-fast** — cycle detection, middle of linked list
- **Opposite ends** — palindrome check, two-sum in sorted array

## Time Complexity
Typically $O(n)$ with $O(1)$ extra space — one pass instead of nested loops.
