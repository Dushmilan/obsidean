# 6.1 Atomic Structure

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 6, Topic 6.1

## Definition

The Bohr model explains hydrogen spectra using quantized energy levels. Electrons orbit in specific allowed orbits without radiating.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $E_n = -\frac{13.6}{n^2}$ eV | Energy levels (hydrogen) |
| $\frac{1}{\lambda} = R_H\left(\frac{1}{n_1^2} - \frac{1}{n_2^2}\right)$ | Spectral series |
| $r_n = n^2 a_0$ | Orbital radius |
| $hf = E_2 - E_1$ | Photon emission/absorption |

$R_H = 1.097 \times 10^7$ m⁻¹ (Rydberg constant)

**Spectral series:**
| Series | $n_1$ | Region |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Lyman | 1 | UV |
| Balmer | 2 | Visible |
| Paschen | 3 | Infrared |

## Worked Example

Find wavelength of photon emitted when electron falls from $n=3$ to $n=2$ (Balmer).

$$\frac{1}{\lambda} = 1.097 \times 10^7 \left(\frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{9}\right) = 1.097 \times 10^7 \times \frac{5}{36} = 1.524 \times 10^6$$
$$\lambda = 656\ \text{nm (red)}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Forgetting energy is negative (bound states)
- ❌ Using wrong $n$ values for emission vs absorption
- ❌ Confusing frequency and wavelength in spectral calculations
