# 6.2 Nuclear Physics

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 6, Topic 6.2

## Definition

Nuclear physics studies the nucleus: radioactive decay, binding energy, fission, and fusion. Mass-energy equivalence underpins nuclear energy.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $E = mc^2$ | Mass-energy equivalence |
| $N = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}$ | Radioactive decay |
| $t_{1/2} = \frac{\ln 2}{\lambda}$ | Half-life |
| $A = \lambda N$ | Activity |
| $BE = [Zm_p + Nm_n - M]c^2$ | Binding energy |

## Worked Example

A sample has half-life 10 years. Find fraction remaining after 30 years.

$$N = N_0 e^{-\lambda t} = N_0 \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{t/t_{1/2}} = N_0 \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{N_0}{8}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Confusing mass number ($A$) with atomic mass
- ❌ Forgetting binding energy per nucleon determines stability
- ❌ Mixing up fission (heavy splits) and fusion (light combines)
