# 6.3 Quantum Mechanics

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 6, Topic 6.3

## Definition

Quantum mechanics describes behaviour at atomic scales. The Schrödinger equation gives wave functions; the uncertainty principle sets fundamental limits.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $\Delta x \cdot \Delta p \geq \frac{h}{4\pi}$ | Heisenberg uncertainty |
| $\lambda = \frac{h}{mv}$ | de Broglie wavelength |
| $E = hf = \frac{hc}{\lambda}$ | Photon energy |
| $\hat{H}\Psi = E\Psi$ | Schrödinger equation (time-independent) |

## Worked Example

Find de Broglie wavelength of an electron ($m = 9.1 \times 10^{-31}$ kg) moving at $10^6$ m/s.

$$\lambda = \frac{6.63 \times 10^{-34}}{9.1 \times 10^{-31} \times 10^6} = 7.3 \times 10^{-10}\ \text{m} = 0.73\ \text{nm}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Applying quantum rules to macroscopic objects (wavelength too small)
- ❌ Thinking uncertainty means measurement limitation (it's fundamental)
- ❌ Confusing wave function $\Psi$ with observable quantities
