# 6.5 Particle Physics

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 6, Topic 6.5

## Definition

Particle physics studies fundamental particles and forces. The Standard Model classifies particles into quarks, leptons, and gauge bosons.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |

| $p = \frac{h}{\lambda}$ | Momentum of photon |
| $E = pc$ | Massless particle |
| $\tau = \frac{d}{c}$ | Lifetime from track length |

## Worked Example

A pion ($m = 140$ MeV/c²) at rest decays into a muon and neutrino. Find energy of each product (assuming massless neutrino).

By conservation: $E_\mu + E_\nu = 140$ MeV. With $p_\mu = p_\nu$: $E_\mu^2 = (p_\mu c)^2 + (140)^2$ and $E_\nu = p_\mu c$. Solving: $E_\mu \approx 70$ MeV, $E_\nu \approx 70$ MeV.

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Confusing quarks (interact via all 4 forces) with leptons (no strong force)
- ❌ Forgetting antimatter has same mass, opposite charge
- ❌ Using classical mechanics at relativistic speeds
