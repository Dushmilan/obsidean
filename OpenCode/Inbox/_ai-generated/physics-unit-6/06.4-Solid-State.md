# 6.4 Solid State Physics

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 6, Topic 6.4

## Definition

Solid state physics explains conductor, semiconductor, and insulator behaviour through energy band theory. Doping controls conductivity.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $n_i^2 = n_e \cdot n_h$ | Intrinsic carrier product |
| $\sigma = ne\mu$ | Conductivity |
| $E_g = hf_{min}$ | Band gap energy |

**Band gaps:**
| Material | $E_g$ (eV) | Type |
|----------|-----------|------|
| Copper | ~0 | Conductor |
| Silicon | 1.1 | Semiconductor |
| Germanium | 0.67 | Semiconductor |
| Diamond | 5.5 | Insulator |

## Worked Example

Find minimum photon energy to excite electron across silicon band gap.

$$E = E_g = 1.1\ \text{eV}$$
$$\lambda = \frac{1240}{1.1} = 1127\ \text{nm (infrared)}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Confusing n-type (extra electrons) with p-type (extra holes)
- ❌ Thinking semiconductors have zero conductivity at 0 K
- ❌ Forgetting doping is intentional impurity addition
