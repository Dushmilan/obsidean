# 4.5 Optical Instruments

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 4, Topic 4.5

## Definition

Optical instruments use lenses and mirrors to magnify objects or resolve distant sources.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $M = \frac{f_o}{f_e}$ | Telescope magnification |
| $M = \frac{D}{f}$ | Simple magnifier |
| $\theta_{min} = \frac{1.22\lambda}{a}$ | Rayleigh criterion |
| $d_{min} = \frac{\lambda}{2a}$ | Resolving power |

## Worked Example

Telescope with objective $f_o = 120$ cm, eyepiece $f_e = 4$ cm. Find magnification.

$$M = \frac{120}{4} = 30\times$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Forgetting objective lens is always the larger one
- ❌ Confusing magnification with resolution
- ❌ Assuming microscope and telescope use same magnification formula
