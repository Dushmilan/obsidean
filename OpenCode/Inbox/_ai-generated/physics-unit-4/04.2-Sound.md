# 4.2 Sound

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 4, Topic 4.2

## Definition

Sound is a longitudinal mechanical wave. It requires a medium. The Doppler effect changes frequency when source or observer moves.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $v = 331 + 0.6\theta$ | Speed of sound in air (°C) |
| $I = \frac{P}{4\pi r^2}$ | Intensity (point source) |
| $\beta = 10\log\frac{I}{I_0}$ | Sound level (dB), $I_0 = 10^{-12}$ W/m² |
| $f' = f\frac{v \pm v_o}{v \mp v_s}$ | Doppler effect |

## Worked Example

A source moves toward observer at 30 m/s, emitting 440 Hz. Find observed frequency ($v = 340$ m/s).

$$f' = 440 \times \frac{340}{340 - 30} = 440 \times \frac{340}{310} = 483\ \text{Hz}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Forgetting Doppler sign convention (approach: minus in denominator)
- ❌ Confusing intensity with intensity level (linear vs logarithmic)
- ❌ Assuming sound travels in vacuum
