# 4.1 Wave Motion

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 4, Topic 4.1

## Definition

Waves transfer energy without transferring matter. Transverse waves oscillate perpendicular to propagation; longitudinal waves oscillate parallel.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $v = f\lambda$ | Wave speed |
| $v = \sqrt{\frac{T}{\mu}}$ | Speed on stretched string |
| $y = A\sin(kx - \omega t)$ | Wave equation |
| $k = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$, $\omega = 2\pi f$ | Wave number, angular frequency |

## Worked Example

A wave has frequency 500 Hz and wavelength 0.68 m. Find speed.

$$v = f\lambda = 500 \times 0.68 = 340\ \text{m/s}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Confusing wave speed with particle speed
- ❌ Forgetting standing waves are formed by superposition
- ❌ Mixing up transverse and longitudinal examples
