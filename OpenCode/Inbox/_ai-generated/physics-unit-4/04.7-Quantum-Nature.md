# 4.7 Quantum Nature of Light

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 4, Topic 4.7

## Definition

The photoelectric effect proves light has particle properties. Wave-particle duality states all matter exhibits both behaviours.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $E = hf$ | Photon energy |
| $KE_{max} = hf - \phi$ | Photoelectric equation |
| $\lambda = \frac{h}{mv}$ | de Broglie wavelength |

$h = 6.63 \times 10^{-34}$ J·s, $1\ \text{eV} = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}$ J

## Worked Example

Light of frequency $1.2 \times 10^{15}$ Hz hits a metal with work function 3.0 eV. Find max KE.

$$E = hf = \frac{6.63 \times 10^{-34} \times 1.2 \times 10^{15}}{1.6 \times 10^{-19}} = 4.97\ \text{eV}$$
$$KE_{max} = 4.97 - 3.0 = 1.97\ \text{eV}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Thinking intensity affects KE (it only affects current)
- ❌ Forgetting there's a threshold frequency below which no electrons are emitted
- ❌ Using de Broglie for macroscopic objects (wavelength negligible)
