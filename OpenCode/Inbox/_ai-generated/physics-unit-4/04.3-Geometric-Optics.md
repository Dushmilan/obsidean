# 4.3 Geometric Optics

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 4, Topic 4.3

## Definition

Geometric optics treats light as rays. Reflection and refraction govern image formation by mirrors and lenses.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $\frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{f}$ | Mirror/lens equation |
| $m = -\frac{v}{u}$ | Magnification |
| $n_1\sin\theta_1 = n_2\sin\theta_2$ | Snell's law |
| $n = \frac{c}{v}$ | Refractive index |
| $\sin\theta_c = \frac{n_2}{n_1}$ | Critical angle (total internal reflection) |

## Worked Example

An object 10 cm from a convex lens ($f = 5$ cm). Find image distance.

$$\frac{1}{v} = \frac{1}{f} - \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{5} - \frac{1}{10} = \frac{1}{10} \implies v = 10\ \text{cm}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Sign convention errors (real is positive for mirrors, negative for lenses)
- ❌ Forgetting total internal reflection requires $n_1 > n_2$
- ❌ Mixing up convex and concave lens behaviour
