# 4.6 Electromagnetic Waves

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 4, Topic 4.6

## Definition

EM waves are transverse waves of oscillating electric and magnetic fields. They travel at $c = 3 \times 10^8$ m/s in vacuum and require no medium.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $c = f\lambda$ | Speed of light |
| $c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0 \epsilon_0}}$ | From Maxwell's equations |
| $E = hf$ | Photon energy |
| $E_{eV} = \frac{1240}{\lambda_{nm}}$ | Energy in eV |

**EM Spectrum:**
| Type | $\lambda$ range | Source |
|------|----------------|--------|
| Radio | > 1 m | AC circuits |
| Microrowave | 1 mm – 1 m | Magnetron |
| Infrared | 700 nm – 1 mm | Hot objects |
| Visible | 400 – 700 nm | Atoms |
| UV | 10 – 400 nm | Sun, discharge |
| X-ray | 0.01 – 10 nm | Decelerating electrons |
| Gamma | < 0.01 nm | Nuclear decay |

## Worked Example

Find energy of a photon with wavelength 500 nm in eV.

$$E = \frac{1240}{500} = 2.48\ \text{eV}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Thinking EM waves need a medium
- ❌ Confusing frequency and wavelength across media (frequency constant)
- ❌ Forgetting X-rays and gamma rays are ionizing
