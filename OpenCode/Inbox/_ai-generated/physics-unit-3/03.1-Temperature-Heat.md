# 3.1 Temperature & Heat

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 3, Topic 3.1

## Definition

Temperature measures average kinetic energy of particles. Heat is energy transferred between objects at different temperatures.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $Q = mc\Delta T$ | Heat capacity |
| $Q = mL$ | Latent heat |
| $\frac{C}{F - 32} = \frac{100}{180}$ | Temperature conversion |

**Temperature scales:**
| Scale | Freezing | Boiling | Absolute zero |
|-------|----------|---------|---------------|
| Celsius | 0°C | 100°C | -273.15°C |
| Fahrenheit | 32°F | 212°F | -459.67°F |
| Kelvin | 0 K | 373.15 K | 0 K |

## Worked Example

Find heat to raise 0.5 kg water from 20°C to 80°C ($c = 4200$ J/kg·K).

$$Q = mc\Delta T = 0.5 \times 4200 \times 60 = 126{,}000\ \text{J} = 126\ \text{kJ}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Using Celsius instead of Kelvin in gas law calculations
- ❌ Confusing specific heat capacity ($c$) with heat capacity ($C = mc$)
- ❌ Forgetting latent heat has no temperature change
