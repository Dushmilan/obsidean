# 3.3 First Law of Thermodynamics

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 3, Topic 3.3

## Definition

Energy cannot be created or destroyed. The change in internal energy equals heat added minus work done by the system.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $\Delta U = Q - W$ | First law |
| $W = p\Delta V$ | Work at constant pressure |
| $W = nRT\ln\frac{V_2}{V_1}$ | Work in isothermal process |

**Process summary:**
| Process | Condition | $\Delta U$ | $Q$ | $W$ |
|---------|-----------|------------|-----|-----|
| Isothermal | $T$ const | 0 | $W$ | $nRT\ln(V_2/V_1)$ |
| Isochoric | $V$ const | $nC_v\Delta T$ | $\Delta U$ | 0 |
| Isobaric | $p$ const | $nC_v\Delta T$ | $nC_p\Delta T$ | $p\Delta V$ |

## Worked Example

2 mol ideal gas expands isothermally at 300 K from 0.01 m³ to 0.03 m³. Find work.

$$W = nRT\ln\frac{V_2}{V_1} = 2 \times 8.31 \times 300 \times \ln 3 = 5{,}480\ \text{J}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Forgetting $\Delta U = 0$ for isothermal (ideal gas)
- ❌ Using $\Delta U = Q + W$ (sign convention varies)
- ❌ Confusing $C_p$ and $C_v$
