# 3.4 Heat Transfer

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 3, Topic 3.4

## Definition

Heat transfers via conduction (molecular vibration), convection (fluid motion), and radiation (electromagnetic waves).

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $\frac{Q}{t} = \frac{kA\Delta T}{d}$ | Conduction rate |
| $P = \epsilon \sigma A T^4$ | Radiation (Stefan-Boltzmann) |
| $e = 1 - \frac{T_{cold}}{T_{hot}}$ | Carnot efficiency |

$\sigma = 5.67 \times 10^{-8}$ W/(m²·K⁴)

## Worked Example

A metal plate (k = 200 W/m·K, area 0.1 m², thickness 0.02 m) has $\Delta T = 60$ K. Find heat flow rate.

$$\frac{Q}{t} = \frac{200 \times 0.1 \times 60}{0.02} = 60{,}000\ \text{W} = 60\ \text{kW}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Forgetting radiation works in vacuum (no medium needed)
- ❌ Confusing conductors and insulators
- ❌ Using Celsius in Stefan-Boltzmann (must use Kelvin)
