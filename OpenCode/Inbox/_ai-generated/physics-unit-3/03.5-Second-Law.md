# 3.5 Second Law of Thermodynamics

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 3, Topic 3.5

## Definition

Heat flows spontaneously from hot to cold. No engine can be 100% efficient. Entropy of an isolated system never decreases.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $e_{max} = 1 - \frac{T_C}{T_H}$ | Carnot efficiency |
| $\Delta S = \frac{Q}{T}$ | Entropy change |
| $\Delta S_{total} \geq 0$ | Entropy principle |

## Worked Example

A Carnot engine operates between 600 K and 300 K. Find maximum efficiency.

$$e = 1 - \frac{300}{600} = 0.5 = 50\%$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Thinking second law is violated by refrigerators (work input required)
- ❌ Using Celsius in Carnot efficiency (must use Kelvin)
- ❌ Confusing efficiency of real engines with Carnot limit
