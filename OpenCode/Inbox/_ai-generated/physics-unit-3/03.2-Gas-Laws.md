# 3.2 Gas Laws

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 3, Topic 3.2

## Definition

The ideal gas law relates pressure, volume, temperature, and amount of gas. Kinetic theory explains macroscopic properties from molecular motion.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $pV = nRT$ | Ideal gas law |
| $\frac{p_1 V_1}{T_1} = \frac{p_2 V_2}{T_2}$ | Combined gas law |
| $pV = \frac{1}{3}Nm\overline{c^2}$ | Kinetic theory |
| $\overline{KE} = \frac{3}{2}kT$ | Mean KE per molecule |

$R = 8.31$ J/(mol·K), $k = 1.38 \times 10^{-23}$ J/K

## Worked Example

2 mol gas at 300 K occupies 0.05 m³. Find pressure.

$$p = \frac{nRT}{V} = \frac{2 \times 8.31 \times 300}{0.05} = 99{,}720\ \text{Pa} \approx 100\ \text{kPa}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Using Celsius in $pV = nRT$ (must use Kelvin)
- ❌ Confusing $R$ (8.31) with $k$ (1.38 × 10⁻²³)
- ❌ Assuming ideal gas law applies at high pressure/low temperature
