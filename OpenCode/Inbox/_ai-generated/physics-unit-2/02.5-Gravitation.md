# 2.5 Gravitation

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 2, Topic 2.5

## Definition

Newton's law of universal gravitation states every mass attracts every other mass with a force proportional to the product of masses and inversely proportional to the square of distance.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $F = G\frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$ | $G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11}\ \text{Nm}^2/\text{kg}^2$ |
| $g = \frac{GM}{r^2}$ | Gravitational field strength |
| $v_{orb} = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}}$ | Orbital velocity |
| $T^2 = \frac{4\pi^2}{GM}r^3$ | Kepler's 3rd law |

## Worked Example

Find orbital velocity of Earth around Sun ($M = 2 \times 10^{30}$ kg, $r = 1.5 \times 10^{11}$ m).

$$v = \sqrt{\frac{6.67 \times 10^{-11} \times 2 \times 10^{30}}{1.5 \times 10^{11}}} = 3.0 \times 10^4\ \text{m/s}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Using $g = 9.8$ for orbital problems (varies with altitude)
- ❌ Forgetting $r$ is measured from centre of mass
- ❌ Confusing gravitational field strength ($g$, N/kg) with acceleration ($g$, m/s²)
