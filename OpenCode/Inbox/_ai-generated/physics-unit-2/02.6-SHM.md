# 2.6 Simple Harmonic Motion

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 2, Topic 2.6

## Definition

SHM is oscillatory motion where acceleration is proportional to displacement from equilibrium and directed toward it: $a = -\omega^2 x$.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $x = A\cos(\omega t + \phi)$ | Displacement |
| $v = \pm\omega\sqrt{A^2 - x^2}$ | Velocity |
| $a = -\omega^2 x$ | Acceleration |
| $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{l}{g}}$ | Simple pendulum |
| $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}$ | Spring-mass system |
| $E = \frac{1}{2}kA^2$ | Total energy |

## Worked Example

A spring ($k = 100$ N/m) oscillates with amplitude 0.1 m. Find max velocity.

$$v_{max} = \omega A = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} \cdot A$$
For $m = 1$ kg: $v_{max} = \sqrt{100} \times 0.1 = 1.0\ \text{m/s}$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Forgetting $a = -\omega^2 x$ (negative sign means restoring)
- ❌ Confusing frequency ($f = 1/T$) with angular frequency ($\omega = 2\pi f$)
- ❌ Assuming velocity is max at maximum displacement
