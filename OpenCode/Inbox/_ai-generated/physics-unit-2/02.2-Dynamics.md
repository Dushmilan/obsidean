# 2.2 Dynamics

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 2, Topic 2.2

## Definition

Dynamics relates motion to the forces causing it. Newton's three laws form the foundation.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $F = ma$ | Newton's 2nd law |
| $F_{net} = 0$ | Newton's 1st law (equilibrium) |
| $F_{AB} = -F_{BA}$ | Newton's 3rd law |
| $p = mv$ | Momentum |
| $F = \frac{\Delta p}{\Delta t}$ | Force as rate of change of momentum |

## Worked Example

A 5 kg block on frictionless surface pulled by 20 N. Find acceleration.

$$a = \frac{F}{m} = \frac{20}{5} = 4\ \text{m/s}^2$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Confusing mass and weight ($W = mg$, not $W = m$)
- ❌ Forgetting to draw free-body diagrams
- ❌ Assuming $F_{AB} = F_{BA}$ means they cancel (different objects)
