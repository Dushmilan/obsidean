# 2.7 Rotational Dynamics

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 2, Topic 2.7

## Definition

Rotational dynamics extends Newton's laws to rotating bodies. Torque replaces force, moment of inertia replaces mass, angular acceleration replaces linear acceleration.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $\tau = rF\sin\theta$ | Torque |
| $\tau = I\alpha$ | Newton's 2nd law (rotation) |
| $I = \sum m_i r_i^2$ | Moment of inertia |
| $L = I\omega$ | Angular momentum |
| $KE_{rot} = \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$ | Rotational kinetic energy |

**Parallel axis theorem:** $I = I_{cm} + md^2$

## Worked Example

A disc ($I = \frac{1}{2}MR^2$) of mass 2 kg, radius 0.5 m, has 10 N·m torque applied. Find angular acceleration.

$$\alpha = \frac{\tau}{I} = \frac{10}{\frac{1}{2}(2)(0.5)^2} = \frac{10}{0.25} = 40\ \text{rad/s}^2$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Using $I = mr^2$ for extended bodies (need integration or tables)
- ❌ Forgetting $I$ depends on axis of rotation
- ❌ Confusing rotational and translational kinetic energy
