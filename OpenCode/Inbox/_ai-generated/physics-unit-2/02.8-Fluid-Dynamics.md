# 2.8 Fluid Dynamics

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 2, Topic 2.8

## Definition

Fluid dynamics studies flowing fluids. Pressure, velocity, and height are related through Bernoulli's equation. Pascal's and Archimedes' principles govern static fluids.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $p = \frac{F}{A}$ | Pressure |
| $p = p_0 + \rho gh$ | Hydrostatic pressure |
| $F_B = \rho V g$ | Buoyant force (Archimedes) |
| $p + \frac{1}{2}\rho v^2 + \rho gh = \text{const}$ | Bernoulli's equation |
| $A_1 v_1 = A_2 v_2$ | Continuity equation |

## Worked Example

Water flows through a pipe that narrows from 10 cm² to 5 cm². If velocity in wide section is 2 m/s, find velocity in narrow section.

$$A_1 v_1 = A_2 v_2 \implies v_2 = \frac{A_1 v_1}{A_2} = \frac{10 \times 2}{5} = 4\ \text{m/s}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Forgetting Bernoulli assumes incompressible, non-viscous flow
- ❌ Mixing up gauge and absolute pressure
- ❌ Not converting units (cm² to m²)
