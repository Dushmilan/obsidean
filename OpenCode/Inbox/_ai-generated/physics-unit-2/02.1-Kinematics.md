# 2.1 Kinematics

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 2, Topic 2.1

## Definition

Kinematics describes motion without considering forces. It deals with displacement, velocity, acceleration, and time.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $v = u + at$ | $u$ = initial velocity, $v$ = final velocity, $a$ = acceleration, $t$ = time |
| $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$ | $s$ = displacement |
| $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$ | Eliminates time |
| $s = \frac{(u+v)}{2}t$ | Average velocity form |

**Projectile motion:**
| Horizontal | Vertical |
|------------|----------|
| $x = u\cos\theta \cdot t$ | $y = u\sin\theta \cdot t - \frac{1}{2}gt^2$ |
| $a_x = 0$ | $a_y = -g$ |

## Worked Example

A ball is thrown upward at $20\ \text{m/s}$. Find max height.

At max height, $v = 0$:
$$0 = (20)^2 + 2(-10)(s) \implies s = \frac{400}{20} = 20\ \text{m}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Forgetting $g$ acts downward (sign errors)
- ❌ Mixing horizontal and vertical components
- ❌ Using $v = u + at$ for projectile trajectory (need parametric form)
