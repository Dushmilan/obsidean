# 2.3 Work, Energy & Power

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 2, Topic 2.3

## Definition

Work transfers energy via force. Kinetic and potential energy interconvert. Power is the rate of energy transfer.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $W = Fs\cos\theta$ | Work done by force $F$ over displacement $s$ |
| $KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$ | Kinetic energy |
| $PE = mgh$ | Gravitational potential energy |
| $P = \frac{W}{t}$ | Power |
| $P = Fv$ | Power at constant velocity |

**Work-energy theorem:**
$$W_{net} = \Delta KE$$

## Worked Example

A 2 kg ball falls from 10 m. Find speed at ground (no air resistance).

$$mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 \implies v = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{2 \times 10 \times 10} = 14.1\ \text{m/s}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Using $W = Fs$ when force and displacement aren't parallel
- ❌ Confusing mass with weight in PE calculations
- ❌ Forgetting energy is conserved only when no dissipative forces
