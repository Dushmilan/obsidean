# 5.2 Capacitors

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 5, Topic 5.2

## Definition

A capacitor stores charge and energy in an electric field between two plates. Capacitance depends on geometry and dielectric material.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $C = \frac{Q}{V}$ | Capacitance definition |
| $E = \frac{1}{2}CV^2$ | Energy stored |
| $E = \frac{1}{2}QV = \frac{Q^2}{2C}$ | Alternative forms |
| $\frac{1}{C_{eq}} = \frac{1}{C_1} + \frac{1}{C_2}$ | Series |
| $C_{eq} = C_1 + C_2$ | Parallel |

## Worked Example

10 μF capacitor charged to 100 V. Find energy stored.

$$E = \frac{1}{2}CV^2 = \frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 10^{-6} \times (100)^2 = 0.05\ \text{J}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Swapping series and parallel rules (opposite of resistors)
- ❌ Forgetting energy is proportional to $V^2$, not $V$
- ❌ Confusing time constant $\tau = RC$ with capacitor behaviour
