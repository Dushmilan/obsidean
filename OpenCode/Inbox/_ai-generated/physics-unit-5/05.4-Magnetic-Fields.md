# 5.4 Magnetic Fields

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 5, Topic 5.4

## Definition

Magnetic fields exert forces on moving charges and current-carrying wires. The Lorentz force combines electric and magnetic effects.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $F = BIL\sin\theta$ | Force on current-carrying wire |
| $F = qvB\sin\theta$ | Lorentz force on moving charge |
| $F = Bqv$ | Maximum force (perpendicular) |
| $B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$ | Field around long straight wire |

$\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7}$ T·m/A

## Worked Example

Wire of length 0.5 m carries 10 A in a 0.2 T field perpendicular to wire. Find force.

$$F = BIL = 0.2 \times 10 \times 0.5 = 1.0\ \text{N}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Forgetting $\sin\theta$ (force is zero when parallel)
- ❌ Confusing magnetic field ($B$, Tesla) with flux ($\Phi$, Weber)
- ❌ Using right-hand rule incorrectly for force direction
