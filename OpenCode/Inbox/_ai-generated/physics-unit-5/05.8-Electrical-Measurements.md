# 5.8 Electrical Measurements

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 5, Topic 5.8

## Definition

Precision measurements use potentiometers (null method) and Wheatstone bridges. These avoid errors from instrument loading.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $\frac{R_1}{R_2} = \frac{R_3}{R_4}$ | Wheatstone bridge balance |
| $V \propto l$ | Potentiometer (uniform wire) |
| $EMF = \frac{l_1}{l_2} \times V_{std}$ | Unknown EMF |

## Worked Example

Wheatstone bridge: $R_1 = 100\ \Omega$, $R_2 = 200\ \Omega$, $R_3 = 150\ \Omega$. Find $R_4$ at balance.

$$R_4 = \frac{R_2 \times R_3}{R_1} = \frac{200 \times 150}{100} = 300\ \Omega$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Forgetting bridge must be balanced (null condition)
- ❌ Confusing potentiometer with voltage divider (potentiometer has zero current at null)
- ❌ Not accounting for internal resistance of cell
