# 5.7 Electromagnetic Waves

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 5, Topic 5.7

## Definition

Maxwell's equations predict electromagnetic waves: oscillating electric and magnetic fields propagating at $c = 3 \times 10^8$ m/s.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0 \epsilon_0}}$ | Speed of EM waves |
| $c = f\lambda$ | Wave relation |
| $B_0 = \frac{E_0}{c}$ | Field amplitude relation |
| $I = \frac{E_0^2}{2\mu_0 c}$ | Intensity |

## Worked Example

Verify $c$ from constants: $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7}$, $\epsilon_0 = 8.85 \times 10^{-12}$.

$$c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi \times 10^{-7} \times 8.85 \times 10^{-12}}} = 3.0 \times 10^8\ \text{m/s}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Thinking EM waves need a medium
- ❌ Forgetting $E$ and $B$ are perpendicular to each other and to propagation
- ❌ Confusing this with geometric optics (ray model)
