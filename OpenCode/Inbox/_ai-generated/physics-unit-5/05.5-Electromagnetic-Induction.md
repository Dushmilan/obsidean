# 5.5 Electromagnetic Induction

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 5, Topic 5.5

## Definition

A changing magnetic flux induces an EMF. Faraday's law gives the magnitude; Lenz's law gives the direction (opposes the change).

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $\mathcal{E} = -N\frac{d\Phi}{dt}$ | Faraday's law |
| $\Phi = BA\cos\theta$ | Magnetic flux |
| $\mathcal{E} = BLv$ | Motional EMF |
| $\mathcal{E} = NBA\omega\sin\omega t$ | AC generator |

## Worked Example

A coil (100 turns, area 0.01 m²) rotates in 0.5 T field at 50 Hz. Find peak EMF.

$$\mathcal{E}_{max} = NBA\omega = 100 \times 0.5 \times 0.01 \times 2\pi \times 50 = 157\ \text{V}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Forgetting the negative sign (Lenz's law)
- ❌ Confusing flux ($\Phi$, Weber) with flux density ($B$, Tesla)
- ❌ Using DC formulas for AC circuits
