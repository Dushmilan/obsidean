# 5.1 Electric Fields

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 5, Topic 5.1

## Definition

An electric field exists around charged objects. Coulomb's law gives the force between point charges. Field strength is force per unit charge.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $F = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2}$ | Coulomb's law |
| $E = \frac{F}{q}$ | Field strength |
| $E = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{Q}{r^2}$ | Point charge field |
| $V = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{Q}{r}$ | Potential |

$\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} = 9 \times 10^9$ N·m²/C²

## Worked Example

Two charges ($+2\ \mu$C and $-3\ \mu$C) are 0.1 m apart. Find force.

$$F = 9 \times 10^9 \times \frac{2 \times 10^{-6} \times 3 \times 10^{-6}}{(0.1)^2} = 5.4\ \text{N (attractive)}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Forgetting force is along the line joining charges
- ❌ Using $k = 9 \times 10^9$ with wrong units
- ❌ Confusing field strength ($E$, N/C) with potential ($V$, J/C)
