# 5.3 Current Electricity

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 5, Topic 5.3

## Definition

Electric current is the flow of charge. Resistance opposes current. Ohm's law relates voltage, current, and resistance for ohmic conductors.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $V = IR$ | Ohm's law |
| $R = \frac{\rho L}{A}$ | Resistance of wire |
| $P = IV = I^2R = \frac{V^2}{R}$ | Power |
| $\frac{1}{R_{eq}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2}$ | Series |
| $R_{eq} = R_1 + R_2$ | Parallel |

**Kirchhoff's laws:**
- Junction: $\sum I_{in} = \sum I_{out}$
- Loop: $\sum V = 0$

## Worked Example

Three resistors: 2Ω, 3Ω, 6Ω in parallel. Find equivalent resistance.

$$\frac{1}{R} = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{3+2+1}{6} = 1 \implies R = 1\ \Omega$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Confusing series and parallel rules (opposite of capacitors)
- ❌ Applying Ohm's law to non-ohmic devices (diodes, bulbs)
- ❌ Forgetting internal resistance of battery
