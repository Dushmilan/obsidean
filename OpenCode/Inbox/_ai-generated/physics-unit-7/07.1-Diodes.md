# 7.1 Diodes

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 7, Topic 7.1

## Definition

A diode allows current in one direction. The PN junction is the fundamental semiconductor device. Zener diodes operate in reverse breakdown.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $I = I_0(e^{V/V_T} - 1)$ | Diode equation |
| $V_T = \frac{kT}{e} \approx 26$ mV | Thermal voltage (300 K) |
| $V_{avg} = \frac{2V_0}{\pi}$ | Half-wave rectifier output |

**Diode types:**
| Type | Symbol | Use |
|------|--------|-----|
| Standard | $\rightarrow$ | Rectification |
| Zener | $\rightarrow$ with bar | Voltage regulation |
| LED | $\rightarrow$ with arrows | Display, lighting |
| Photodiode | $\rightarrow$ with arrows in | Light detection |

## Worked Example

A silicon diode ($V_f \approx 0.7$ V) in series with 100 Ω resistor and 5 V supply. Find current.

$$I = \frac{5 - 0.7}{100} = 43\ \text{mA}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Forgetting forward voltage drop (0.7 V silicon, 0.3 V germanium)
- ❌ Applying reverse voltage beyond breakdown without Zener rating
- ❌ Confusing rectifier circuit configurations
