# 7.5 Digital Systems

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Physics (022) — Unit 7, Topic 7.5

## Definition

Digital systems process binary data. ADC converts analog to digital; DAC converts digital to analog. Microcontrollers integrate CPU, memory, and I/O.

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Variables |
|---------|-----------|
| $\text{Resolution} = \frac{V_{ref}}{2^n}$ | ADC/DAC resolution |
| $t_{conv} = \frac{1}{f_{clk}}$ | Conversion time |
| $\text{Error} = \pm \frac{1}{2} \text{LSB}$ | Quantization error |

**ADC types:**
| Type | Speed | Complexity |
|------|-------|------------|
| Successive approximation | Medium | Medium |
| Flash | Fast | High |
| Dual-slope | Slow | Low |

## Worked Example

10-bit ADC with $V_{ref} = 5$ V. Find resolution.

$$\text{Resolution} = \frac{5}{2^{10}} = \frac{5}{1024} = 4.88\ \text{mV}$$

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Confusing resolution (number of bits) with accuracy
- ❌ Forgetting ADC output is integer, not voltage
- ❌ Not considering sampling rate (Nyquist theorem)
