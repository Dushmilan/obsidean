# 12.3 The Dot Product

> **Stewart Calculus, Chapter 12, Section 12.3**

---

## Section Overview

Introduces the dot (scalar) product — a way to multiply vectors that produces a scalar. Used to find angles, test orthogonality, compute projections, and calculate work.

---

## Concepts (8)

| # | Concept | Key Idea |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | [[Concepts/Dot-Product-Definition.md]] | $\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = a_1b_1+a_2b_2+a_3b_3$ |
| 2 | [[Concepts/Properties-of-the-Dot-Product.md]] | Commutative, distributive, $\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{a}=\|\mathbf{a}\|^2$ |
| 3 | [[Concepts/Angle-Between-Two-Vectors.md]] | $\cos\theta = \frac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{a}\|\|\mathbf{b}\|}$ |
| 4 | [[Concepts/Geometric-Dot-Product-Formula.md]] | $\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = \|\mathbf{a}\|\|\mathbf{b}\|\cos\theta$ |
| 5 | [[Concepts/Orthogonal-Vectors.md]] | $\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}=0 \iff$ orthogonal |
| 6 | [[Concepts/Scalar-Projection.md]] | $\text{comp}_{\mathbf{a}}\mathbf{b} = \frac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{a}\|}$ |
| 7 | [[Concepts/Vector-Projection.md]] | $\text{proj}_{\mathbf{a}}\mathbf{b} = \frac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{a}\|^2}\mathbf{a}$ |
| 8 | [[Concepts/Work-Done-by-a-Constant-Force.md]] | $W = \mathbf{F}\cdot\mathbf{D}$ |
