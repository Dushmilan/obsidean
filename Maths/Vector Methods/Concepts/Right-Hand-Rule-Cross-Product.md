# Right-Hand Rule (Cross Product)

The direction of $\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}$ follows the right-hand rule:

1. Point the fingers of your right hand in the direction of $\mathbf{a}$
2. Curl them toward $\mathbf{b}$
3. Your thumb points in the direction of $\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}$

## Example

$$\mathbf{i}\times\mathbf{j} = \mathbf{k} \qquad \text{(not } -\mathbf{k}\text{)}$$

Reversing the order flips the direction: $\mathbf{j}\times\mathbf{i} = -\mathbf{k}$.
