# Magnitude of Cross Product

$$\|\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}\| = \|\mathbf{a}\|\|\mathbf{b}\|\sin\theta$$

This equals the **area of the parallelogram** formed by $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$.

The magnitude is zero if and only if $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$ are parallel (since $\sin 0° = 0$).
