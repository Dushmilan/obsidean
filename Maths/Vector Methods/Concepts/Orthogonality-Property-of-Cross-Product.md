# Orthogonality Property of Cross Product

The cross product $\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}$ is orthogonal to both $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$:

$$(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b})\cdot\mathbf{a} = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad (\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b})\cdot\mathbf{b} = 0$$

This is proved by direct computation showing the dot product equals zero.
