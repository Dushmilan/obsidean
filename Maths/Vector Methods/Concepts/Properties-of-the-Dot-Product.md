# Properties of the Dot Product

For vectors $\mathbf{a}$, $\mathbf{b}$, $\mathbf{c}$ and scalar $c$:

- **Commutative:** $\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{b}\cdot\mathbf{a}$
- **Distributive:** $\mathbf{a}\cdot(\mathbf{b}+\mathbf{c}) = \mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{c}$
- **Self-dot:** $\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{a} = \|\mathbf{a}\|^2$
- **Zero vector:** $\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{0} = 0$
- **Scalar multiplication:** $(c\mathbf{a})\cdot\mathbf{b} = c(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b})$
