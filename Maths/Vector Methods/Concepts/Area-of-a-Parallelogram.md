# Area of a Parallelogram

The area of a parallelogram with adjacent sides $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$ is:

$$A = \|\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}\|$$

## Example

For $\mathbf{a}=\langle 1,0,0 \rangle$ and $\mathbf{b}=\langle 0,1,0 \rangle$:

$$\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b} = \langle 0,0,1 \rangle$$

$$A = \|\langle 0,0,1 \rangle\| = 1$$
