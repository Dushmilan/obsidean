# Orthogonal Vectors

Two nonzero vectors $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$ are **orthogonal** if and only if:

$$\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = 0$$

"Orthogonal" generalizes "perpendicular" to all dimensions.

## Example

$\langle 1,1 \rangle$ and $\langle -1,1 \rangle$ are orthogonal since:

$$\langle 1,1 \rangle \cdot \langle -1,1 \rangle = -1 + 1 = 0$$
