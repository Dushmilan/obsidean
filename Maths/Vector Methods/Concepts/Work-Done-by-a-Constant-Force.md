# Work Done by a Constant Force

Work is the dot product of force and displacement:

$$W = \mathbf{F}\cdot\mathbf{D} = \|\mathbf{F}\|\|\mathbf{D}\|\cos\theta$$

Where $\mathbf{F}$ is the force vector and $\mathbf{D}$ is the displacement vector. Work is a **scalar** measured in joules.

## Example

Force $\langle 5,0 \rangle$ N applied over displacement $\langle 3,4 \rangle$ m:

$$W = (5)(3) + (0)(4) = 15 \text{ J}$$
