# Static Equilibrium and Tension Vectors

Balance of physical forces where $\sum \mathbf{F} = \mathbf{0}$.

## Example

Two ropes holding a weight. Each rope exerts a tension force vector. For equilibrium, the vector sum of all tension forces and the gravitational force must equal the zero vector.
