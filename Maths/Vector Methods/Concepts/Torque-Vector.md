# Torque Vector

Torque is the rotational equivalent of force, defined as:

$$\boldsymbol{\tau} = \mathbf{r}\times\mathbf{F}$$

## Magnitude

$$\|\boldsymbol{\tau}\| = \|\mathbf{r}\|\|\mathbf{F}\|\sin\theta$$

## Direction

The direction of torque is along the axis of rotation, determined by the right-hand rule.

## Example

A wrench with $\mathbf{r}=\langle 0.2,0,0 \rangle$ m and force $\mathbf{F}=\langle 0,50,0 \rangle$ N:

$$\boldsymbol{\tau} = \langle 0.2,0,0 \rangle \times \langle 0,50,0 \rangle = \langle 0,0,10 \rangle \text{ N·m}$$
