# Coplanar Vectors Test

Three vectors are coplanar if and only if their scalar triple product equals zero:

$$\mathbf{a}\cdot(\mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{c}) = 0 \iff \text{coplanar}$$

This is equivalent to the vectors being **linearly dependent**.

## Example

$\langle 1,2,3 \rangle$, $\langle 2,4,6 \rangle$, $\langle 1,1,1 \rangle$ are coplanar since the first two are parallel (one is a scalar multiple of the other), making the cross product $\mathbf{0}$.
