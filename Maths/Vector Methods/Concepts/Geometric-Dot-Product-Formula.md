# Geometric Dot Product Formula

$$\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = \|\mathbf{a}\|\|\mathbf{b}\|\cos\theta$$

This connects the algebraic definition to geometric meaning.

## Special Cases

- **Parallel** ($\theta = 0$): $\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = \|\mathbf{a}\|\|\mathbf{b}\|$
- **Perpendicular** ($\theta = 90°$): $\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = 0$
- **Antiparallel** ($\theta = 180°$): $\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = -\|\mathbf{a}\|\|\mathbf{b}\|$
