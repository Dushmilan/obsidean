# Angle Between Two Vectors

The angle $\theta$ between nonzero vectors $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$ is given by:

$$\cos\theta = \frac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{a}\|\|\mathbf{b}\|}$$

$$\theta = \arccos\left(\frac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{a}\|\|\mathbf{b}\|}\right)$$

## Example

Angle between $\langle 1,0,1 \rangle$ and $\langle 0,1,1 \rangle$:

$$\cos\theta = \frac{(1)(0)+(0)(1)+(1)(1)}{\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{2}} = \frac{1}{2}$$

$$\theta = 60°$$
