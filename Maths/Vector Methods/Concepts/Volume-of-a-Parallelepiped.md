# Volume of a Parallelepiped

The volume of a parallelepiped with three adjacent edge vectors $\mathbf{a}$, $\mathbf{b}$, $\mathbf{c}$ is:

$$V = |\mathbf{a}\cdot(\mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{c})|$$

This is the absolute value of the scalar triple product.

## Example

For the unit cube with $\mathbf{a}=\langle 1,0,0 \rangle$, $\mathbf{b}=\langle 0,1,0 \rangle$, $\mathbf{c}=\langle 0,0,1 \rangle$:

$$V = |1| = 1$$
