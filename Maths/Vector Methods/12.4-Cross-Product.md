# 12.4 The Cross Product

> **Stewart Calculus, Chapter 12, Section 12.4**

---

## Section Overview

Introduces the cross (vector) product — a multiplication producing a vector orthogonal to both inputs. Used to find areas, volumes, and torques. Covers triple products and the determinant formulation.
