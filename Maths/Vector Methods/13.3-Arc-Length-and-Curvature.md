# 13.3 Arc Length and Curvature

> **Stewart Calculus, Chapter 13, Section 13.3**

---

## Section Overview

Measures length of space curves via arc length integrals. Introduces curvature (how fast direction changes) and the TNB frame (Frenet-Serret) — three orthogonal unit vectors that move along the curve.

---

## Concepts (8)

| # | Concept | Key Idea |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | [[Concepts/Arc-Length-of-a-Space-Curve.md]] | $L = \int_a^b \|\mathbf{r}'(t)\|\,dt$ |
| 2 | [[Concepts/Arc-Length-Function.md]] | $s(t) = \int_a^t \|\mathbf{r}'(u)\|\,du$ |
| 3 | [[Concepts/Curvature-Definition.md]] | $\kappa = \left\|\frac{d\mathbf{T}}{ds}\right\|$ |
| 4 | [[Concepts/Curvature-via-Cross-Product.md]] | $\kappa = \frac{\|\mathbf{r}'\times\mathbf{r}''\|}{\|\mathbf{r}'\|^3}$ |
| 5 | [[Concepts/Principal-Unit-Normal-Vector.md]] | $\mathbf{N} = \frac{\mathbf{T}'}{\|\mathbf{T}'\|}$ |
| 6 | [[Concepts/Binormal-Vector.md]] | $\mathbf{B} = \mathbf{T}\times\mathbf{N}$ |
| 7 | [[Concepts/TNB-Frame-Frenet-Serret-Frame.md]] | $\{\mathbf{T}, \mathbf{N}, \mathbf{B}\}$ moving frame |
| 8 | [[Concepts/Normal-and-Osculating-Planes.md]] | Plane of $\{\mathbf{N},\mathbf{B}\}$ vs $\{\mathbf{T},\mathbf{N}\}$ |
