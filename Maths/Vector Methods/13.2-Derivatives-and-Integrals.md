# 13.2 Derivatives and Integrals of Vector Functions

> **Stewart Calculus, Chapter 13, Section 13.2**

---

## Section Overview

Extends calculus to vector functions. Derivatives and integrals are computed component-wise. Introduces the tangent vector and unit tangent vector. Covers product rules for dot and cross products.

---

## Concepts (6)

| # | Concept | Key Idea |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | [[Concepts/Derivative-of-a-Vector-Function.md]] | $\mathbf{r}'(t) = \langle f'(t), g'(t), h'(t) \rangle$ |
| 2 | [[Concepts/Tangent-Vector.md]] | $\mathbf{r}'(t)$ — direction of tangent line |
| 3 | [[Concepts/Unit-Tangent-Vector.md]] | $\mathbf{T}(t) = \frac{\mathbf{r}'(t)}{\|\mathbf{r}'(t)\|}$ |
| 4 | [[Concepts/Differentiation-Rules-for-Vector-Functions.md]] | Product rules for scalar, dot, cross |
| 5 | [[Concepts/Definite-Integrals-of-Vector-Functions.md]] | Integrate component-wise |
| 6 | [[Concepts/Fundamental-Theorem-of-Calculus-for-Vector-Functions.md]] | $\int_a^b \mathbf{r}'(t)\,dt = \mathbf{r}(b) - \mathbf{r}(a)$ |
