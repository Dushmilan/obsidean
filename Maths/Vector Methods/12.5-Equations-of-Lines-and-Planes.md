# 12.5 Equations of Lines and Planes

> **Stewart Calculus, Chapter 12, Section 12.5**

---

## Section Overview

Develops vector, parametric, and symmetric equations for lines in 3D. Introduces the scalar equation of a plane using the normal vector. Covers intersections and distance formulas.

---

## Concepts (7)

| # | Concept | Key Idea |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | [[Concepts/Vector-Equation-of-a-Line.md]] | $\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{r}_0 + t\mathbf{v}$ |
| 2 | [[Concepts/Parametric-Equations-of-a-Line.md]] | $x=x_0+at$, $y=y_0+bt$, $z=z_0+ct$ |
| 3 | [[Concepts/Symmetric-Equations-of-a-Line.md]] | $\frac{x-x_0}{a} = \frac{y-y_0}{b} = \frac{z-z_0}{c}$ |
| 4 | [[Concepts/Scalar-Equation-of-a-Plane.md]] | $a(x-x_0)+b(y-y_0)+c(z-z_0)=0$ |
| 5 | [[Concepts/Normal-Vector-to-a-Plane.md]] | $\mathbf{n} = \langle a,b,c \rangle$ |
| 6 | [[Concepts/Intersections-Line-Plane.md]] | Solving systems of equations |
| 7 | [[Concepts/Distance-from-Point-to-Plane.md]] | $D = \frac{|ax_0+by_0+cz_0-d|}{\|\mathbf{n}\|}$ |
