# 12.6 Cylinders and Quadratic Surfaces

> **Stewart Calculus, Chapter 12, Section 12.6**

---

## Section Overview

Classifies quadric surfaces (ellipsoids, hyperboloids, paraboloids, cones) by their equations. Uses traces (cross-sections) to visualize and identify surfaces in 3D.
