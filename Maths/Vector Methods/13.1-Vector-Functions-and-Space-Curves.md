# 13.1 Vector Functions and Space Curves

> **Stewart Calculus, Chapter 13, Section 13.1**

---

## Section Overview

Introduces vector-valued functions $\mathbf{r}(t) = \langle f(t), g(t), h(t) \rangle$ and how they trace out space curves. Covers domain, limits, continuity, and parametric representations.
