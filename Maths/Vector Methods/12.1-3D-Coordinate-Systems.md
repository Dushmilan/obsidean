# 12.1 Three-Dimensional Coordinate Systems

> **Stewart Calculus, Chapter 12, Section 12.1**

---

## Section Overview

Extends 2D coordinate geometry to three dimensions. Introduces points in $\mathbb{R}^3$, the distance formula, spheres, and surfaces.
