# 13.4 Motion in Space: Velocity and Acceleration

> **Stewart Calculus, Chapter 13, Section 13.4**

---

## Section Overview

Applies vector calculus to kinematics. Defines velocity and acceleration vectors, decomposes acceleration into tangential and normal components, and applies to projectile motion and planetary orbits.

---

## Concepts (9)

| # | Concept | Key Idea |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | [[Concepts/Velocity-Vector.md]] | $\mathbf{v}(t) = \mathbf{r}'(t)$ |
| 2 | [[Concepts/Speed.md]] | $v(t) = \|\mathbf{v}(t)\| = \frac{ds}{dt}$ |
| 3 | [[Concepts/Acceleration-Vector.md]] | $\mathbf{a}(t) = \mathbf{v}'(t) = \mathbf{r}''(t)$ |
| 4 | [[Concepts/Newtons-Second-Law-in-Vector-Form.md]] | $\mathbf{F} = m\mathbf{a}$ |
| 5 | [[Concepts/Projectile-Motion.md]] | $\mathbf{a}(t) = -g\mathbf{k}$ |
| 6 | [[Concepts/Tangential-Component-of-Acceleration.md]] | $a_T = \frac{\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{a}}{\|\mathbf{v}\|}$ |
| 7 | [[Concepts/Normal-Component-of-Acceleration.md]] | $a_N = \frac{\|\mathbf{v}\times\mathbf{a}\|}{\|\mathbf{v}\|} = \kappa v^2$ |
| 8 | [[Concepts/Acceleration-Vector-Decomposition.md]] | $\mathbf{a} = a_T\mathbf{T} + a_N\mathbf{N}$ |
| 9 | [[Concepts/Keplers-Laws-of-Planetary-Motion.md]] | Planetary orbits as applications of vector calculus |
