# 6.2 Moment of Inertia of Standard Bodies

**Syllabus Reference:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) - Applied Mathematics Paper II, Topic 6.2

---

## 1. Thin Uniform Rod

### 1.1 About an Axis Through Centre, Perpendicular to Rod
Let rod lie along $x$-axis from $-L/2$ to $L/2$, axis at $x=0$ perpendicular to rod.

$$\lambda = \frac{M}{L}, \quad dm = \lambda dx, \quad r = |x|$$

$$I = \int_{-L/2}^{L/2} x^2 \lambda \, dx = \lambda \left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_{-L/2}^{L/2} = \frac{M}{L} \cdot \frac{L^3}{12} = \frac{1}{12}ML^2$$

**Radius of gyration:** $k^2 = \frac{L^2}{12}$

### 1.2 About an Axis Through One End, Perpendicular to Rod
Using parallel axes theorem: $d = L/2$

$$I = I_{CM} + M(L/2)^2 = \frac{1}{12}ML^2 + \frac{1}{4}ML^2 = \frac{1}{3}ML^2$$

**Radius of gyration:** $k^2 = \frac{L^2}{3}$

---

## 2. Thin Circular Ring (Radius $R$)

### 2.1 About Axis Through Centre, Perpendicular to Plane
All mass at distance $R$:

$$I = \int R^2 dm = R^2 \int dm = MR^2$$

**Radius of gyration:** $k^2 = R^2$

### 2.2 About a Diameter
By perpendicular axes theorem: $I_z = I_x + I_y = 2I_{diam}$

$$I_{diam} = \frac{1}{2}MR^2$$

**Radius of gyration:** $k^2 = \frac{R^2}{2}$

### 2.3 About a Tangent in the Plane
Parallel axes: $d = R$

$$I = I_{diam} + MR^2 = \frac{1}{2}MR^2 + MR^2 = \frac{3}{2}MR^2$$

### 2.4 About a Tangent Perpendicular to Plane
Parallel axes: $d = R$

$$I = MR^2 + MR^2 = 2MR^2$$

---

## 3. Uniform Circular Disc (Radius $R$)

### 3.1 About Axis Through Centre, Perpendicular to Plane
Disc = concentric rings of radius $r$, width $dr$.

Area of ring: $dA = 2\pi r dr$, mass: $dm = \sigma dA = \frac{M}{\pi R^2} 2\pi r dr = \frac{2M}{R^2} r dr$

$$I = \int_0^R r^2 dm = \frac{2M}{R^2} \int_0^R r^3 dr = \frac{2M}{R^2} \cdot \frac{R^4}{4} = \frac{1}{2}MR^2$$

**Radius of gyration:** $k^2 = \frac{R^2}{2}$

### 3.2 About a Diameter
By perpendicular axes theorem:

$$I_{diam} = \frac{1}{2} I_{\perp} = \frac{1}{4}MR^2$$

**Radius of gyration:** $k^2 = \frac{R^2}{4}$

### 3.3 About a Tangent in the Plane
Parallel axes: $d = R$

$$I = \frac{1}{4}MR^2 + MR^2 = \frac{5}{4}MR^2$$

### 3.4 About a Tangent Perpendicular to Plane
Parallel axes: $d = R$

$$I = \frac{1}{2}MR^2 + MR^2 = \frac{3}{2}MR^2$$

---

## 4. Uniform Solid Sphere (Radius $R$)

### 4.1 About a Diameter
Sphere = discs of thickness $dx$ at distance $x$ from centre.

Radius of disc: $y = \sqrt{R^2 - x^2}$, area: $\pi y^2 = \pi(R^2 - x^2)$
Volume of disc: $dV = \pi(R^2 - x^2)dx$
Mass: $dm = \rho dV = \frac{M}{\frac{4}{3}\pi R^3} \pi(R^2 - x^2)dx = \frac{3M}{4R^3}(R^2 - x^2)dx$

MI of disc about its diameter: $dI = \frac{1}{4} dm y^2 = \frac{1}{4} dm (R^2 - x^2)$

$$I = \int_{-R}^R \frac{1}{4} \cdot \frac{3M}{4R^3}(R^2 - x^2)^2 dx = \frac{3M}{16R^3} \int_{-R}^R (R^2 - x^2)^2 dx$$

$$= \frac{3M}{16R^3} \cdot \frac{16}{15}R^5 = \frac{2}{5}MR^2$$

**Radius of gyration:** $k^2 = \frac{2R^2}{5}$

---

## 5. Uniform Hollow Sphere (Thin Spherical Shell, Radius $R$)

### 5.1 About a Diameter
Shell = rings of radius $R\sin\theta$, width $R d\theta$.

Circumference: $2\pi R\sin\theta$, area: $dA = 2\pi R^2 \sin\theta d\theta$
Mass: $dm = \sigma dA = \frac{M}{4\pi R^2} 2\pi R^2 \sin\theta d\theta = \frac{M}{2} \sin\theta d\theta$

Distance from diameter: $r = R\sin\theta$

$$I = \int_0^\pi r^2 dm = \int_0^\pi (R\sin\theta)^2 \cdot \frac{M}{2} \sin\theta d\theta = \frac{MR^2}{2} \int_0^\pi \sin^3\theta d\theta$$

$$\int_0^\pi \sin^3\theta d\theta = \frac{4}{3} \quad \Rightarrow \quad I = \frac{2}{3}MR^2$$

**Radius of gyration:** $k^2 = \frac{2R^2}{3}$

---

## 6. Rectangular Lamina ($a \times b$)

### 6.1 About Axis Through Centre, Perpendicular to Plane
$$\sigma = \frac{M}{ab}, \quad dm = \sigma dx dy$$

$$I = \int_{-a/2}^{a/2} \int_{-b/2}^{b/2} (x^2 + y^2) \sigma dy dx = \sigma \left( \frac{a^3b}{12} + \frac{ab^3}{12} \right) = \frac{1}{12}M(a^2 + b^2)$$

### 6.2 About Axis Through Centre, Parallel to Side $b$
$$I = \int_{-a/2}^{a/2} \int_{-b/2}^{b/2} x^2 \sigma dy dx = \frac{1}{12}Ma^2$$

### 6.3 About Axis Through Centre, Parallel to Side $a$
$$I = \frac{1}{12}Mb^2$$

## 8. Common Composite Bodies

### Rod with Ring at End
$I = \frac{1}{3}ML^2 + MR^2 + M_{\text{ring}}L^2$

### Disc with Rod as Diameter
$I_{\perp} = \frac{1}{2}MR^2 + \frac{1}{12}M_{\text{rod}}L^2$ (rod through centre)

### Sphere on Rod
Use parallel axes for each component about the chosen axis.
