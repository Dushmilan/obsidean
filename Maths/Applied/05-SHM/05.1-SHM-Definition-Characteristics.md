# 5.1 SHM Definition & Characteristics

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 5.1  
**Prerequisites:** Pure Maths Calculus (2nd order ODEs), Trigonometry (cos/sin), Algebra

---

## Defining Equation

Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM) is defined by the differential equation:

$$
\frac{d^2x}{dt^2} = -\omega^2 x \quad \text{or} \quad \ddot{x} + \omega^2 x = 0
$$

where:
- $x$ = displacement from equilibrium position
- $\omega$ = **angular frequency** (rad/s), constant for a given system
- The negative sign indicates the acceleration is always directed towards equilibrium (restoring)

---

## General Solution

The general solution to $\ddot{x} + \omega^2 x = 0$:

$$
x(t) = A\cos(\omega t + \phi) \quad \text{or} \quad x(t) = A\sin(\omega t + \phi')
$$

where:
- $A$ = **amplitude** (maximum displacement from equilibrium)
- $\phi$ = **phase constant** (determined by initial conditions)
- $\phi' = \phi + \pi/2$

**Equivalent forms:**
$$
\begin{aligned}
x(t) &= C_1\cos(\omega t) + C_2\sin(\omega t) \quad (C_1 = A\cos\phi,\; C_2 = -A\sin\phi) \\
x(t) &= \operatorname{Re}\left[A e^{i(\omega t + \phi)}\right] \quad \text{(complex exponential form)}
\end{aligned}
$$

---

## Key Quantities

| Quantity | Symbol | Expression | Units |
|----------|--------|------------|-------|
| Angular frequency | $\omega$ | $\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$ (spring) or $\sqrt{\frac{g}{L}}$ (pendulum) | rad/s |
| Period | $T$ | $\frac{2\pi}{\omega} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}$ or $2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}$ | s |
| Frequency | $f$ | $\frac{1}{T} = \frac{\omega}{2\pi}$ | Hz |
| Amplitude | $A$ | $\sqrt{x_0^2 + (v_0/\omega)^2}$ | m |
| Phase constant | $\phi$ | $\arctan(-v_0/\omega x_0)$ | rad |

---

## Velocity and Acceleration

Given $x = A\cos(\omega t + \phi)$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Velocity: } v &= \frac{dx}{dt} = -A\omega\sin(\omega t + \phi) = \pm\omega\sqrt{A^2 - x^2} \\
\text{Acceleration: } a &= \frac{dv}{dt} = -A\omega^2\cos(\omega t + \phi) = -\omega^2 x
\end{aligned}
$$

**Key relationships:**
- $v_{max} = A\omega$ (at $x = 0$)
- $a_{max} = A\omega^2$ (at $x = \pm A$)
- $v$ leads $x$ by $\pi/2$; $a$ leads $v$ by $\pi/2$ (or $a$ is $\pi$ out of phase with $x$)

---

## Phase Space Representation

The phase space trajectory is an **ellipse**:

$$
\frac{x^2}{A^2} + \frac{v^2}{A^2\omega^2} = 1 \quad \text{or} \quad \frac{x^2}{A^2} + \frac{v^2}{v_{max}^2} = 1
$$

Area of ellipse = $\pi A \cdot A\omega = \pi A^2 \omega = \frac{2\pi E}{\omega}$ (proportional to total energy)

---

## Initial Conditions

Given $x(0) = x_0$, $v(0) = v_0$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
A &= \sqrt{x_0^2 + \left(\frac{v_0}{\omega}\right)^2} \\
\phi &= \arctan\left(-\frac{v_0}{\omega x_0}\right) \quad \text{(adjust quadrant)}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Special cases:**
- Released from rest at $x = A$: $x_0 = A,\; v_0 = 0 \implies \phi = 0 \implies x = A\cos(\omega t)$
- Given impulse at equilibrium: $x_0 = 0,\; v_0 = v_0 \implies \phi = -\pi/2 \implies x = A\sin(\omega t)$

---

## Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Equilibrium position** | $x = 0$; net force zero |
| **Amplitude ($A$)** | Maximum displacement from equilibrium |
| **Period ($T$)** | Time for one complete oscillation |
| **Frequency ($f$)** | Number of oscillations per unit time ($f = 1/T$) |
| **Angular frequency ($\omega$)** | $2\pi f = 2\pi/T$; relates to system parameters |
| **Phase ($\omega t + \phi$)** | Argument of cosine/sine; evolves linearly with time |
| **Phase constant ($\phi$)** | Initial phase at $t = 0$ |
| **Phase difference** | Difference in phase between two oscillators |

---

## Isochronous Property

**For ideal SHM: Period $T$ is independent of amplitude $A$.**  
This follows from $\omega$ depending only on system parameters ($k, m$ or $g, L$), not $A$.

> **Exception:** Simple pendulum is only isochronous for small angles ($\theta \lesssim 10^\circ$). For large angles, $T$ increases with $A$ (see Topic 5.3).

---

## Identifying SHM

A motion is SHM if **any** of these equivalent conditions hold:

1. **Acceleration equation:** $a = -\omega^2 x$
2. **Force equation:** $F = -kx$ (Hooke's law type restoring force)
3. **Potential energy:** $U = \frac{1}{2}kx^2$ (quadratic in $x$)
4. **Differential equation:** $\ddot{x} + \omega^2 x = 0$
5. **Solution form:** $x = A\cos(\omega t + \phi)$ with constant $\omega$

**To prove SHM:**
1. Find equation of motion (Newton's 2nd law or energy method)
2. Show it reduces to $\ddot{x} + \omega^2 x = 0$ for displacement from equilibrium
3. Identify $\omega$ and hence $T = 2\pi/\omega$
