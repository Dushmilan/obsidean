# 5.4 Energy in Simple Harmonic Motion

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 5.4  
**Prerequisites:** Work-Energy Theorem, Conservation of Energy, SHM Definition (5.1), Spring Systems (5.2)

---

## Energy in a Spring-Mass System

### Potential Energy (Spring)
For a spring with constant $k$, extension $x$ from natural length:
$$
U_{spring} = \frac{1}{2}kx^2
$$

### Potential Energy (Vertical Spring)
With mass $m$, equilibrium extension $x_0 = mg/k$, displacement $y$ from equilibrium:
$$
U_{total} = U_{spring} + U_{grav} = \frac{1}{2}k(x_0 + y)^2 - mg(x_0 + y)
$$
Substitute $mg = kx_0$:
$$
U_{total} = \frac{1}{2}k(x_0^2 + 2x_0y + y^2) - kx_0(x_0 + y)
$$
$$
= \frac{1}{2}ky^2 - \frac{1}{2}kx_0^2
$$

**Key result:** Near equilibrium, $U_{total} = \frac{1}{2}ky^2 + \text{constant}$. The constant doesn't affect dynamics.

### Kinetic Energy
$$
K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2}m\dot{x}^2
$$

For SHM: $x = A\cos(\omega t + \phi)$, $\dot{x} = -A\omega\sin(\omega t + \phi)$
$$
K = \frac{1}{2}mA^2\omega^2\sin^2(\omega t + \phi) = \frac{1}{2}kA^2\sin^2(\omega t + \phi)
$$

### Total Energy
$$
E = K + U = \frac{1}{2}kA^2\sin^2(\omega t + \phi) + \frac{1}{2}kA^2\cos^2(\omega t + \phi) = \frac{1}{2}kA^2
$$

**Total energy is constant** (conserved) and equals maximum potential energy = maximum kinetic energy.

## Energy-Time Graphs

| Quantity | Expression | Phase | Max Value |
|----------|------------|-------|-----------|
| Displacement | $x = A\cos(\omega t + \phi)$ | $\omega t + \phi$ | $A$ |
| Velocity | $v = -A\omega\sin(\omega t + \phi)$ | $\omega t + \phi + \pi/2$ | $A\omega$ |
| Acceleration | $a = -A\omega^2\cos(\omega t + \phi)$ | $\omega t + \phi + \pi$ | $A\omega^2$ |
| Kinetic Energy | $K = \frac{1}{2}kA^2\sin^2(\omega t + \phi)$ | $2(\omega t + \phi) + \pi$ | $\frac{1}{2}kA^2$ |
| Potential Energy | $U = \frac{1}{2}kA^2\cos^2(\omega t + \phi)$ | $2(\omega t + \phi)$ | $\frac{1}{2}kA^2$ |
| Total Energy | $E = \frac{1}{2}kA^2$ | constant | $\frac{1}{2}kA^2$ |

**Key observations:**
- $K$ and $U$ oscillate at **twice the frequency** of $x$ and $v$ ($\omega_K = 2\omega$)
- $K$ and $U$ are **out of phase by $\pi$** (when one is max, other is min)
- $E = K + U$ is constant

## Energy as a Function of Displacement

Using $v^2 = \omega^2(A^2 - x^2)$ and $\omega^2 = k/m$:

**Kinetic energy:**
$$
K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2(A^2 - x^2) = \frac{1}{2}k(A^2 - x^2)
$$

**Potential energy:**
$$
U = \frac{1}{2}kx^2
$$

**Total energy:**
$$
E = K + U = \frac{1}{2}k(A^2 - x^2) + \frac{1}{2}kx^2 = \frac{1}{2}kA^2
$$

**Velocity at displacement $x$:**
$$
v = \pm\omega\sqrt{A^2 - x^2} = \pm\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}(A^2 - x^2)}
$$

## Energy Method for Finding $\omega$

For any conservative 1D system with potential energy $U(x)$ near stable equilibrium $x_0$:

1. Expand $U(x)$ about $x_0$:
   $$
   U(x) \approx U(x_0) + U'(x_0)(x-x_0) + \frac{1}{2}U''(x_0)(x-x_0)^2
   $$
   At equilibrium: $U'(x_0) = 0$.

2. Effective potential: $U_{eff}(y) = \frac{1}{2}U''(x_0)y^2$ where $y = x - x_0$.

3. Compare with $\frac{1}{2}ky^2$: effective spring constant $k_{eff} = U''(x_0)$.

4. Angular frequency:
   $$
   \omega = \sqrt{\frac{k_{eff}}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{U''(x_0)}{m}}
   $$

**Example: Simple pendulum**
$$
U = mgL(1-\cos\theta) \approx \frac{1}{2}mgL\theta^2 \quad (U''(0) = mgL)
$$
$$
\omega = \sqrt{\frac{mgL}{mL^2}} = \sqrt{\frac{g}{L}}
$$

## Potential Energy Diagrams

For SHM, potential energy is a **parabola** near equilibrium:

```
U
↑
|        /\
|       /  \
|      /    \      Parabolic well
|     /      \
|    /        \
|___/__________\___→ x
    x₀
```

**Stable equilibrium:** $U''(x_0) > 0$ (minimum of $U$) → SHM for small displacements.

**Unstable equilibrium:** $U''(x_0) < 0$ (maximum of $U$) → exponential divergence.

**Neutral equilibrium:** $U''(x_0) = 0$ → no restoring force.

## Energy in Compound Systems

### Two masses on a spring (free-free)

Masses $m_1, m_2$ connected by spring $k$, no external forces.

**Relative coordinate:** $x = x_2 - x_1 - L_0$

**Reduced mass:** $\mu = \frac{m_1 m_2}{m_1 + m_2}$

**Total kinetic energy in COM frame:**
$$
K = \frac{1}{2}\mu \dot{x}^2
$$

**Potential energy:** $U = \frac{1}{2}kx^2$

**Total energy:** $E = \frac{1}{2}\mu\dot{x}^2 + \frac{1}{2}kx^2 = \frac{1}{2}kA^2$

**Frequency:** $\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{\mu}}$

---

### Coupled Oscillators (Two masses, three springs)

**Setup:** Wall — $k_1$ — $m_1$ — $k_2$ — $m_2$ — $k_3$ — Wall

**Normal modes:**
- Symmetric: $x_1 = x_2$, middle spring unstretched
- Antisymmetric: $x_1 = -x_2$, middle spring maximally active

**Energy exchange:** Energy transfers between masses (beats).

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Energy Fractions
SHM oscillator $m = 0.5$ kg, $k = 200$ N/m, amplitude $A = 0.1$ m.
Find $K$ and $U$ when $x = 0.05$ m.

**Solution:**
$$
E = \frac{1}{2}kA^2 = \frac{1}{2}(200)(0.1)^2 = 1 \text{ J}
$$
$$
U = \frac{1}{2}kx^2 = \frac{1}{2}(200)(0.05)^2 = 0.25 \text{ J}
$$
$$
K = E - U = 0.75 \text{ J}
$$
$K/E = 75\%$, $U/E = 25\%$.

---

### Example 2: Finding Amplitude from Energy
Spring $k = 400$ N/m, mass 2 kg, total energy 5 J. Find amplitude and max speed.

**Solution:**
$$
E = \frac{1}{2}kA^2 \implies A = \sqrt{\frac{2E}{k}} = \sqrt{\frac{10}{400}} = \sqrt{0.025} = 0.158 \text{ m}
$$
$$
v_{max} = \omega A = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}A = \sqrt{200} \times 0.158 \approx 2.24 \text{ m/s}
$$

---

### Example 3: Energy Method for Pendulum
Simple pendulum length $L$, mass $m$, amplitude $\theta_0$. Find speed at angle $\theta$.

**Solution:**
Total energy: $E = mgL(1-\cos\theta_0)$
At angle $\theta$: $E = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + mgL(1-\cos\theta)$
$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^2 = mgL(\cos\theta - \cos\theta_0)
$$
$$
v = \sqrt{2gL(\cos\theta - \cos\theta_0)}
$$

---

### Example 4: Particle in Potential Well
Potential $U(x) = \frac{1}{2}ax^2 + \frac{1}{4}bx^4$ ($a,b > 0$). Find frequency of small oscillations about $x=0$.

**Solution:**
$$
U'(x) = ax + bx^3 \implies U'(0) = 0 \text{ (equilibrium)}
$$
$$
U''(x) = a + 3bx^2 \implies U''(0) = a
$$
$$
k_{eff} = a, \quad \omega = \sqrt{\frac{a}{m}}
$$

## GCE A/L Exam Patterns

| Question Type | Key Points |
|---------------|------------|
| "Show that total energy is constant" | $E = \frac{1}{2}kA^2$, differentiate to show $dE/dt = 0$ |
| "Find KE/PE at given $x$" | $K = \frac{1}{2}k(A^2 - x^2)$, $U = \frac{1}{2}kx^2$ |
| "Energy method to find $\omega$" | $U(x) \approx \frac{1}{2}U''(x_0)y^2$, $\omega = \sqrt{U''(x_0)/m}$ |
| "Fraction of energy kinetic at $x = A/2$" | $K/E = 1 - (x/A)^2 = 1 - 1/4 = 3/4$ |
| "Speed at given displacement" | $v = \omega\sqrt{A^2 - x^2}$ |
| "Potential well, find period" | $U''(x_0) = k_{eff}$, $T = 2\pi\sqrt{m/k_{eff}}$ |
