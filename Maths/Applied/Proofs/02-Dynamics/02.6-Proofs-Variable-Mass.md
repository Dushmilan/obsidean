# Proofs: Variable Mass Systems

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 2.6

---

## Proof 1: General Variable Mass Equation (Momentum Form)

**Statement:** For system of variable mass $M(t)$:
$$
M\frac{dv}{dt} = v_{\text{rel}}\frac{dM}{dt} + \sum F_{\text{ext}}
$$
where $v_{\text{rel}}$ = velocity of added/removed mass relative to system.

**Proof:**
At time $t$: system mass $M$, velocity $v$, momentum $p = Mv$.
At $t+dt$: mass $M+dM$, velocity $v+dv$.
Mass added/removed: $dm = -dM$ (if $dM<0$, mass leaves).

Momentum at $t+dt$:
- System: $(M+dM)(v+dv)$
- Added/removed mass: $dm(v + v_{\text{rel}})$

Total momentum (system + added/removed):
$$
p(t+dt) = (M+dM)(v+dv) + dm(v + v_{\text{rel}})
$$

Change in momentum:
$$
dp = p(t+dt) - p(t) = M\,dv + v\,dM + dm\,v + dm\,v_{\text{rel}}
$$
Since $dm = -dM$:
$$
dp = M\,dv + v\,dM - dM\,v - dM\,v_{\text{rel}} = M\,dv - v_{\text{rel}}\,dM
$$

Newton's 2nd law for whole system: $dp = \sum F_{\text{ext}}\,dt$.
Thus $M\,dv - v_{\text{rel}}\,dM = \sum F_{\text{ext}}\,dt$.
Divide by $dt$: $M\frac{dv}{dt} = v_{\text{rel}}\frac{dM}{dt} + \sum F_{\text{ext}}$. ∎

## Proof 2: Rocket Equation (No External Forces)

**Statement:** For rocket ejecting mass at rate $\dot{m} = -\frac{dM}{dt} > 0$ with exhaust velocity $u$ relative to rocket:
$$
M\frac{dv}{dt} = u\frac{dM}{dt} \implies \Delta v = u\ln\left(\frac{M_0}{M}\right)
$$

**Proof:**
No external forces: $\sum F_{\text{ext}} = 0$.
Ejected mass velocity relative to rocket: $v_{\text{rel}} = -u$ (opposite to rocket motion).
Equation: $M\frac{dv}{dt} = -u\frac{dM}{dt}$.

Rearrange: $\frac{dv}{dt} = -u\frac{1}{M}\frac{dM}{dt} = -u\frac{d}{dt}(\ln M)$.
Integrate: $v - v_0 = -u(\ln M - \ln M_0) = u\ln\left(\frac{M_0}{M}\right)$. ∎

## Proof 3: Rocket with Gravity (Vertical Launch)

**Statement:** $v = u\ln\left(\frac{M_0}{M}\right) - gt$

**Proof:**
External force: gravity $F_{\text{ext}} = -Mg$ (upward positive).
Equation: $M\frac{dv}{dt} = u\frac{dM}{dt} - Mg$.
Divide by $M$: $\frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{u}{M}\frac{dM}{dt} - g$.
Integrate: $v - v_0 = u\ln\left(\frac{M_0}{M}\right) - gt$. ∎

## Proof 4: Rocket with Constant Burn Rate and Gravity

**Statement:** If $\frac{dM}{dt} = -k$ (constant), $M = M_0 - kt$:
$$
v = u\ln\left(\frac{M_0}{M_0 - kt}\right) - gt
$$

**Proof:**
Substitute $M(t) = M_0 - kt$ into Proof 3 result. ∎

## Proof 5: Chain Falling onto Scale

**Statement:** Force on scale when chain length $x$ has fallen: $F = 3\lambda g x$

**Proof:**
Chain linear density $\lambda$. At time $t$, length $x$ at rest on scale, length $L-x$ falling.
Velocity of falling part: $v = gt$ (from rest).
Mass falling rate: $\frac{dm}{dt} = \lambda v = \lambda gt$.

Force on scale = weight of resting part + rate of momentum change:
- Weight: $\lambda x g$
- Momentum arrival: falling mass $dm$ stops in $dt$, impulse $dm \cdot v$.
  Force = $\frac{dm}{dt} v = (\lambda gt)(gt) = \lambda g^2 t^2 = \lambda v^2$.

Since $x = \frac{1}{2}gt^2$, $v^2 = g^2t^2 = 2gx$.
Momentum force = $\lambda(2gx) = 2\lambda gx$.

Total force: $F = \lambda g x + 2\lambda g x = 3\lambda g x$. ∎

## Proof 6: Chain Sliding off Smooth Table

**Statement:** Velocity when length $x$ hanging: $v^2 = \frac{g}{L}(x^2 - x_0^2)$

**Proof:**
Let $x$ = length hanging, $L$ = total length, $\lambda$ = linear density.
Mass of chain: $M = \lambda L$.
Hanging mass: $m = \lambda x$.

External force (weight of hanging part): $F_{\text{ext}} = \lambda x g$.

System momentum: $p = Mv = \lambda L v$.
Variable mass equation: $\frac{dp}{dt} = F_{\text{ext}}$.
$\lambda L \frac{dv}{dt} = \lambda x g \implies L v \frac{dv}{dx} = x g$.

Integrate: $\int_{x_0}^x x\,dx = \int_0^v L v\,dv \implies \frac{1}{2}(x^2 - x_0^2) = \frac{1}{2}L v^2$.
$v^2 = \frac{g}{L}(x^2 - x_0^2)$. ∎

## Proof 7: Chain Lifted Vertically at Constant Speed

**Statement:** Force required: $F = \lambda g x + \lambda v^2$

**Proof:**
At time $t$, lifted length $x = vt$, mass lifted $m = \lambda x$.
Weight of lifted part: $\lambda x g$.
Rate of momentum increase: mass added per $dt$ is $dm = \lambda v\,dt$.
Momentum given to added mass: $dm \cdot v = \lambda v^2\,dt$.
Force = weight + momentum rate = $\lambda g x + \lambda v^2$. ∎

## Proof 8: Raindrop Accretion (Mass Gain)

**Statement:** $m\frac{dv}{dt} = -v\frac{dm}{dt} + mg \implies \frac{d}{dt}(mv) = mg$

**Proof:**
Drop mass $m$, velocity $v$ downward.
Accretes stationary water at rate $\frac{dm}{dt} > 0$.
Relative velocity of accreted mass: $v_{\text{rel}} = 0 - v = -v$.

Equation: $m\frac{dv}{dt} = v_{\text{rel}}\frac{dm}{dt} + mg = -v\frac{dm}{dt} + mg$.
Rearrange: $m\frac{dv}{dt} + v\frac{dm}{dt} = mg \implies \frac{d}{dt}(mv) = mg$. ∎

## Proof 9: Rocket with Linear Drag

**Statement:** $M\frac{dv}{dt} = u\frac{dM}{dt} - Mg - kv$

**Proof:**
External forces: gravity $-Mg$, drag $-kv$.
Equation: $M\frac{dv}{dt} = u\frac{dM}{dt} - Mg - kv$. ∎

## Proof 10: Rocket with Quadratic Drag

**Statement:** $M\frac{dv}{dt} = u\frac{dM}{dt} - Mg - kv^2$

**Proof:**
Same as Proof 9 with drag $F_d = -kv^2$. ∎

## Proof 11: Chain on Table with Friction

**Statement:** $L v \frac{dv}{dx} = x g - \mu(L-x)g$

**Proof:**
Hanging part weight: $\lambda x g$.
Friction on horizontal part: $\mu \lambda (L-x) g$ (opposes motion).
Net force: $\lambda x g - \mu \lambda (L-x) g$.

Equation: $M\frac{dv}{dt} = F_{\text{ext}}$.
$\lambda L v \frac{dv}{dx} = \lambda g[x - \mu(L-x)]$.
$L v \frac{dv}{dx} = g[x - \mu(L-x)]$. ∎

## Proof 12: Rope Unwinding from Cylinder

**Statement:** $(M - \lambda x)\dot{v} = \lambda x g - T$, with $T = \frac{I}{R^2}v$

**Proof:**
Mass of rope: $M = \lambda L$.
Mass still on cylinder: $m = M - \lambda x$.
Weight of hanging part: $\lambda x g$.
Tension in rope: $T$.

Translation: $(M - \lambda x)\dot{v} = \lambda x g - T$.
Rotation: $I\alpha = TR$, with $\alpha = v/R \implies T = \frac{I}{R^2}v$.
Combine: $(M - \lambda x)\dot{v} = \lambda x g - \frac{I}{R^2}v$. ∎

## Proof 13: Avalanche / Landslide (Entrainment)

**Statement:** $\frac{d}{dt}(Mv) = Mg\sin\theta - \mu Mg\cos\theta$

**Proof:**
Avalanche mass $M(t)$, gains stationary material.
Relative velocity of entrained mass: $v_{\text{rel}} = -v$ (avalanche catches stationary snow).
Equation: $M\dot{v} = v_{\text{rel}}\dot{M} + Mg\sin\theta - \mu Mg\cos\theta$
$M\dot{v} = -v\dot{M} + Mg(\sin\theta - \mu\cos\theta)$.

Since $\frac{d}{dt}(Mv) = M\dot{v} + v\dot{M}$:
$\frac{d}{dt}(Mv) = Mg(\sin\theta - \mu\cos\theta)$. ∎

## Proof 14: Water Jet Hitting Wall

**Statement:** Force on wall $F = \rho A v^2$ (jet stops) or $F = \rho A v^2(1+e)$ (rebounds with $e$)

**Proof:**
Mass per second: $\dot{m} = \rho A v$.
Momentum per second arriving: $\dot{m}v = \rho A v^2$.

If jet stops: $\Delta p/\Delta t = \rho A v^2 = F$.
If rebounds with $e$: outgoing momentum $\dot{m}(ev)$.
Force = rate of momentum change = $\rho A v(1+e)v = \rho A v^2(1+e)$. ∎

---

## Proof 15: Hovercraft / Air Cushion

**Statement:** Upward force $F = \rho A v^2$ supports weight $Mg$.

**Proof:**
Air blown downward at speed $v$ through area $A$.
Mass flow: $\dot{m} = \rho A v$.
Momentum per second downward: $\dot{m}v = \rho A v^2$.
Reaction force upward = $\rho A v^2$.
For hover: $\rho A v^2 = Mg$. ∎

---

## Proof 16: Sand Falling on Conveyor Belt

**Statement:** Extra force needed: $F = \dot{m}v$ (if sand added at speed $v$)

**Proof:**
Belt speed $v$. Sand added at rate $\dot{m}$ with zero horizontal velocity.
Sand must be accelerated to $v$.
Rate of momentum increase: $\dot{m}v$.
Force required = $\dot{m}v$. ∎

## Proof 17: Rocket Optimal Staging (Conceptual)

**Statement:** For multistage rocket, optimal mass ratio maximizes $\Delta v$ when stages have equal $\Delta v$ (if same $u$).

**Proof:**
Total $\Delta v = \sum u_i \ln(M_{0i}/M_{fi})$.
For fixed total mass and same $u$, by symmetry equal $\Delta v$ per stage maximizes sum.
(Tsiolkovsky's result for identical stages). ∎

## Proof 18: Photon Rocket

**Statement:** $M\frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{P}{c}$ where $P$ = power, $c$ = speed of light.

**Proof:**
Photon momentum: $p = E/c$.
Power $P = dE/dt \implies$ momentum rate $dp/dt = P/c$.
Thrust = $P/c$.
Equation: $M\frac{dv}{dt} = P/c$. ∎

## Proof 19: Variable Mass System Energy

**Statement:** $\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{1}{2}Mv^2\right) = \vec{F}_{\text{ext}}\cdot\vec{v} + \frac{1}{2}v_{\text{rel}}^2\frac{dM}{dt} + \vec{v}\cdot\vec{v}_{\text{rel}}\frac{dM}{dt}$

**Proof:**
$K = \frac{1}{2}Mv^2$.
$\frac{dK}{dt} = \frac{1}{2}\dot{M}v^2 + Mv\dot{v}$.
From variable mass eq: $M\dot{v} = v_{\text{rel}}\dot{M} + F_{\text{ext}}$.
Substitute:
$\frac{dK}{dt} = \frac{1}{2}\dot{M}v^2 + v(v_{\text{rel}}\dot{M} + F_{\text{ext}})$
$= F_{\text{ext}}v + \dot{M}(v v_{\text{rel}} + \frac{1}{2}v^2)$.

For mass ejection ($\dot{M} < 0$, $v_{\text{rel}} = -u$):
$\frac{dK}{dt} = F_{\text{ext}}v - \dot{m}(-uv + \frac{1}{2}v^2) = F_{\text{ext}}v + \dot{m}(uv - \frac{1}{2}v^2)$.

The term $\dot{m}\frac{1}{2}u^2$ is KE of exhaust. ∎

## Proof 20: Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation with Gravity (Integral Form)

**Statement:** $v = u\ln\frac{M_0}{M} - \int_0^t g(t')\,dt'$

**Proof:**
From $M\frac{dv}{dt} = u\frac{dM}{dt} - Mg(t)$.
$\frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{u}{M}\frac{dM}{dt} - g(t)$.
Integrate: $v(t) - v_0 = u\ln\frac{M_0}{M} - \int_0^t g(t')\,dt'$. ∎
