# Proofs: Collisions

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 2.5

---

## Proof 1: Direct Collision Velocity Formulas

**Statement:** For masses $m_1, m_2$ with initial velocities $u_1, u_2$:
$$
v_1 = \frac{m_1 - em_2}{m_1 + m_2}u_1 + \frac{m_2(1+e)}{m_1 + m_2}u_2
$$
$$
v_2 = \frac{m_1(1+e)}{m_1 + m_2}u_1 + \frac{m_2 - em_1}{m_1 + m_2}u_2
$$

**Proof:**
From momentum: $m_1u_1 + m_2u_2 = m_1v_1 + m_2v_2$  (1)
From restitution: $v_2 - v_1 = e(u_1 - u_2)$  (2)

From (2): $v_2 = v_1 + e(u_1 - u_2)$.

Substitute into (1):
$$
m_1u_1 + m_2u_2 = m_1v_1 + m_2[v_1 + e(u_1 - u_2)]
= (m_1 + m_2)v_1 + m_2e(u_1 - u_2)
$$
$$
(m_1 + m_2)v_1 = m_1u_1 + m_2u_2 - m_2e(u_1 - u_2)
= (m_1 - em_2)u_1 + m_2(1+e)u_2
$$
Divide by $m_1+m_2$ for $v_1$.

Then $v_2 = v_1 + e(u_1 - u_2)$ gives the second formula. ∎

## Proof 2: Kinetic Energy Loss in Direct Collision

**Statement:**
$$
\Delta K = -\frac{1}{2}\frac{m_1m_2}{m_1+m_2}(1-e^2)(u_1 - u_2)^2 \leq 0
$$

**Proof:**
Let $u = u_1 - u_2$, $v = v_2 - v_1 = eu$ (restitution).
Centre of mass velocity: $V = \frac{m_1u_1 + m_2u_2}{m_1+m_2}$ (constant).

Initial KE: $K_i = \frac{1}{2}(m_1+m_2)V^2 + \frac{1}{2}\mu u^2$, where $\mu = \frac{m_1m_2}{m_1+m_2}$ (reduced mass).
Final KE: $K_f = \frac{1}{2}(m_1+m_2)V^2 + \frac{1}{2}\mu (eu)^2$.

$\Delta K = K_f - K_i = \frac{1}{2}\mu(e^2 - 1)u^2 = -\frac{1}{2}\frac{m_1m_2}{m_1+m_2}(1-e^2)(u_1-u_2)^2 \leq 0$. ∎

## Proof 3: Impulse in Direct Collision

**Statement:** $I = \frac{m_1m_2(1+e)}{m_1+m_2}(u_1 - u_2)$

**Proof:**
Impulse on $m_1$: $I = m_1(v_1 - u_1)$.

Substitute $v_1$:
$$
I = m_1\left[\frac{m_1 - em_2}{m_1+m_2}u_1 + \frac{m_2(1+e)}{m_1+m_2}u_2 - u_1\right]
= \frac{m_1m_2(1+e)}{m_1+m_2}(u_2 - u_1)
$$
Magnitude: $|I| = \frac{m_1m_2(1+e)}{m_1+m_2}|u_1 - u_2|$. ∎

## Proof 4: Oblique Collision — Tangential Velocity Unchanged

**Statement:** For smooth spheres, $v_{1t} = u_{1t}$, $v_{2t} = u_{2t}$.

**Proof:**
No friction $\implies$ no tangential impulse.
$\Delta p_t = I_t = 0 \implies m_1(v_{1t} - u_{1t}) = 0 \implies v_{1t} = u_{1t}$.
Similarly $v_{2t} = u_{2t}$. ∎

---

## Proof 5: Oblique Collision — Normal Velocity Formulas

**Statement:** Normal components satisfy same formulas as direct collision:
$$
v_{1n} = \frac{m_1 - em_2}{m_1+m_2}u_{1n} + \frac{m_2(1+e)}{m_1+m_2}u_{2n}
$$
$$
v_{2n} = \frac{m_1(1+e)}{m_1+m_2}u_{1n} + \frac{m_2 - em_1}{m_1+m_2}u_{2n}
$$

**Proof:**
Momentum and restitution apply only to normal components.
Tangential components are unchanged.
The equations for $u_{1n}, u_{2n}$ are identical to direct collision case. ∎

## Proof 6: Deflection Angle in Oblique Collision

**Statement:** For sphere 1, deflection angle $\phi$:
$$
\tan\phi = \frac{u_{1t}}{v_{1n}}
$$

**Proof:**
Initial velocity: $\vec{u}_1 = u_{1n}\hat{n} + u_{1t}\hat{t}$.
Final velocity: $\vec{v}_1 = v_{1n}\hat{n} + u_{1t}\hat{t}$.
Angle between $\vec{v}_1$ and line of centres: $\tan\phi = \frac{u_{1t}}{v_{1n}}$. ∎

## Proof 7: Collision with Fixed Smooth Plane

**Statement:** $v_n = -eu_n$, $v_t = u_t$.
Rebound angle: $\tan\theta' = \frac{\tan\theta}{e}$.

**Proof:**
Plane has infinite mass $\implies$ momentum conservation trivial.
Restitution: $v_n - 0 = e(0 - u_n) \implies v_n = -eu_n$.
No friction $\implies v_t = u_t$.

Rebound angle $\theta'$ to normal: $\tan\theta' = \frac{v_t}{|v_n|} = \frac{u_t}{eu_n} = \frac{\tan\theta}{e}$. ∎

## Proof 8: Ballistic Pendulum

**Statement:** $v_{\text{bullet}} = \frac{M+m}{m}\sqrt{2gh}$

**Proof:**
Stage 1 (inelastic collision): $mv = (M+m)V$.
Stage 2 (swing): $\frac{1}{2}(M+m)V^2 = (M+m)gh \implies V = \sqrt{2gh}$.
Substitute: $mv = (M+m)\sqrt{2gh} \implies v = \frac{M+m}{m}\sqrt{2gh}$. ∎

## Proof 9: Bouncing Ball — Total Time and Distance

**Statement:** For ball dropped from height $H$, restitution $e<1$:
Total time: $T = \frac{2u}{g}\frac{1}{1-e}$ where $u = \sqrt{2gH}$.
Total distance: $S = H\frac{1+e^2}{1-e^2}$.

**Proof:**
First fall time: $t_1 = \sqrt{2H/g} = u/g$.
1st bounce: $v_1 = eu$. Time to max height: $eu/g$. Round trip: $2eu/g$.
2nd bounce: $v_2 = e^2u$. Round trip: $2e^2u/g$.
Total time:
$$
T = \frac{u}{g} + \frac{2eu}{g} + \frac{2e^2u}{g} + \dots
= \frac{u}{g}\left(1 + 2\sum_{n=1}^\infty e^n\right)
= \frac{u}{g}\left(1 + \frac{2e}{1-e}\right)
= \frac{u}{g}\frac{1+e}{1-e}
= \frac{2u}{g}\frac{1}{1-e}
$$

Distances:
First fall: $H$.
1st bounce height: $h_1 = \frac{(eu)^2}{2g} = e^2H$.
Each round trip: $2e^2H, 2e^4H, \dots$

Total distance:
$$
S = H + 2e^2H + 2e^4H + \dots
= H\left(1 + 2\sum_{n=1}^\infty e^{2n}\right)
= H\left(1 + \frac{2e^2}{1-e^2}\right)
= H\frac{1+e^2}{1-e^2}
$$
∎

## Proof 10: Successive Impacts (Three Balls in Line)

**Statement:** For $m_1$ hitting stationary $m_2$, then $m_2$ hitting stationary $m_3$ ($e=1$):
$m_1$ stops, $m_3$ moves with original speed of $m_1$.

**Proof:**
First collision ($m_1$ hits $m_2$): $v_1 = 0$, $v_2 = u_1$ (equal masses, $e=1$).
Second collision ($m_2$ hits $m_3$): $v_2' = 0$, $v_3 = u_1$.
Result: $m_1$ and $m_2$ at rest, $m_3$ moves with $u_1$. ∎

## Proof 11: Impulse on Wall from Ball Collision

**Statement:** $I = m(1+e)u\cos\theta$ for ball mass $m$, speed $u$, angle $\theta$ to normal.

**Proof:**
Normal component: $u_n = u\cos\theta$.
Wall fixed $\implies$ $v_n = -eu_n$.
Momentum change normal: $\Delta p_n = m(v_n - u_n) = -m(1+e)u_n$.
Impulse on ball: $I_{\text{ball}} = -m(1+e)u\cos\theta$.
Impulse on wall: equal and opposite: $I_{\text{wall}} = m(1+e)u\cos\theta$. ∎

## Proof 12: Newton's Cradle (Equal Masses, $e=1$)

**Statement:** Number of balls ejected = number of balls dropped.

**Proof:**
Initial momentum: $nu$, initial KE: $\frac{1}{2}nu^2$.
Final: $k$ balls ejected at speed $v$.
Momentum: $kv = nu \implies v = \frac{n}{k}u$.
KE: $\frac{1}{2}kv^2 = \frac{1}{2}nu^2 \implies k(\frac{n}{k}u)^2 = nu^2 \implies \frac{n^2}{k} = n \implies k = n$.
Thus $k=n$, $v=u$. ∎

## Proof 13: Collision with Moving Wall

**Statement:** If wall moves with speed $V$ (toward ball), $v_n = (1+e)V - eu_n$.

**Proof:**
Relative velocity of approach: $u_{\text{rel}} = u_n - (-V) = u_n + V$.
Relative velocity of separation: $v_{\text{rel}} = v_n - V$.
Restitution: $v_{\text{rel}} = e u_{\text{rel}} \implies v_n - V = e(u_n + V)$.
$v_n = V + e(u_n + V) = (1+e)V + eu_n$.
If wall moves away from ball ($V$ positive opposite direction), sign changes. ∎

---

## Proof 14: Oblique Collision — Final Speeds

**Statement:** $v_1^2 = v_{1n}^2 + u_{1t}^2$, $v_2^2 = v_{2n}^2 + u_{2t}^2$.

**Proof:**
Tangential components unchanged (smooth): $v_{1t} = u_{1t}$, $v_{2t} = u_{2t}$.
Normal components from Proof 5.
Speed squared = normal² + tangential². ∎

---

## Proof 15: Minimum $e$ for Second Collision

**Statement:** For $m_1$ hitting $m_2$ (both mass $m$), $m_2$ hits wall ($e_w$), returns to hit $m_1$ again.
Condition: $v_2 > v_1$ after wall collision.

**Proof:**
After first collision ($e=1$): $v_1 = 0$, $v_2 = u$.
Wall collision: $v_2' = -e_w u$.
Second collision requires $v_2' > v_1 \implies -e_w u > 0$ which is false.
For general $e$: $v_1 = \frac{1-e}{2}u$, $v_2 = \frac{1+e}{2}u$.
Wall: $v_2' = -e_w\frac{1+e}{2}u$.
Second collision: $v_2' > v_1 \implies e_w\frac{1+e}{2} > \frac{1-e}{2} \implies e_w > \frac{1-e}{1+e}$. ∎

## Proof 16: Energy Loss in Oblique Collision

**Statement:** $\Delta K = -\frac{1}{2}\frac{m_1m_2}{m_1+m_2}(1-e^2)(u_{1n}-u_{2n})^2$.

**Proof:**
Tangential KE unchanged. Only normal components change.
Same formula as direct collision but with normal relative velocity $u_n = u_{1n} - u_{2n}$.
$\Delta K = \frac{1}{2}\mu(e^2-1)u_n^2 = -\frac{1}{2}\frac{m_1m_2}{m_1+m_2}(1-e^2)(u_{1n}-u_{2n})^2$. ∎

---

## Proof 17: Coefficient of Restitution from Heights

**Statement:** For ball dropped from $h_1$, rebounds to $h_2$: $e = \sqrt{h_2/h_1}$.

**Proof:**
Impact speed: $u = \sqrt{2gh_1}$.
Rebound speed: $v = \sqrt{2gh_2}$.
$e = v/u = \sqrt{2gh_2}/\sqrt{2gh_1} = \sqrt{h_2/h_1}$. ∎

---

## Proof 18: Oblique Impact with Wall — Angle Change

**Statement:** If $\theta_i$ is incident angle to normal, $\tan\theta_r = \frac{\tan\theta_i}{e}$.

**Proof:**
From Proof 7: $\tan\theta' = \frac{v_t}{|v_n|} = \frac{u_t}{eu_n} = \frac{1}{e}\tan\theta_i$. ∎

---

## Proof 19: Multiple Bounces — Velocity After $n$ Bounces

**Statement:** $v_n = e^n u$.

**Proof:**
Induction.
Base: $v_1 = e u$.
Assume $v_k = e^k u$.
Then $v_{k+1} = e v_k = e^{k+1} u$. ∎

## Proof 20: Explosion (Superelastic)

**Statement:** For body mass $M$ exploding into $m_1, m_2$ with energy $Q$:
$$
\frac{1}{2}m_1v_1^2 + \frac{1}{2}m_2v_2^2 = Q
$$

**Proof:**
Momentum conservation: $m_1v_1 + m_2v_2 = 0 \implies v_2 = -\frac{m_1}{m_2}v_1$.
Final KE: $\frac{1}{2}m_1v_1^2 + \frac{1}{2}m_2v_2^2 = \frac{1}{2}\frac{m_1m_2}{m_1+m_2}(v_1-v_2)^2$.
Energy conservation: Initial KE + $Q$ = Final KE. Initial KE = 0.
$Q = \frac{1}{2}\frac{m_1m_2}{m_1+m_2}(v_1-v_2)^2$. ∎
