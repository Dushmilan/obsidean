# Proofs: Motion on Spherical Surfaces

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 4.5

---

## Proof 1: Particle on Outside of Smooth Sphere — Normal Reaction

**Statement:** For particle of mass $m$ on outside of smooth sphere radius $R$, released from rest at top, normal reaction at angle $\theta$ from vertical: $R_N = mg(3\cos\theta - 2)$.

**Proof:**
Radial equation (towards centre):
$$
mg\cos\theta - R_N = \frac{mv^2}{R} \tag{1}
$$

Energy conservation from top ($\theta = 0$, $v = 0$):
Height lost: $R(1 - \cos\theta)$.
$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^2 = mgR(1 - \cos\theta) \implies v^2 = 2gR(1 - \cos\theta) \tag{2}
$$

Substitute (2) into (1):
$$
mg\cos\theta - R_N = \frac{m}{R} \cdot 2gR(1 - \cos\theta) = 2mg(1 - \cos\theta)
$$
$$
R_N = mg\cos\theta - 2mg(1 - \cos\theta) = mg\cos\theta - 2mg + 2mg\cos\theta
$$
$$
\boxed{R_N = mg(3\cos\theta - 2)}
$$
∎

---

## Proof 2: Particle on Outside of Smooth Sphere — Loss of Contact

**Statement:** Particle leaves sphere when $\cos\theta = 2/3$, i.e., $\theta = \cos^{-1}(2/3) \approx 48.2^\circ$.

**Proof:**
Contact lost when $R_N = 0$:
$$
mg(3\cos\theta - 2) = 0 \implies 3\cos\theta = 2 \implies \boxed{\cos\theta = \frac{2}{3}}
$$

Angle is independent of $m$, $R$, $g$ (as long as $g$ uniform).
∎

---

## Proof 3: Particle on Outside of Smooth Sphere — Speed at Loss of Contact

**Statement:** Speed when contact is lost: $v = \sqrt{2gR/3}$.

**Proof:**
From energy (Proof 1): $v^2 = 2gR(1 - \cos\theta)$.
At loss of contact: $\cos\theta = 2/3$.
$$
v^2 = 2gR(1 - 2/3) = 2gR/3 \implies \boxed{v = \sqrt{\frac{2gR}{3}}}
$$
∎

---

## Proof 4: Particle on Outside of Smooth Sphere — Height at Loss of Contact

**Statement:** Height above centre when contact lost: $h = 2R/3$.

**Proof:**
Height above centre: $h = R\cos\theta$.
At $\cos\theta = 2/3$:
$$
\boxed{h = \frac{2R}{3}}
$$
∎

## Proof 5: Particle on Outside of Smooth Sphere — Given Initial Speed $u$ at Top

**Statement:** If particle has initial speed $u$ at top, contact lost when $\cos\theta = \frac{2}{3} + \frac{u^2}{3gR}$.

**Proof:**
Energy from top with initial speed $u$:
$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2}mu^2 + mgR(1 - \cos\theta) \implies v^2 = u^2 + 2gR(1 - \cos\theta)
$$

Radial equation: $mg\cos\theta - R_N = mv^2/R$.
Substitute $v^2$:
$$
R_N = mg\cos\theta - \frac{m}{R}\left[u^2 + 2gR(1 - \cos\theta)\right]
$$
$$
R_N = mg\cos\theta - \frac{mu^2}{R} - 2mg(1 - \cos\theta)
$$
$$
R_N = mg(3\cos\theta - 2) - \frac{mu^2}{R}
$$

Set $R_N = 0$:
$$
mg(3\cos\theta - 2) = \frac{mu^2}{R} \implies 3\cos\theta - 2 = \frac{u^2}{gR}
$$
$$
\boxed{\cos\theta = \frac{2}{3} + \frac{u^2}{3gR}}
$$

Check: $u = 0 \implies \cos\theta = 2/3$ (Proof 2). ✓
If $u^2 \geq gR \implies \cos\theta \geq 1 \implies$ leaves immediately at top.
∎

## Proof 6: Particle on Inside of Smooth Sphere — Normal Reaction

**Statement:** For particle in smooth sphere/bowl radius $R$, speed $u$ at bottom, normal reaction at angle $\theta$ from bottom:
$$
R_N = \frac{mu^2}{R} - 2mg + 3mg\cos\theta
$$

**Proof:**
Radial equation (inward, towards centre):
$$
R_N - mg\cos\theta = \frac{mv^2}{R} \tag{1}
$$

Energy from bottom ($\theta = 0$, $v = u$):
Height gained: $R(1 - \cos\theta)$.
$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2}mu^2 - mgR(1 - \cos\theta) \implies v^2 = u^2 - 2gR(1 - \cos\theta) \tag{2}
$$

Substitute (2) into (1):
$$
R_N = mg\cos\theta + \frac{m}{R}\left[u^2 - 2gR(1 - \cos\theta)\right]
$$
$$
R_N = mg\cos\theta + \frac{mu^2}{R} - 2mg(1 - \cos\theta)
$$
$$
R_N = \frac{mu^2}{R} + mg\cos\theta - 2mg + 2mg\cos\theta
$$
$$
\boxed{R_N = \frac{mu^2}{R} - 2mg + 3mg\cos\theta}
$$
∎

## Proof 7: Particle on Inside of Smooth Sphere — Complete Circles Condition

**Statement:** Particle completes full vertical circles inside sphere $\iff u \geq \sqrt{5gR}$.

**Proof:**
Contact maintained $\iff R_N \geq 0$ for all $\theta \in [0, \pi]$.
From Proof 6: $R_N = \frac{mu^2}{R} - 2mg + 3mg\cos\theta$.
Minimum at $\theta = \pi$ (top): $\cos\theta = -1$.
$$
R_{N,min} = \frac{mu^2}{R} - 2mg - 3mg = \frac{mu^2}{R} - 5mg
$$
Require $R_{N,min} \geq 0$:
$$
\frac{mu^2}{R} \geq 5mg \implies u^2 \geq 5gR \implies \boxed{u \geq \sqrt{5gR}}
$$

Same condition as string vertical circle (Proof 6 in 4.3-Proofs).
∎

## Proof 8: Particle on Inside of Smooth Sphere — Oscillation Limits

**Statement:** If $u < \sqrt{5gR}$, particle oscillates between $\pm\theta_{max}$ where $\cos\theta_{max} = 1 - \frac{u^2}{2gR}$.

**Proof:**
Oscillation limits when $v = 0$.
From energy: $v^2 = u^2 - 2gR(1 - \cos\theta)$.
Set $v = 0$:
$$
0 = u^2 - 2gR(1 - \cos\theta_{max}) \implies 2gR(1 - \cos\theta_{max}) = u^2
$$
$$
1 - \cos\theta_{max} = \frac{u^2}{2gR} \implies \boxed{\cos\theta_{max} = 1 - \frac{u^2}{2gR}}
$$

Note: $u^2 < 5gR \implies \cos\theta_{max} > 1 - 5/2 = -3/2$, always $\geq -1$.
∎

## Proof 9: Small Oscillations in Spherical Bowl — Period

**Statement:** Period of small oscillations about bottom: $T = 2\pi\sqrt{R/g}$.

**Proof:**
For small $\theta$: $\cos\theta \approx 1 - \theta^2/2$, $\sin\theta \approx \theta$.
Height above bottom: $h = R(1 - \cos\theta) \approx R\theta^2/2$.
PE: $U = mgh \approx mgR\theta^2/2$.
KE: $K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2}m(R\dot{\theta})^2 = \frac{1}{2}mR^2\dot{\theta}^2$.
Lagrangian: $\mathcal{L} = \frac{1}{2}mR^2\dot{\theta}^2 - \frac{1}{2}mgR\theta^2$.
Euler-Lagrange: $\frac{d}{dt}\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\dot{\theta}} = \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\theta}$
$$
mR^2\ddot{\theta} = -mgR\theta \implies \ddot{\theta} + \frac{g}{R}\theta = 0
$$
SHM with $\omega = \sqrt{g/R}$, period:
$$
\boxed{T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{R}{g}}}
$$

Same as simple pendulum of length $R$.
∎

## Proof 10: Conical Motion on Smooth Sphere

**Statement:** Particle in horizontal circle on smooth sphere at angle $\alpha$ from vertical:
$\omega = \sqrt{g/(R\cos\alpha)}$, $R_N = mg/\cos\alpha$.

**Proof:**
Forces: $mg$ down, $R_N$ normal at angle $\alpha$ to vertical.
Vertical equilibrium:
$$
R_N\cos\alpha = mg \implies R_N = \frac{mg}{\cos\alpha} \tag{1}
$$

Horizontal (centripetal) force:
$$
R_N\sin\alpha = m\omega^2 r = m\omega^2 R\sin\alpha \tag{2}
$$

Cancel $R\sin\alpha$ (assuming $\alpha \neq 0$):
$$
R_N = m\omega^2 R \tag{3}
$$

Substitute (1) into (3):
$$
\frac{mg}{\cos\alpha} = m\omega^2 R \implies \omega^2 = \frac{g}{R\cos\alpha}
$$
$$
\boxed{\omega = \sqrt{\frac{g}{R\cos\alpha}}}
$$

Height of circle: $h = R\cos\alpha = g/\omega^2$.
Radius of circle: $r = R\sin\alpha$.
Identical to conical pendulum with $L \to R$.
∎

## Proof 12: Comparison — String vs Rod vs Sphere (Inside) Vertical Circles

**Statement:** Minimum speed at bottom for complete vertical circles:

| System | $u_{min}$ | Reason |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| String | $\sqrt{5gR}$ | $T \geq 0$ at top $\implies v_{top}^2 \geq gR$ |
| Rod | $2\sqrt{gR}$ | $v_{top}^2 \geq 0$ (rod can compress) |
| Inside sphere | $\sqrt{5gR}$ | $R_N \geq 0$ at top $\implies v_{top}^2 \geq gR$ |

**Proof:**
All three use energy: $v_{top}^2 = u^2 - 4gR$.

String: $T = mv_{top}^2/R - mg \geq 0 \implies v_{top}^2 \geq gR \implies u^2 \geq 5gR$.

Rod: Force $F = mv_{top}^2/R - mg$. Rod can push ($F < 0$ allowed). Only need $v_{top}^2 \geq 0 \implies u^2 \geq 4gR$.

Inside sphere: $R_N = mv_{top}^2/R - mg \geq 0 \implies v_{top}^2 \geq gR \implies u^2 \geq 5gR$.
∎

## Proof 13: General Condition for Contact on Inside Sphere with Initial Speed $u$ at Top

**Statement:** If particle inside sphere has initial speed $u$ at top ($\theta = 0$), contact lost when $\cos\theta = \frac{2}{3} - \frac{u^2}{3gR}$.

**Proof:**
Energy from top: $v^2 = u^2 + 2gR(1 - \cos\theta)$.
Radial: $R_N - mg\cos\theta = mv^2/R$.
$R_N = mg\cos\theta + \frac{m}{R}[u^2 + 2gR(1 - \cos\theta)] = mg(3\cos\theta - 2) + \frac{mu^2}{R}$.
Set $R_N = 0$:
$mg(3\cos\theta - 2) = -\frac{mu^2}{R} \implies 3\cos\theta - 2 = -\frac{u^2}{gR}$.
$\cos\theta = \frac{2}{3} - \frac{u^2}{3gR}$.
Note: If $u^2 > 2gR$, $\cos\theta < 0$ — contact lost after horizontal.
∎
