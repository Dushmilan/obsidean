# Proofs: Motion on Horizontal and Vertical Circles

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 4.3

---

## Proof 1: Horizontal Circle — String (Tension Provides Centripetal Force)

**Statement:** For mass $m$ on smooth horizontal table, string through hole: $T = m\omega^2 r = mv^2/r$.

**Proof:**
Only horizontal force on mass is tension $T$ towards centre.
Newton's 2nd Law radially: $T = ma_c$.
Centripetal acceleration: $a_c = v^2/r = \omega^2 r$.
Thus: $T = m\omega^2 r = mv^2/r$.
∎

---

## Proof 2: Horizontal Circle — Rod (Compression or Tension)

**Statement:** For mass on light rod of length $L$ rotating horizontally: $F = m\omega^2 r$ (can be positive or negative).

**Proof:**
Rod constrains mass to circle of radius $r = L$.
Radial force from rod: $F$ (positive = tension/pulling, negative = compression/pushing).
Newton's 2nd Law: $F = ma_c = m\omega^2 r$.
Rod can exert either sign; string can only pull ($T \geq 0$).
∎

---

## Proof 3: Vertical Circle — Energy Conservation

**Statement:** For mass $m$ on string/rod of length $r$ in vertical plane, speed at angle $\theta$ from bottom:
$$
v^2 = u^2 - 2gr(1 - \cos\theta)
$$
where $u$ = speed at bottom ($\theta = 0$).

**Proof:**
Take zero PE at centre (or bottom). At angle $\theta$ from bottom:
Height above bottom: $h = r(1 - \cos\theta)$.
PE gained: $mgh = mgr(1 - \cos\theta)$.
By conservation of energy:
$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^2 + mgr(1 - \cos\theta) = \frac{1}{2}mu^2
$$
$$
v^2 = u^2 - 2gr(1 - \cos\theta)
$$
∎

---

## Proof 4: Vertical Circle — Tension in String at Angle $\theta$

**Statement:** $T = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 2mg + 3mg\cos\theta$.

**Proof:**
Radial forces at angle $\theta$ (towards centre):
Tension $T$ inward, weight component $mg\cos\theta$ inward (since $\theta$ from bottom).
$$
T + mg\cos\theta = \frac{mv^2}{r}
$$
Substitute $v^2$ from Proof 3:
$$
T = \frac{m}{r}\left[u^2 - 2gr(1 - \cos\theta)\right] - mg\cos\theta
$$
$$
T = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 2mg(1 - \cos\theta) - mg\cos\theta
$$
$$
T = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 2mg + 2mg\cos\theta - mg\cos\theta
$$
$$
\boxed{T = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 2mg + 3mg\cos\theta}
$$
∎

---


**Statement:**
- $T_{bottom} = \frac{mu^2}{r} + mg$
- $T_{horizontal} = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 2mg$
- $T_{top} = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 5mg$

**Proof:**
From Proof 4: $T = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 2mg + 3mg\cos\theta$.

Bottom ($\theta = 0$, $\cos\theta = 1$): $T_{bottom} = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 2mg + 3mg = \frac{mu^2}{r} + mg$.

Horizontal ($\theta = \pi/2$, $\cos\theta = 0$): $T_{horiz} = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 2mg$.

Top ($\theta = \pi$, $\cos\theta = -1$): $T_{top} = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 2mg - 3mg = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 5mg$.
∎

## Proof 6: Condition for Complete Circles — String

**Statement:** String remains taut throughout $\iff u \geq \sqrt{5gr}$.

**Proof:**
String taut $\iff T \geq 0$ at all points.
Minimum tension at top ($\theta = \pi$): $T_{top} = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 5mg \geq 0$.
$$
\frac{mu^2}{r} \geq 5mg \implies u^2 \geq 5gr \implies \boxed{u \geq \sqrt{5gr}}
$$

Alternatively, at top: $v_{top}^2 = u^2 - 4gr$.
Require $v_{top}^2 \geq gr$ (for $T_{top} \geq 0$):
$$
u^2 - 4gr \geq gr \implies u^2 \geq 5gr \implies u \geq \sqrt{5gr}
$$
∎

## Proof 7: Condition for Complete Circles — Rod

**Statement:** Rod remains in contact (doesn't go slack) $\iff u \geq 2\sqrt{gr}$.

**Proof:**
For rod, force $F$ can be negative (compression).
Require $F \geq 0$ for complete circles? Actually rod can support compression, so condition is just that particle reaches top.

At top: $v_{top}^2 = u^2 - 4gr$.
For particle to reach top: $v_{top}^2 \geq 0 \implies u^2 \geq 4gr \implies u \geq 2\sqrt{gr}$.

If $u = 2\sqrt{gr}$: $v_{top} = 0$, $F_{top} = -mg$ (compression).
If $u > 2\sqrt{gr}$: $v_{top} > 0$, $F_{top} > -mg$.
If $u < 2\sqrt{gr}$: particle doesn't reach top, oscillates.

**Wait:** "Complete circles" usually means passes through top and continues. So condition is $u \geq 2\sqrt{gr}$.
∎

---


**Statement:**
- $u < \sqrt{2gr}$: oscillates (doesn't reach horizontal)
- $\sqrt{2gr} \leq u < \sqrt{3gr}$: reaches horizontal, goes slack before top
- $\sqrt{3gr} \leq u < \sqrt{5gr}$: passes horizontal, goes slack between horizontal and top
- $u \geq \sqrt{5gr}$: complete circles

**Proof:**
String goes slack when $T = 0$.
From Proof 4: $T = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 2mg + 3mg\cos\theta = 0$.
$$
3mg\cos\theta = 2mg - \frac{mu^2}{r} \implies \cos\theta = \frac{2}{3} - \frac{u^2}{3gr}
$$

String slack angle $\theta_{slack}$ given by this equation. Requires $-1 \leq \cos\theta_{slack} \leq 1$.

- If $u^2 < 2gr$: $\cos\theta_{slack} > 0$ but motion never reaches $\theta$ where $T=0$ because $v=0$ first.
  Max $\theta$ when $v=0$: $u^2 = 2gr(1-\cos\theta_{max}) \implies \cos\theta_{max} = 1 - u^2/2gr > 0$.
  Since $\theta_{max} < \pi/2$, doesn't reach horizontal. Oscillates.

- If $2gr \leq u^2 < 3gr$: $v=0$ at $\cos\theta_{max} = 1 - u^2/2gr \leq 0$, so reaches horizontal.
  Slack when $\cos\theta_{slack} = \frac{2}{3} - \frac{u^2}{3gr} \geq \frac{2}{3} - \frac{3gr}{3gr} = -\frac{1}{3}$.
  But $\theta_{slack} > \pi/2$ (since $\cos\theta_{slack} < 0$).
  Goes slack after horizontal, before top.

- If $3gr \leq u^2 < 5gr$: $\cos\theta_{slack} \leq -\frac{1}{3}$. Slack between horizontal and top.
  Check: $u^2 = 3gr \implies \cos\theta_{slack} = 2/3 - 1 = -1/3$.
  $u^2 = 5gr \implies \cos\theta_{slack} = 2/3 - 5/3 = -1 = \cos\pi$ (top).

- If $u^2 \geq 5gr$: $T \geq 0$ everywhere. Complete circles.
∎

## Proof 9: Particle on Outside of Smooth Sphere — Leaves at $\cos\theta = 2/3$

**Statement:** Particle released from rest at top of smooth sphere of radius $R$ loses contact when $\cos\theta = 2/3$.

**Proof:**
Radial equation (towards centre): $mg\cos\theta - R_N = mv^2/R$.
Energy from top: $\frac{1}{2}mv^2 = mgR(1-\cos\theta) \implies v^2 = 2gR(1-\cos\theta)$.

Substitute:
$$
R_N = mg\cos\theta - \frac{m}{R} \cdot 2gR(1-\cos\theta) = mg\cos\theta - 2mg(1-\cos\theta)
$$
$$
R_N = mg(3\cos\theta - 2)
$$

Contact lost when $R_N = 0$:
$$
3\cos\theta - 2 = 0 \implies \boxed{\cos\theta = \frac{2}{3}}
$$
∎

## Proof 10: Particle on Inside of Smooth Sphere — Complete Circles Condition

**Statement:** For particle in smooth sphere/bowl, complete circles $\iff u \geq \sqrt{5gR}$.

**Proof:**
Radial equation (inward): $R_N - mg\cos\theta = mv^2/R$.
Energy from bottom: $v^2 = u^2 - 2gR(1-\cos\theta)$.

Normal reaction:
$$
R_N = mg\cos\theta + \frac{m}{R}[u^2 - 2gR(1-\cos\theta)]
$$
$$
R_N = \frac{mu^2}{R} + mg\cos\theta - 2mg(1-\cos\theta)
$$
$$
R_N = \frac{mu^2}{R} - 2mg + 3mg\cos\theta
$$

Minimum at top ($\theta = \pi$, $\cos\theta = -1$):
$$
R_{N,min} = \frac{mu^2}{R} - 2mg - 3mg = \frac{mu^2}{R} - 5mg
$$

Contact maintained $\iff R_N \geq 0 \implies \frac{mu^2}{R} \geq 5mg \implies \boxed{u \geq \sqrt{5gR}}$
∎

## Proof 11: Conical Motion on Sphere

**Statement:** Particle in horizontal circle on smooth sphere at angle $\alpha$ from vertical: $\omega = \sqrt{g/(R\cos\alpha)}$.

**Proof:**
Forces: $mg$ down, $R_N$ normal at angle $\alpha$ to vertical.
Vertical: $R_N\cos\alpha = mg \implies R_N = mg/\cos\alpha$.
Horizontal: $R_N\sin\alpha = m\omega^2 r = m\omega^2 R\sin\alpha$.
Cancel $R_N\sin\alpha$: $\omega^2 = R_N/(mR) = \frac{mg}{mR\cos\alpha} = \frac{g}{R\cos\alpha}$.
$$
\boxed{\omega = \sqrt{\frac{g}{R\cos\alpha}}}
$$
Identical to conical pendulum with $L \to R$.
∎

---

## Proof 12: Small Oscillations in Spherical Bowl

**Statement:** Period of small oscillations about bottom: $T = 2\pi\sqrt{R/g}$.

**Proof:**
For small $\theta$, $\sin\theta \approx \theta$, $\cos\theta \approx 1 - \theta^2/2$.
Height above bottom: $h = R(1-\cos\theta) \approx R\theta^2/2$.
PE: $U = mgh \approx mgR\theta^2/2$.
KE: $K = \frac{1}{2}m(R\dot{\theta})^2 = \frac{1}{2}mR^2\dot{\theta}^2$.
Lagrangian: $\mathcal{L} = \frac{1}{2}mR^2\dot{\theta}^2 - \frac{1}{2}mgR\theta^2$.
Equation: $mR^2\ddot{\theta} + mgR\theta = 0 \implies \ddot{\theta} + \frac{g}{R}\theta = 0$.
SHM with $\omega = \sqrt{g/R}$, $T = 2\pi\sqrt{R/g}$.
∎
