
## Definition

A body in equilibrium (no acceleration, no rotation) satisfies **three conditions** (2D):

$$\sum F_x = 0, \qquad \sum F_y = 0, \qquad \sum M_O = 0$$

Exactly three equations → solve for up to three unknowns. The moment equation is what separates particle mechanics (force balance only) from rigid-body mechanics.

## The Intuition

Holding a rod at its centre: equal-and-opposite pushes at the ends keep it still (force balance). But push at different heights — the rod can stay put while *starting to rotate*. So you also need moment balance: left-right, up-down, and no-spin — all three simultaneously. Forces not sharing a line of action create a **couple** (pure rotation).

## The Toolkit

| Result | Formula | Use when |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Translational | $\sum\vec{F} = 0$ | resolve $x$, $y$ |
| Rotational | $\sum\vec{M}_O = 0$ | any pivot |
| Moment of a force | $M = F \cdot d_{\perp}$ | perpendicular distance |
| Principle of moments | ΣCW = ΣACW | pivot choice |
| Lami's Theorem | $\dfrac{F_1}{\sin\alpha_1} = \dfrac{F_2}{\sin\alpha_2} = \dfrac{F_3}{\sin\alpha_3}$ | exactly three forces at a point |

## Derivation

Equilibrium is Newton's first law ($\sum F = 0$) plus the rotational analogue ($\sum M = 0$). Lami's theorem comes from the sine rule applied to the closed triangle of forces — the three forces form a triangle head-to-tail. [Full derivations: 03.1-Proofs-Forces-Equilibrium]

## Method

1. **Draw the FBD** with all forces at their actual points of application.
2. **Resolve** into components ($x$ and $y$).
3. **Take moments** about a convenient pivot — choose the point where unknown forces pass through to eliminate them.
4. Solve the three equations.

## Worked Examples

**Setup:** A 10 N weight hangs from two strings making angles $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$ with the horizontal. Find the tensions.

**Solution:** Resolve: $T_1\cos30 = T_2\cos60$ and $T_1\sin30 + T_2\sin60 = 10$. Solving: $T_1 = 5$ N, $T_2 = 5\sqrt3$ N.

**Key insight:** Two equilibrium equations, two unknowns — resolve at the junction point.

---

**Setup:** A uniform rod of weight $W$ rests with one end on the ground and the other against a smooth wall. Find reactions.

**Solution:** $\sum F_x = 0$: $R_x = N$ (wall). $\sum F_y = 0$: $R_y = W$. Moments about the base eliminate $R_x, R_y$ and give $N$ directly.

**Key insight:** Pivot at the ground contact — the two unknown ground reactions vanish from the moment equation.

## Common Traps

- Treating a force's moment with the wrong perpendicular distance
- Counting couples incorrectly (no net force, but nonzero moment)
- Using Lami's theorem when there are more than three forces
- Picking a pivot through an unknown force — you lose the equation you need
- Forgetting that forces at the same point but different lines of action produce rotation
