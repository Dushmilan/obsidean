# 3.6 Rigid Bodies in Equilibrium

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 3.6

---

## General Equilibrium Conditions (3D)

For a rigid body in 3D:
$$
\sum \vec{F} = 0 \quad \text{(3 scalar equations)}
$$
$$
\sum \vec{M}_O = 0 \quad \text{(3 scalar equations about any point O)}
$$

**Total: 6 independent equations**
- $\sum F_x = 0$, $\sum F_y = 0$, $\sum F_z = 0$
- $\sum M_x = 0$, $\sum M_y = 0$, $\sum M_z = 0$

Can take moments about any point, but 3 moment equations must be about **independent axes**.

---

## 2D Equilibrium (Planar Problems)

Reduces to 3 equations:
$$
\sum F_x = 0, \quad \sum F_y = 0, \quad \sum M_O = 0
$$

Where $M_O$ is moment about $z$-axis (perpendicular to plane).

---

## Types of Supports and Reactions (2D)

| Support Type | Reactions | Degrees of Freedom Removed |
|--------------|-----------|---------------------------|
| **Roller** | 1 force (normal) | 1 (translation normal to surface) |
| **Pin / Hinge** | 2 forces ($R_x, R_y$) | 2 (translation) |
| **Fixed / Built-in** | 2 forces + 1 moment ($R_x, R_y, M$) | 3 (translation + rotation) |
| **Smooth Contact** | 1 force (normal) | 1 |
| **Rough Contact** | 2 forces (normal + friction) | 2 |

---

## Types of Supports and Reactions (3D)

| Support | Reactions | DOF Removed |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| Ball-and-socket | 3 forces ($R_x, R_y, R_z$) | 3 translations |
| Hinge (axis along $z$) | 3 forces + 2 moments ($M_x, M_y$) | 3 trans + 2 rot |
| Fixed support | 3 forces + 3 moments | 6 (all) |
| Roller on plane | 1 force (normal) | 1 |

## Analysis of Plane Trusses (Frames)

### Assumptions
1. Members are straight, connected by **frictionless pins**
2. Loads applied **only at joints**
3. Members are **two-force members** (axial force only)
4. Self-weight neglected or applied at joints

### Method of Joints
- Isolate each joint as free body
- Apply $\sum F_x = 0$, $\sum F_y = 0$ at each joint
- Solve sequentially: start at joint with ≤ 2 unknowns
- **Tension positive** (pulling on joint), compression negative

### Method of Sections
- Cut through truss (max 3 unknown members)
- Isolate one part as free body
- Apply $\sum F_x = 0$, $\sum F_y = 0$, $\sum M = 0$
- Solve directly for cut members

### Zero-Force Members
- Two members at joint, no external load → both zero
- Three members, two collinear, no external load → third zero

### Common Truss Types
- **Warren:** Equilateral triangles
- **Pratt:** Verticals in compression, diagonals in tension
- **Howe:** Verticals in tension, diagonals in compression
- **K-truss:** Verticals with K-bracing

## Analysis of Frames and Machines

### Difference from Trusses
- Members are **not** two-force members
- Can have multi-force members (bending, shear)
- Machines: designed to **transmit/modify forces** (mechanical advantage)

### Analysis Procedure
1. **Determine external reactions** — treat whole frame as rigid body
2. **Disassemble** — free-body diagram of each member
3. **Apply equilibrium** to each member: $\sum F_x=0, \sum F_y=0, \sum M=0$
4. **Newton's 3rd Law** — action-reaction at connections

### Mechanical Advantage
$$
\text{MA} = \frac{\text{Output force}}{\text{Input force}} = \frac{\text{Input displacement}}{\text{Output displacement}}
$$

From virtual work: $F_{\text{in}} \delta x_{\text{in}} = F_{\text{out}} \delta x_{\text{out}}$

## Distributed Loads

### Types
- **Uniform (UDL):** $w = \text{constant}$
- **Linearly varying (UVL):** $w = w_0 + kx$
- **Arbitrary:** $w(x)$

### Reduction to Resultant
- **Magnitude:** $R = \int w(x)\,dx$ = area under load diagram
- **Location:** $\bar{x} = \frac{\int x w(x)\,dx}{\int w(x)\,dx}$ = centroid of load diagram

### Common Resultants
| Load Type | Resultant | Location from start |
|-----------|-----------|---------------------|
| UDL $w$ over $L$ | $wL$ | $L/2$ |
| Triangular $0 \to w_0$ over $L$ | $\frac{1}{2}w_0 L$ | $2L/3$ from zero end |
| Trapezoidal $w_1 \to w_2$ | $\frac{w_1+w_2}{2}L$ | $L\frac{2w_1+w_2}{3(w_1+w_2)}$ from $w_1$ |

## Beams and Bending (Introduction)

### Shear Force and Bending Moment
- **Shear force $V$:** Algebraic sum of vertical forces to one side
- **Bending moment $M$:** Algebraic sum of moments to one side

### Relationships
$$
\frac{dV}{dx} = -w(x), \quad \frac{dM}{dx} = V
$$

### Sign Convention (Standard)
- **Positive shear:** Up on left face, down on right face
- **Positive moment:** Sagging (tension at bottom)

### Drawing SFD and BMD
1. Find reactions
2. Cut beam at distance $x$, apply equilibrium to left segment
3. $V(x)$ and $M(x)$ as functions
4. Plot diagrams

## Three-Hinged Arches

### Properties
- Three hinges: two at supports, one at crown
- Statically determinate (4 unknowns, 4 equations: 3 global + 1 moment at crown = 0)

### Analysis
1. Find vertical reactions (moments about support)
2. Find horizontal thrust $H$ (moment about crown = 0)
3. Internal forces: normal thrust, radial shear, bending moment

## Cables

### Flexible Cable with Concentrated Loads
- Shape = funicular polygon
- Horizontal tension $H$ constant
- Vertical tension varies: $V = \sum$ loads to one side
- Tension $T = \sqrt{H^2 + V^2}$

### Uniformly Loaded Cable (Self-weight negligible)
Parabolic shape:
$$
y = \frac{w}{2H}x^2 \quad (\text{lowest point at origin})
$$
Sag $d$ at midspan $L/2$:
$$
H = \frac{wL^2}{8d}
$$

## 3D Force Systems

### Resultant of a General Force System
Any system reduces to:
- A **force-couple system** at a point: $\vec{R} = \sum \vec{F}_i$, $\vec{M}_O = \sum (\vec{r}_i \times \vec{F}_i)$

### Wrench (Screw)
If $\vec{R} \neq 0$ and $\vec{M} \neq 0$ with $\vec{R} \cdot \vec{M} \neq 0$:
- Can reduce to a **wrench**: force $\vec{R}$ along a line + moment parallel to $\vec{R}$
- **Pitch:** $p = \frac{\vec{R} \cdot \vec{M}}{|\vec{R}|^2}$
- **Axis:** Line where moment is parallel to force

### Equilibrium in 3D
$$
\vec{R} = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad \vec{M}_O = 0
$$

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Simple Truss
Warren truss, span $L$, height $h$, load $P$ at midspan. Find force in members.

**Solution:**
- Symmetry → vertical reactions $P/2$ each
- Method of joints at support: 2 unknowns
- Or method of sections through midspan

### Example 2: Frame with Distributed Load
Frame ABC, AB horizontal, BC vertical. UDL on AB. Find reactions at A and C.

**Solution:**
- Replace UDL with resultant at centroid
- 3 equations for whole frame
- Disassemble for member forces

### Example 3: 3D Cable System
Weight $W$ suspended by 3 cables from points $A, B, C$. Find tensions.

**Solution:**
- Let tensions $T_A, T_B, T_C$
- Unit vectors along cables: $\hat{u}_A, \hat{u}_B, \hat{u}_C$
- Equilibrium: $T_A\hat{u}_A + T_B\hat{u}_B + T_C\hat{u}_C + W\hat{k} = 0$
- 3 equations, 3 unknowns
