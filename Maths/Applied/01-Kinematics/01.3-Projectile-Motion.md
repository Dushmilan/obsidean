# 1.3 Projectile Motion

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 1.3

---

## Model Assumptions

1. **No air resistance** — only force is weight $mg$ downward
2. **Flat Earth** — $g$ constant, no curvature
3. **Particle model** — size negligible
4. **Uniform gravity** — $g \approx 9.8$ m/s² (or $10$ m/s²)

---

## Coordinate System

- Origin: point of projection
- $x$-axis: horizontal (range direction)
- $y$-axis: vertical upward
- Initial velocity: $u$ at angle $\theta$ to horizontal

### Initial Components
$$
u_x = u\cos\theta, \quad u_y = u\sin\theta
$$

### Acceleration Components
$$
a_x = 0, \quad a_y = -g
$$

---

## Equations of Motion

### Horizontal (uniform velocity)
$$
v_x = u\cos\theta \quad (\text{constant})
$$
$$
x = (u\cos\theta)t
$$

### Vertical (SUVAT with $a = -g$)
$$
v_y = u\sin\theta - gt
$$
$$
y = (u\sin\theta)t - \frac{1}{2}gt^2
$$
$$
v_y^2 = u^2\sin^2\theta - 2gy
$$

### Velocity Vector
$$
\vec{v} = (u\cos\theta)\,\hat{i} + (u\sin\theta - gt)\,\hat{j}
$$
$$
v = \sqrt{u^2\cos^2\theta + (u\sin\theta - gt)^2}
$$
$$
\tan\phi = \frac{v_y}{v_x} = \frac{u\sin\theta - gt}{u\cos\theta} \quad (\phi = \text{angle to horizontal})
$$

---

## Key Results

### Time of Flight ($T$)
Time until $y = 0$ again:
$$
T = \frac{2u\sin\theta}{g}
$$

### Maximum Height ($H$)
At $v_y = 0$:
$$
H = \frac{u^2\sin^2\theta}{2g}
$$

### Range ($R$)
Horizontal distance at $t = T$:
$$
R = (u\cos\theta)T = \frac{u^2\sin2\theta}{g}
$$

### Equation of Trajectory (Cartesian)
Eliminate $t = x/(u\cos\theta)$:
$$
y = x\tan\theta - \frac{gx^2}{2u^2\cos^2\theta} = x\tan\theta - \frac{gx^2}{2u^2}\sec^2\theta
$$
This is a **parabola** with vertex at $(R/2, H)$.

---

## Complementary Angles

For same $u$, angles $\theta$ and $(90^\circ - \theta)$ give **same range**:
$$
R(\theta) = R(90^\circ - \theta) = \frac{u^2\sin2\theta}{g}
$$

**Reason:** $\sin2\theta = \sin(180^\circ - 2\theta) = \sin2(90^\circ - \theta)$

---

## Maximum Range for Given $u$

Max when $\sin2\theta = 1 \implies 2\theta = 90^\circ \implies \theta = 45^\circ$:
$$
R_{\text{max}} = \frac{u^2}{g}
$$
At $\theta = 45^\circ$: $H = \frac{u^2}{4g} = \frac{R_{\text{max}}}{4}$

---

## Speed at Any Height

From energy conservation (or $v_y^2$ equation):
$$
v^2 = u^2 - 2gy
$$
Speed depends **only on height**, not on angle or horizontal position.

---

## Angle of Projection for Given Range

Given $R$, find $\theta$:
$$
\sin2\theta = \frac{gR}{u^2} \implies 2\theta = \sin^{-1}\left(\frac{gR}{u^2}\right) \quad \text{or} \quad 180^\circ - \sin^{-1}\left(\frac{gR}{u^2}\right)
$$
Two solutions if $R < u^2/g$, one if $R = u^2/g$, none if $R > u^2/g$.

---

## Projectile on Inclined Plane

### Plane Inclined at $\alpha$ to Horizontal

**Coordinate along plane ($x'$) and perpendicular ($y'$):**
- Initial velocity components:
  $$
  u_{x'} = u\cos(\theta - \alpha), \quad u_{y'} = u\sin(\theta - \alpha)
  $$
- Acceleration components:
  $$
  a_{x'} = -g\sin\alpha, \quad a_{y'} = -g\cos\alpha
  $$

### Range Up/Down Plane
- **Up plane:** $R_{\text{up}} = \frac{2u^2\cos(\theta - \alpha)\sin(\theta - \alpha)}{g\cos\alpha} = \frac{u^2\sin2(\theta - \alpha)}{g\cos\alpha}$
- **Down plane:** $R_{\text{down}} = \frac{u^2\sin2(\theta + \alpha)}{g\cos\alpha}$

### Maximum Range Up Plane
Optimal $\theta = 45^\circ + \alpha/2$:
$$
R_{\text{max, up}} = \frac{u^2}{g(1+\sin\alpha)}
$$

### Time of Flight on Inclined Plane
$$
T = \frac{2u\sin(\theta - \alpha)}{g\cos\alpha} \quad (\text{up plane})
$$

---

## Projectile Passing Through a Point $(x_0, y_0)$

Substitute into trajectory equation:
$$
y_0 = x_0\tan\theta - \frac{gx_0^2}{2u^2}\sec^2\theta
$$
Use $\sec^2\theta = 1 + \tan^2\theta$ to get quadratic in $\tan\theta$:
$$
\frac{gx_0^2}{2u^2}\tan^2\theta - x_0\tan\theta + \left(y_0 + \frac{gx_0^2}{2u^2}\right) = 0
$$

**Discriminant condition for real $\theta$:**
$$
x_0^2 \geq 2\frac{gx_0^2}{u^2}\left(y_0 + \frac{gx_0^2}{2u^2}\right)
$$

---

## Clearing a Wall/Obstacle

**Problem:** Projectile must clear wall of height $h$ at distance $d$.

**Method 1:** Check if trajectory $y(d) > h$ for given $\theta$.

**Method 2:** Find minimum $u$ to clear wall at distance $d$:
At $x=d$, need $y \geq h$. Minimum $u$ occurs when trajectory just grazes wall:
- Equation of parabola with vertex at $(d, h)$: $y - h = -A(x-d)^2$
- Match with standard form → find $u$, $\theta$

---

## Velocity at Impact (on horizontal ground)

$$
v = \sqrt{u^2\cos^2\theta + u^2\sin^2\theta} = u
$$
**Speed on landing = speed of projection** (same height, no air resistance)

Angle of impact: $\phi = -\theta$ (symmetric trajectory)

---

## Velocity at Any Time

### Components
$$
v_x = u\cos\theta, \quad v_y = u\sin\theta - gt
$$

### Magnitude and Direction
$$
v = \sqrt{u^2 - 2ugt\sin\theta + g^2t^2}
$$
$$
\tan\phi = \frac{u\sin\theta - gt}{u\cos\theta}
$$

---

## Time to Reach Given Height

Solve $y = (u\sin\theta)t - \frac{1}{2}gt^2$ for $t$:
$$
t = \frac{u\sin\theta \pm \sqrt{u^2\sin^2\theta - 2gy}}{g}
$$
- Two times for $y < H$ (ascending/descending)
- One time for $y = H$ (top)
- No real time for $y > H$

---

## Two Particles Projected

### From Same Point, Different Angles
- Equate $y$ coordinates to find collision time
- Check if $x$ coordinates match at that time

### From Different Points
- Write $x_A(t), y_A(t)$ and $x_B(t), y_B(t)$
- Solve $x_A = x_B$, $y_A = y_B$ simultaneously

### Relative Motion Approach
$$
\vec{r}_{A/B} = \vec{r}_A - \vec{r}_B, \quad \vec{v}_{A/B} = \vec{v}_A - \vec{v}_B
$$
Collision when $\vec{r}_{A/B} = 0$.

---

## Energy Approach (Alternative)

**Kinetic Energy:** $K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2}m(u^2 - 2gy)$

**Potential Energy:** $U = mgy$

**Total Energy:** $E = \frac{1}{2}mu^2$ (constant)

Useful for:
- Speed at height $y$: $v = \sqrt{u^2 - 2gy}$
- Max height: $mgH = \frac{1}{2}mu_y^2 \implies H = \frac{u^2\sin^2\theta}{2g}$

---

## Summary of Formulae

| Quantity | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| Time of flight | $T = \frac{2u\sin\theta}{g}$ |
| Max height | $H = \frac{u^2\sin^2\theta}{2g}$ |
| Range | $R = \frac{u^2\sin2\theta}{g}$ |
| Max range (level ground) | $R_{\text{max}} = \frac{u^2}{g}$ at $\theta = 45^\circ$ |
| Trajectory | $y = x\tan\theta - \frac{gx^2}{2u^2}\sec^2\theta$ |
| Speed at height $y$ | $v = \sqrt{u^2 - 2gy}$ |
