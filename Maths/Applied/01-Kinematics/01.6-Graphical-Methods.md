# 1.6 Graphical Methods (Motion Graphs)

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 1.6

---

## Three Fundamental Motion Graphs

| Graph | Axes | Gradient | Area Under Curve |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| **Displacement–Time** | $x$–$t$ | Velocity $v = dx/dt$ | — |
| **Velocity–Time** | $v$–$t$ | Acceleration $a = dv/dt$ | **Displacement** $\int v\,dt$ |
| **Acceleration–Time** | $a$–$t$ | Jerk $j = da/dt$ | **Change in velocity** $\int a\,dt$ |

---

## Displacement–Time ($x$–$t$) Graph

### Gradient = Velocity
$$
v = \frac{dx}{dt} = \text{gradient of } x\text{–}t \text{ graph}
$$

### Features
| Feature | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Straight line | Constant velocity |
| Horizontal line | At rest ($v=0$) |
| Steepness | Speed magnitude |
| Curvature | Acceleration (concave up = +ve $a$) |
| Tangent at point | Instantaneous velocity |

### Converting $x$–$t$ → $v$–$t$
- Draw tangents at several points
- Measure gradient → $v$
- Plot $v$ vs $t$

---

## Velocity–Time ($v$–$t$) Graph

### Gradient = Acceleration
$$
a = \frac{dv}{dt} = \text{gradient of } v\text{–}t \text{ graph}
$$

### Area = Displacement
$$
\Delta x = \int v\,dt = \text{area under } v\text{–}t \text{ graph}
$$

### Area Sign Convention
- **Area above $t$-axis** → +ve displacement
- **Area below $t$-axis** → -ve displacement
- **Total area** → distance travelled
- **Signed area** → net displacement

### Features
| Feature | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Horizontal line | Constant velocity ($a=0$) |
| Straight sloping line | Constant acceleration |
| Curve | Variable acceleration |
| Crossing $t$-axis | Change of direction |

---

## Acceleration–Time ($a$–$t$) Graph

### Gradient = Jerk
$$
j = \frac{da}{dt}
$$

### Area = Change in Velocity
$$
\Delta v = \int a\,dt = \text{area under } a\text{–}t \text{ graph}
$$

### Converting $a$–$t$ → $v$–$t$
- Integrate graphically: area from $t=0$ to $t$ gives $v(t)$
- $v(t) = u + \text{area under } a\text{–}t \text{ from } 0 \text{ to } t$

---

## Interconversion Summary

```
x–t graph  ────d/dt────►  v–t graph  ────d/dt────►  a–t graph
   ▲                              │                         │
   │                              │                         │
   │──────────∫ dt────────────────┘                         │
   │                                                      │
   └──────────────────────∫∫ dt²──────────────────────────┘
```

| From → To | Operation |
|-----------|-----------|
| $x$–$t$ → $v$–$t$ | Gradient (differentiate) |
| $v$–$t$ → $a$–$t$ | Gradient (differentiate) |
| $v$–$t$ → $x$–$t$ | Area (integrate) |
| $a$–$t$ → $v$–$t$ | Area (integrate) |
| $a$–$t$ → $x$–$t$ | Double area (double integrate) |

---

## Constant Acceleration Graphs (SUVAT)

### $v$–$t$ Graph
- Straight line: $v = u + at$
- Gradient = $a$
- Area = trapezium = $\frac{1}{2}(u+v)t = s$

### $x$–$t$ Graph
- Parabola: $x = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$
- Tangent gradient = $v$

### $a$–$t$ Graph
- Horizontal line at $a$
- Area = $at$ = change in $v$

---

## Variable Acceleration Graphs

### Example: $a = -kt$ (linearly decreasing)
- $a$–$t$: straight line through origin, slope $-k$
- $v$–$t$: parabola $v = u - \frac{1}{2}kt^2$
- $x$–$t$: cubic $x = ut - \frac{1}{6}kt^3$

### Example: Simple Harmonic Motion ($a = -\omega^2 x$)
- $a$–$t$: sinusoidal (cosine)
- $v$–$t$: sinusoidal (sine), $\pi/2$ out of phase
- $x$–$t$: sinusoidal (sine/cosine)

---

## Distance vs Displacement from Graphs

| Quantity | From $v$–$t$ Graph |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Displacement** | Signed area (above axis +, below axis -) |
| **Distance** | Total area (all positive) |

**Rule:** Split at $v=0$ crossings.

### Example: $v = t^2 - 4t$ ($t=0$ to $4$)
- $v=0$ at $t=0, 4$
- Displacement: $\int_0^4 (t^2-4t)\,dt = \left[\frac{t^3}{3} - 2t^2\right]_0^4 = \frac{64}{3} - 32 = -\frac{32}{3}$
- Distance: $\int_0^4 |t^2-4t|\,dt = \int_0^4 (4t-t^2)\,dt = \left[2t^2 - \frac{t^3}{3}\right]_0^4 = 32 - \frac{64}{3} = \frac{32}{3}$

---

## Average Velocity / Speed from Graphs

| Average | Formula | Graphical Interpretation |
|---------|---------|--------------------------|
| Average velocity | $\bar{v} = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}$ | Slope of chord on $x$–$t$ graph |
| Average speed | $\frac{\text{distance}}{\Delta t}$ | (Total area on $v$–$t$) / $\Delta t$ |
| Mean value of $v$ | $\frac{1}{\Delta t}\int v\,dt$ | Height of rectangle with same area as $v$–$t$ graph |

---

## Finding Maximum/Minimum Displacement

From $v$–$t$ graph:
- **Maximum $x$**: When $v$ changes from +ve to -ve (area stops increasing)
- **Minimum $x$**: When $v$ changes from -ve to +ve (area stops decreasing)
- At $v=0$ crossings: check sign change

---

## Area Calculations for Common Shapes

| Shape | Area Formula | Example |
|-------|--------------|---------|
| Rectangle | $v \times t$ | Constant velocity |
| Triangle | $\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$ | Uniform acceleration from rest |
| Trapezium | $\frac{1}{2}(u+v)t$ | Constant acceleration |
| Parabolic segment | $\frac{2}{3} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$ | $v = kt^2$ from $t=0$ |
| Simpson's rule | $\frac{h}{3}(y_0 + 4y_1 + y_2)$ | Approximate curved area |

## Sketching Graphs from Description

### Given: "Particle starts from rest, accelerates uniformly for 4s, then constant velocity for 6s, then decelerates uniformly to rest in 2s"

**$v$–$t$:**
1. Triangle (0 to 4s): $v$ from 0 to $v_{\text{max}}$
2. Rectangle (4 to 10s): $v = v_{\text{max}}$
3. Triangle (10 to 12s): $v$ from $v_{\text{max}}$ to 0

**$a$–$t$:**
1. Constant $a_1$ (0 to 4s)
2. $a=0$ (4 to 10s)
3. Constant $a_2$ (10 to 12s)

**$x$–$t$:**
1. Parabola (concave up)
2. Straight line (constant gradient = $v_{\text{max}}$)
3. Parabola (concave down)

## Graphical Solution of Meeting Problems

**Problem:** Two particles A and B start from same point. A: $u_A=5$, $a_A=2$. B starts 2s later: $u_B=0$, $a_B=4$. When do they meet?

**Graphical method:**
1. Sketch both $v$–$t$ graphs on same axes
2. Area under A's graph = area under B's graph (same displacement)
3. Equate areas:
   - For $t \geq 2$: $A_A = 5t + t^2$, $A_B = 2(t-2)^2$
   - Solve: $5t + t^2 = 2(t-2)^2$

## Non-Uniform Acceleration: Numerical/Graphical Integration

When $a(t)$ given as data points or complex function:

### Trapezoidal Rule (Velocity)
$$
v_{i+1} \approx v_i + \frac{a_i + a_{i+1}}{2} \Delta t
$$

### Trapezoidal Rule (Displacement)
$$
x_{i+1} \approx x_i + \frac{v_i + v_{i+1}}{2} \Delta t
$$

### Midpoint Rule (Better for oscillatory)
$$
v_{i+1} \approx v_i + a(t_i + \frac{\Delta t}{2}) \Delta t
$$
