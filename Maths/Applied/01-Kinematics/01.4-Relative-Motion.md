# 1.4 Relative Motion (1D and 2D)

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 1.4

---

## Relative Velocity (1D)

### Definition
Velocity of A relative to B:
$$
v_{A/B} = v_A - v_B
$$
where all velocities are measured in the same inertial frame (usually ground).

### Sign Convention
- Choose positive direction
- $v_A$, $v_B$ are signed scalars
- $v_{A/B} > 0$: A moves in +ve direction relative to B

### Relative Acceleration
$$
a_{A/B} = a_A - a_B
$$
(valid for same frame, non-rotating)

---

## Relative Motion in 2D (Vectors)

### Position, Velocity, Acceleration
$$
\vec{r}_{A/B} = \vec{r}_A - \vec{r}_B
$$
$$
\vec{v}_{A/B} = \vec{v}_A - \vec{v}_B
$$
$$
\vec{a}_{A/B} = \vec{a}_A - \vec{a}_B
$$

### Magnitude and Direction
$$
|\vec{v}_{A/B}| = \sqrt{v_A^2 + v_B^2 - 2v_A v_B \cos\theta}
$$
where $\theta$ = angle between $\vec{v}_A$ and $\vec{v}_B$.

Direction $\phi$ from $\vec{v}_A$:
$$
\tan\phi = \frac{v_B \sin\theta}{v_A - v_B \cos\theta}
$$

## Applications

### 1. Closest Approach
**Problem:** Two particles moving with constant velocities. Find minimum distance between them.

**Method:**
1. Relative position: $\vec{r}_{A/B}(t) = \vec{r}_{A/B}(0) + \vec{v}_{A/B}t$
2. Distance squared: $d^2(t) = |\vec{r}_{A/B}(t)|^2$
3. Minimize: $\frac{d}{dt}d^2 = 0 \implies t_{\text{min}} = -\frac{\vec{r}_{A/B}(0) \cdot \vec{v}_{A/B}}{|\vec{v}_{A/B}|^2}$
4. Minimum distance: $d_{\text{min}} = |\vec{r}_{A/B}(0) \times \hat{v}_{A/B}|$

### 2. Interception / Collision
**Problem:** A at $\vec{r}_A$ with $\vec{v}_A$. B at $\vec{r}_B$ can choose $\vec{v}_B$ (fixed magnitude $v_B$). Find $\vec{v}_B$ to intercept.

**Condition for collision:**
$$
\vec{r}_A + \vec{v}_A t = \vec{r}_B + \vec{v}_B t \quad \text{for some } t > 0
$$
$$
\implies \vec{v}_B = \frac{\vec{r}_A - \vec{r}_B}{t} + \vec{v}_A
$$
With $|\vec{v}_B|$ fixed, solve for $t$ then $\vec{v}_B$.

### 3. Apparent Velocity (Rain/Wind Problems)
**Rain falling vertically** at $v_r$. **Person moving horizontally** at $v_p$.
- Rain's velocity relative to person: $\vec{v}_{r/p} = \vec{v}_r - \vec{v}_p$
- Angle of rain to vertical: $\tan\theta = \frac{v_p}{v_r}$
- To keep rain off face, hold umbrella tilted forward at $\theta$

### 4. River Crossing
- River velocity: $\vec{v}_r$ (parallel to banks)
- Boat speed in still water: $v_b$
- **Shortest path (perpendicular to banks):** Point upstream at $\theta$ where $v_b\sin\theta = v_r$. Time = $\frac{d}{v_b\cos\theta}$.
- **Shortest time:** Point straight across. Drift = $v_r \cdot \frac{d}{v_b}$.

### 5. Aircraft Navigation
- Airspeed: $\vec{v}_a$ (relative to air)
- Wind velocity: $\vec{v}_w$
- Ground speed: $\vec{v}_g = \vec{v}_a + \vec{v}_w$
- **Course to steer:** Given desired ground track $\vec{v}_g$, find $\vec{v}_a$ with $|\vec{v}_a| = v_{\text{air}}$.

## Relative Motion with Acceleration

If $\vec{a}_A$ and $\vec{a}_B$ are constant:
$$
\vec{v}_{A/B}(t) = \vec{v}_{A/B}(0) + \vec{a}_{A/B}t
$$
$$
\vec{r}_{A/B}(t) = \vec{r}_{A/B}(0) + \vec{v}_{A/B}(0)t + \frac{1}{2}\vec{a}_{A/B}t^2
$$
All SUVAT equations apply to **relative** quantities.

### Example: Two Cars Accelerating
Car A: $u_A=0$, $a_A=2$ m/s². Car B: $u_B=10$ m/s, $a_B=0$.
Relative: $u_{A/B} = -10$, $a_{A/B} = 2$.
Overtake when $s_{A/B} = 0$: $-10t + t^2 = 0 \implies t = 10$ s.

## Constraint Relations (Connected Particles)

### Inextensible String Over Pulley
- **Same speed magnitude:** $v_A = v_B$
- **Opposite directions:** $v_A = -v_B$ (if same +ve direction chosen)
- **Acceleration:** $a_A = -a_B$

### Multiple Pulleys (Fixed + Movable)
If movable pulley B has acceleration $a_B$, and string length $L$ constant:
$$
2a_B + a_A = 0 \quad \text{(if A fixed end, B movable)}
$$
**General method:** Write $L = \text{sum of segments}$, differentiate twice.

### Wedge/Block Systems
Block on wedge, wedge on smooth surface:
- Block velocity relative to wedge: $\vec{v}_{b/w}$ along plane
- Wedge velocity: $\vec{v}_w$ horizontal
- Block absolute velocity: $\vec{v}_b = \vec{v}_w + \vec{v}_{b/w}$
- Constraint: horizontal component of $\vec{v}_b$ relates to $v_w$

---

## Minimum Distance Between Two Moving Particles (General)

**Given:** $\vec{r}_A(t)$, $\vec{r}_B(t)$ (any motion, not necessarily constant velocity)

1. $\vec{r}_{\text{rel}}(t) = \vec{r}_A(t) - \vec{r}_B(t)$
2. $D^2(t) = |\vec{r}_{\text{rel}}(t)|^2$
3. Solve $\frac{d}{dt}D^2(t) = 0$ for $t$
4. Check $t \geq 0$ and $\frac{d^2}{dt^2}D^2 > 0$

---

## Relative Angular Velocity

For particles moving in circles about same fixed point:
$$
\vec{\omega}_{A/B} = \vec{\omega}_A - \vec{\omega}_B
$$

---

## Centre of Mass Frame

For system of particles:
$$
\vec{v}_{CM} = \frac{\sum m_i \vec{v}_i}{\sum m_i}
$$
Relative to CM: $\vec{v}_i' = \vec{v}_i - \vec{v}_{CM}$

**Useful because:**
- Total momentum in CM frame = 0
- Kinetic energy: $K = K_{CM} + K'$
- Collision problems simplify in CM frame

## Problem-Solving Checklist

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Draw diagram with all velocities as vectors |
| 2 | Choose coordinate system (axes) |
| 3 | Write $\vec{v}_A$, $\vec{v}_B$ in components |
| 4 | Compute $\vec{v}_{A/B} = \vec{v}_A - \vec{v}_B$ |
| 5 | Apply relevant condition (collision, closest approach, etc.) |
| 6 | Solve for unknowns |
| 7 | Check physical sense (time > 0, directions consistent) |

## Common Exam Questions

| Type | Key Insight |
|------|-------------|
| **Two particles meet** | Equate position vectors $\vec{r}_A(t) = \vec{r}_B(t)$ |
| **Overtaking** | Relative motion: $s_{\text{rel}} = u_{\text{rel}}t + \frac{1}{2}a_{\text{rel}}t^2$ |
| **River/rain/wind** | Vector subtraction $\vec{v}_{\text{rel}} = \vec{v} - \vec{v}_{\text{medium}}$ |
| **Interception** | $\vec{v}_B = \frac{\vec{r}_A - \vec{r}_B}{t} + \vec{v}_A$ with $|\vec{v}_B|$ fixed |
| **Closest approach** | $t_{\text{min}} = -\frac{\vec{r}_0 \cdot \vec{v}_{\text{rel}}}{|\vec{v}_{\text{rel}}|^2}$ |
| **Connected particles** | Constraint from constant string length |
