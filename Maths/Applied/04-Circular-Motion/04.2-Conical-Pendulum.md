# 4.2 Conical Pendulum

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 4.2  
**Prerequisites:** Uniform Circular Motion (4.1), Forces in Equilibrium (Statics 3.1), Trigonometry  
**Cross-refs:** Physics Mechanics Unit 2, Simple Harmonic Motion (5.3 — small oscillations)

---

## Physical Setup

A particle of mass $m$ attached to a light inextensible string of length $L$, fixed at point $O$. The particle moves in a horizontal circle of radius $r$ with constant angular velocity $\omega$. The string makes angle $\theta$ with the vertical.

```
        O
        |
        | L
        |
       / \
      / θ \
     /     \
    ●───────●  (horizontal circle, radius r)
     mass m
```

**Geometry:** $r = L\sin\theta$, $h = L\cos\theta$ (vertical height below $O$)

---

## Force Resolution

**Forces on particle:**
1. Weight $mg$ (vertically down)
2. Tension $T$ (along string, at angle $\theta$ to vertical)

Resolve vertically (equilibrium, no vertical acceleration):
$$
T\cos\theta = mg \tag{1}
$$

Resolve horizontally (towards centre, centripetal acceleration):
$$
T\sin\theta = m\omega^2 r = m\omega^2 L\sin\theta \tag{2}
$$

---

## Key Results

### Angular Velocity
From (2), assuming $\sin\theta \neq 0$:
$$
\omega^2 = \frac{T}{mL} \quad \text{and from (1) } T = \frac{mg}{\cos\theta}
$$
$$
\boxed{\omega = \sqrt{\frac{g}{L\cos\theta}}}
$$

### Tension
$$
\boxed{T = \frac{mg}{\cos\theta}}
$$

### Radius of Circle
$$
r = L\sin\theta
$$

### Period
$$
T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L\cos\theta}{g}}
$$

### Height of Plane
$$
h = L\cos\theta = \frac{g}{\omega^2}
$$
**Remark:** Period depends only on vertical height $h$, not on $L$ or $\theta$ individually.

### Linear Speed
$$
v = \omega r = \omega L\sin\theta = \sqrt{\frac{g}{L\cos\theta}} \cdot L\sin\theta = \sqrt{gL\tan\theta\sin\theta}
$$

---

## Constraints and Limits

| Parameter | Constraint | Physical Meaning |
|-----------|------------|------------------|
| $\cos\theta$ | $0 < \cos\theta \leq 1$ | $\theta \in [0, 90^\circ)$ |
| $\omega$ | $\omega \geq \sqrt{g/L}$ | Minimum $\omega$ when $\theta \to 0$ |
| $\theta$ | $\theta \to 0$ as $\omega \to \sqrt{g/L}$ | String nearly vertical, small circle |
| $\theta$ | $\theta \to 90^\circ$ as $\omega \to \infty$ | String horizontal, infinite $\omega$ needed |

**Minimum angular velocity:** $\omega_{min} = \sqrt{g/L}$ (when $\theta = 0$, particle hangs vertically, no circular motion)

**String cannot be horizontal** ($\theta = 90^\circ$) as it would require infinite tension and infinite $\omega$.

---

## Energy Perspective

Kinetic energy: $K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2 L^2\sin^2\theta$  
Potential energy (relative to $O$): $U = -mgL\cos\theta$  
Total energy: $E = \frac{1}{2}m\frac{g}{L\cos\theta}L^2\sin^2\theta - mgL\cos\theta$  
(Not conserved if $\omega$ is maintained externally)

---

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Find $\omega$ and $T$
$m = 0.8 \text{ kg}$, $L = 1.5 \text{ m}$, $\theta = 40^\circ$, $g = 9.8 \text{ m/s}^2$

**Solution:**
$$
T = \frac{0.8 \times 9.8}{\cos 40^\circ} = \frac{7.84}{0.766} \approx 10.24 \text{ N}
$$
$$
\omega = \sqrt{\frac{9.8}{1.5 \cos 40^\circ}} = \sqrt{\frac{9.8}{1.149}} \approx 2.92 \text{ rad/s}
$$
$$
T_{period} = \frac{2\pi}{2.92} \approx 2.15 \text{ s}
$$

### Example 2: Given $\omega$, find $\theta$ and $T$
$m = 1.2 \text{ kg}$, $L = 2.0 \text{ m}$, $\omega = 3 \text{ rad/s}$

**Solution:**
$$
\cos\theta = \frac{g}{L\omega^2} = \frac{9.8}{2 \times 9} = \frac{9.8}{18} \approx 0.5444
$$
$$
\theta = \cos^{-1}(0.5444) \approx 57.0^\circ
$$
$$
T = \frac{mg}{\cos\theta} = \frac{1.2 \times 9.8}{0.5444} \approx 21.6 \text{ N}
$$

### Example 3: Maximum angle before string breaks
$m = 0.5 \text{ kg}$, $L = 1 \text{ m}$, $T_{max} = 20 \text{ N}$

**Solution:**
$$
T = \frac{mg}{\cos\theta} \leq 20 \implies \cos\theta \geq \frac{0.5 \times 9.8}{20} = 0.245
$$
$$
\theta_{max} = \cos^{-1}(0.245) \approx 75.8^\circ
$$
At this angle: $\omega = \sqrt{\frac{g}{L\cos\theta}} = \sqrt{\frac{9.8}{0.245}} \approx 6.32 \text{ rad/s}$

---

## Conical Pendulum vs Simple Pendulum

| Aspect | Conical Pendulum | Simple Pendulum |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| Motion | Horizontal circle | Vertical plane oscillation |
| Period | $T = 2\pi\sqrt{L\cos\theta/g}$ | $T = 2\pi\sqrt{L/g}$ (small angles) |
| Forces | $T\cos\theta = mg$, $T\sin\theta = m\omega^2 r$ | $T - mg\cos\theta = mv^2/L$ |
| Energy | Not conserved (driven) | Conserved (ideal) |
| Angle | Constant $\theta$ | Varies with time |

**Small oscillation limit:** If conical pendulum is disturbed slightly from equilibrium, it executes SHM with period $T = 2\pi\sqrt{L\cos\theta/g}$ — same as steady circular motion period. (See Topic 5: SHM)

---

## Common Exam Questions

1. **Given $m, L, \theta$** — find $\omega$, $T$, period, speed
2. **Given $m, L, \omega$** — find $\theta$, $T$, $r$
3. **Maximum angle/speed** before string breaks
4. **Minimum $\omega$** for conical motion to be possible
5. **Prove period depends only on height** $h = L\cos\theta$
6. **Comparison with simple pendulum** period
7. **Transition from vertical** — show $\omega \geq \sqrt{g/L}$
