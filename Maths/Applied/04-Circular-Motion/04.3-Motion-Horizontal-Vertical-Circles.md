# 4.3 Motion on Horizontal and Vertical Circles

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 4.3  
**Prerequisites:** Uniform Circular Motion (4.1), Work-Energy (Dynamics 2.3), Newton's Laws  
**Cross-refs:** Conical Pendulum (4.2), Banked Tracks (4.4), SHM (5.3)

---

## Part A: Horizontal Circle

### String (Particle on Smooth Horizontal Table)

**Setup:** Mass $m$ on smooth table, attached to string through hole, rotating horizontally.

| Force | Equation |
|-------|----------|
| Tension $T$ | $T = m\omega^2 r = \frac{mv^2}{r}$ |
| Max $\omega$ (string breaks at $T_{max}$) | $\omega_{max} = \sqrt{T_{max}/mr}$ |

**No gravity effect** on horizontal motion — tension provides pure centripetal force.

---

### Rod (Rigid Rod, Hinged at Centre)

**Setup:** Mass $m$ on light rod of length $L$, free to rotate in horizontal plane.

| Force | Equation |
|-------|----------|
| Rod force $F$ (compression/tension) | $F = m\omega^2 r$ |
| Rod can push or pull | $F > 0$: tension; $F < 0$: compression |

**Key difference from string:** Rod can support compression — particle can rotate with $\omega$ below "slack" threshold.

---

### Particle Inside Smooth Hollow Cylinder/Sphere

**Setup:** Particle moves in horizontal circle inside smooth vertical cylinder (radius $R$) or sphere.

| Force | Equation |
|-------|----------|
| Normal reaction $R$ (radial) | $R = m\omega^2 r$ |
| Vertical equilibrium | $R_{vertical} = mg$ (cylinder) or component (sphere) |

## Part B: Vertical Circle (String)

### Setup
Mass $m$ on light inextensible string of length $r$, moving in vertical plane.

**Position measured by $\theta$ from vertically downward:**
- $\theta = 0$: Bottom
- $\theta = \pi$: Top

### Forces at General Position $\theta$

Radial direction (towards centre):
$$
T - mg\cos\theta = \frac{mv^2}{r} \quad \text{or} \quad T = \frac{mv^2}{r} + mg\cos\theta
$$

Tangential direction (not needed for circular motion condition):
$$
mg\sin\theta = m a_t \quad (a_t = dv/dt)
$$

### Energy Conservation
Take zero PE at centre (or bottom). At angle $\theta$ from bottom:
$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2}mu^2 - mgr(1 - \cos\theta)
$$
where $u$ = speed at bottom ($\theta = 0$).

**Speed at angle $\theta$:**
$$
v^2 = u^2 - 2gr(1 - \cos\theta)
$$

### Tension at Angle $\theta$
Substitute $v^2$ into tension equation:
$$
T = \frac{m}{r}\left[u^2 - 2gr(1 - \cos\theta)\right] + mg\cos\theta
$$
$$
T = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 2mg(1 - \cos\theta) + mg\cos\theta
$$
$$
T = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 2mg + 3mg\cos\theta
$$

### Key Tensions
| Position | $\theta$ | $\cos\theta$ | Tension $T$ |
|----------|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Bottom | $0$ | $1$ | $T_{bottom} = \frac{mu^2}{r} + mg$ |
| Horizontal | $\pi/2$ | $0$ | $T_{horiz} = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 2mg$ |
| Top | $\pi$ | $-1$ | $T_{top} = \frac{mu^2}{r} - 5mg$ |

### Condition for Complete Circles (String)

String must remain taut: $T \geq 0$ at all points. Critical point is top ($\theta = \pi$):
$$
T_{top} = \frac{mv_{top}^2}{r} - mg \geq 0 \implies v_{top}^2 \geq gr
$$
Using energy from bottom:
$$
v_{top}^2 = u^2 - 4gr \geq gr \implies u^2 \geq 5gr
$$
$$
\boxed{u \geq \sqrt{5gr}} \quad \text{(minimum speed at bottom for complete circles)}
$$

**If $u < \sqrt{5gr}$:** String goes slack before top. Particle becomes projectile.

### Motion Types by Initial Speed $u$

| Range | Motion |
|-------|--------|
| $u < \sqrt{2gr}$ | Oscillates like pendulum (doesn't reach horizontal) |
| $\sqrt{2gr} \leq u < \sqrt{3gr}$ | Reaches horizontal, goes slack before top |
| $\sqrt{3gr} \leq u < \sqrt{5gr}$ | Passes horizontal, goes slack between horizontal and top |
| $u \geq \sqrt{5gr}$ | Complete circles, string always taut |

## Part C: Vertical Circle (Rod)

### Setup
Mass $m$ on light rigid rod of length $r$, pivoted at one end.

**Key difference:** Rod can exert compression ($F < 0$) or tension ($F > 0$).

### Force at Angle $\theta$
$$
F = \frac{mv^2}{r} + mg\cos\theta
$$
where $v^2 = u^2 - 2gr(1 - \cos\theta)$ (same energy equation).

### Condition for Complete Circles (Rod)
Rod must not go slack: $F \geq 0$ at all points. At top ($\theta = \pi$):
$$
F_{top} = \frac{mv_{top}^2}{r} - mg \geq 0 \implies v_{top}^2 \geq gr
$$
Same as string! But rod can support compression, so minimum condition is $v_{top} \geq 0$ (just reaches top):
$$
v_{top}^2 = u^2 - 4gr \geq 0 \implies u \geq \sqrt{4gr} = 2\sqrt{gr}
$$

**Wait** — if $v_{top} = 0$, rod is in compression $F_{top} = -mg$. This is allowed for rigid rod.

**Correct condition for rod:**
- **Complete circles:** $u \geq 2\sqrt{gr}$ (reaches top with $v \geq 0$)
- **Oscillates:** $u < 2\sqrt{gr}$

**But wait** — if $u < 2\sqrt{gr}$, it doesn't reach top. It oscillates.
If $u = 2\sqrt{gr}$, just reaches top with $v=0$, then falls back.

Actually, for **complete circles without losing contact**, we need $v_{top} > 0$ strictly. But "complete circles" usually means passes through top. For rod:
- $u \geq 2\sqrt{gr}$: passes through top (may have $v_{top} = 0$ instantaneously)
- $u < 2\sqrt{gr}$: oscillates

**Comparison string vs rod:**
| | String | Rod |
|---|--------|-----|
| Complete circles | $u \geq \sqrt{5gr}$ | $u \geq 2\sqrt{gr}$ |
| Min speed at top | $\sqrt{gr}$ | $0$ |
| At top, $v = 0$ | String slack, particle falls | Rod in compression, particle stays on circle |

## Part D: Particle on Inside/Outside of Smooth Sphere

### Inside Sphere (e.g., bead on wire hoop)
Vertical circle of radius $R$. Normal reaction $R_N$ replaces tension.

Radial equation:
$$
mg\cos\theta - R_N = \frac{mv^2}{R}
$$
Condition for maintaining contact: $R_N \geq 0 \implies v^2 \leq gR\cos\theta$

Using energy: $v^2 = u^2 - 2gR(1 - \cos\theta)$

Contact lost when:
$$
u^2 - 2gR(1 - \cos\theta) = gR\cos\theta \implies u^2 = 2gR - gR\cos\theta = gR(2 - \cos\theta)
$$

### Outside Sphere (Particle on Smooth Sphere)
Particle starts at top ($\theta = 0$) with small push. Slides down.

Radial equation (outward positive):
$$
R_N - mg\cos\theta = -\frac{mv^2}{R} \implies R_N = mg\cos\theta - \frac{mv^2}{R}
$$
Energy: $v^2 = 2gR(1 - \cos\theta)$ (from rest at top)

Contact lost when $R_N = 0$:
$$
mg\cos\theta = \frac{m}{R} \cdot 2gR(1 - \cos\theta) = 2mg(1 - \cos\theta)
$$
$$
\cos\theta = 2 - 2\cos\theta \implies 3\cos\theta = 2 \implies \cos\theta = \frac{2}{3}
$$
$$
\boxed{\theta = \cos^{-1}(2/3) \approx 48.2^\circ}
$$
This is a classic result — particle leaves sphere at fixed angle regardless of $R$ or $g$!

## Summary: Conditions for Complete Vertical Circles

| System | Min Speed at Bottom $u_{min}$ | Min Speed at Top $v_{top}$ | Notes |
|--------|------------------------------|---------------------------|-------|
| String | $\sqrt{5gr}$ | $\sqrt{gr}$ | String taut throughout |
| Rod | $2\sqrt{gr}$ | $0$ | Rod can compress |
| Inside sphere (wire) | $\sqrt{4gr}$? | — | Normal reaction $\geq 0$ |
| Outside sphere | — | — | Leaves at $\cos\theta = 2/3$ |

**Derivation check for rod:**
Energy: $\frac{1}{2}mu^2 = \frac{1}{2}mv_{top}^2 + 2mgr$
Complete circle $\iff v_{top}^2 \geq 0 \iff u^2 \geq 4gr \iff u \geq 2\sqrt{gr}$. ✓

**For string:** $T_{top} \geq 0 \implies v_{top}^2 \geq gr \implies u^2 \geq 5gr$. ✓

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: String, find max height if $u = \sqrt{3gr}$
$v^2 = u^2 - 2gr(1-\cos\theta) = 3gr - 2gr + 2gr\cos\theta = gr + 2gr\cos\theta$
String slack when $T = 0 = \frac{mv^2}{r} + mg\cos\theta$:
$$
0 = \frac{m}{r}(gr + 2gr\cos\theta) + mg\cos\theta = mg + 2mg\cos\theta + mg\cos\theta
$$
$$
3mg\cos\theta = -mg \implies \cos\theta = -\frac{1}{3} \implies \theta \approx 109.5^\circ
$$
Height above bottom: $h = r(1-\cos\theta) = r(1 + 1/3) = 4r/3$

### Example 2: Rod, $u = 3\sqrt{gr}$, find force at top
$v_{top}^2 = u^2 - 4gr = 9gr - 4gr = 5gr$
$F_{top} = \frac{m(5gr)}{r} - mg = 5mg - mg = 4mg$ (tension)
