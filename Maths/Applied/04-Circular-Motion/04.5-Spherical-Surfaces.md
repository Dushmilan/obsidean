# 4.5 Motion on Spherical Surfaces

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 4.5  
**Prerequisites:** Uniform Circular Motion (4.1), Vertical Circles (4.3), Work-Energy (Dynamics 2.3), Conservation of Energy  
**Cross-refs:** Physics Mechanics Unit 2, Particle on Sphere (classic problem), Conical Pendulum (4.2)

---

## Physical Setup

A particle of mass $m$ moves on a smooth (frictionless) spherical surface of radius $R$. Two main cases:

1. **Inside** the sphere (bead on wire hoop, particle in spherical bowl)
2. **Outside** the sphere (particle on top of sphere, sliding down)

---

## Case 1: Particle on Outside of Smooth Sphere

### Setup
Particle placed at top of sphere ($\theta = 0$) and given a small horizontal push (or released from rest). Slides down under gravity.

**Coordinates:** Angle $\theta$ measured from vertical (downward).

### Forces
1. Weight $mg$ (vertically down)
2. Normal reaction $R$ (radially outward from sphere centre)

### Radial Equation (Newton's 2nd Law towards centre)
$$
mg\cos\theta - R = \frac{mv^2}{R} \tag{1}
$$

### Energy Conservation (from top, $\theta = 0$, $v = u$)
Take zero PE at centre of sphere. At angle $\theta$:
Height above centre: $R\cos\theta$
$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^2 + mgR\cos\theta = \frac{1}{2}mu^2 + mgR \tag{2}
$$

If released from rest at top: $u = 0$
$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^2 = mgR(1 - \cos\theta) \implies v^2 = 2gR(1 - \cos\theta) \tag{3}
$$

### Normal Reaction
Substitute (3) into (1):
$$
R = mg\cos\theta - \frac{m}{R} \cdot 2gR(1 - \cos\theta)
$$
$$
R = mg\cos\theta - 2mg(1 - \cos\theta)
$$
$$
\boxed{R = mg(3\cos\theta - 2)}
$$

### Condition for Maintaining Contact
Contact is lost when $R = 0$:
$$
mg(3\cos\theta - 2) = 0 \implies \cos\theta = \frac{2}{3}
$$
$$
\boxed{\theta = \cos^{-1}(2/3) \approx 48.2^\circ}
$$

**Key result:** Independent of $m$, $R$, $g$! (As long as $g$ is uniform)

### Speed at Loss of Contact
$$
v^2 = 2gR(1 - 2/3) = 2gR/3 \implies v = \sqrt{\frac{2gR}{3}}
$$

### Height Above Centre at Loss of Contact
$$
h = R\cos\theta = \frac{2R}{3}
$$

### Subsequent Motion
After losing contact, particle becomes a projectile with:
- Initial position: $(x, y) = (R\sin\theta, R\cos\theta) = (R\sqrt{5}/3, 2R/3)$
- Initial velocity: $v = \sqrt{2gR/3}$ tangential to sphere

## Case 2: Particle on Inside of Smooth Sphere (or Bowl)

### Setup
Particle moves in a vertical plane inside a smooth spherical bowl of radius $R$. Can also be a bead on a smooth circular wire hoop of radius $R$.

### Forces
1. Weight $mg$ (down)
2. Normal reaction $R$ (radially inward — sphere pushes particle toward centre)

### Radial Equation (towards centre)
$$
R - mg\cos\theta = \frac{mv^2}{R} \tag{4}
$$
Note sign difference: $R$ is inward, $mg\cos\theta$ component is outward.

### Energy Conservation (from bottom, $\theta = 0$, speed $u$)
Height above bottom: $R(1 - \cos\theta)$
$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2}mu^2 - mgR(1 - \cos\theta)
$$
$$
v^2 = u^2 - 2gR(1 - \cos\theta) \tag{5}
$$

### Normal Reaction
Substitute (5) into (4):
$$
R = mg\cos\theta + \frac{m}{R}\left[u^2 - 2gR(1 - \cos\theta)\right]
$$
$$
R = \frac{mu^2}{R} + mg\cos\theta - 2mg(1 - \cos\theta)
$$
$$
\boxed{R = \frac{mu^2}{R} - 2mg + 3mg\cos\theta}
$$

### Condition for Maintaining Contact (Complete Circles)
$R \geq 0$ for all $\theta$. Minimum at $\theta = \pi$ (top): $\cos\theta = -1$
$$
R_{min} = \frac{mu^2}{R} - 2mg - 3mg = \frac{mu^2}{R} - 5mg \geq 0
$$
$$
\boxed{u^2 \geq 5gR} \quad \text{or} \quad \boxed{u \geq \sqrt{5gR}}
$$

**Same condition as string vertical circle!** (Because $R$ replaces $T$, and both must be $\geq 0$)

### Oscillations (If $u < \sqrt{5gR}$)
Particle oscillates between $\pm\theta_{max}$ where $v = 0$:
$$
0 = u^2 - 2gR(1 - \cos\theta_{max}) \implies \cos\theta_{max} = 1 - \frac{u^2}{2gR}
$$

For small oscillations about bottom ($\theta \approx 0$):
$$
v^2 \approx u^2 - gR\theta^2, \quad \text{SHM with } \omega = \sqrt{g/R}
$$
Period: $T = 2\pi\sqrt{R/g}$ (same as simple pendulum of length $R$)

## Case 3: Conical Motion on Sphere (Horizontal Circles)

### Setup
Particle moves in a horizontal circle at constant height on smooth sphere. String attached to top? No — just normal reaction.

**Angle from vertical:** $\alpha$ (constant)

### Forces
- Weight $mg$ down
- Normal reaction $R$ at angle $\alpha$ to vertical

### Resolve
Vertically: $R\cos\alpha = mg \implies R = mg/\cos\alpha$
Horizontally: $R\sin\alpha = m\omega^2 r = m\omega^2 R\sin\alpha$
Cancel $R\sin\alpha$ (assuming $\alpha \neq 0$):
$$
\omega^2 = \frac{g}{R\cos\alpha} \quad \text{or} \quad \omega = \sqrt{\frac{g}{R\cos\alpha}}
$$

**Compare with conical pendulum:** Identical form with $L \to R$!

Height of circle: $h = R\cos\alpha = g/\omega^2$
Radius of circle: $r = R\sin\alpha$

**Condition for existence:** $\cos\alpha > 0 \implies \alpha < 90^\circ$, always true for inside sphere. For outside sphere, $\omega$ must be large enough that $R > 0$.

---

## Case 4: Particle on Outside, Given Initial Speed $u$ at Top

Generalizes Case 1. Energy from top:
$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2}mu^2 + mgR(1 - \cos\theta)
$$
$$
v^2 = u^2 + 2gR(1 - \cos\theta)
$$

Normal reaction:
$$
R = mg\cos\theta - \frac{m}{R}\left[u^2 + 2gR(1 - \cos\theta)\right]
$$
$$
R = mg(3\cos\theta - 2) - \frac{mu^2}{R}
$$

Contact lost when $R = 0$:
$$
mg(3\cos\theta - 2) = \frac{mu^2}{R} \implies \cos\theta = \frac{2}{3} + \frac{u^2}{3gR}
$$

**Check:** $u = 0 \implies \cos\theta = 2/3$ (Case 1). As $u$ increases, $\cos\theta$ increases, $\theta$ decreases — particle leaves sooner.

If $u^2 \geq gR$, then $\cos\theta \geq 1$ — particle leaves immediately at top (or never makes contact).

---

## Summary: Key Formulae

| Scenario | Normal Reaction $R$ | Contact Lost When | Critical Angle/Speed |
|----------|---------------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Outside, from rest | $mg(3\cos\theta - 2)$ | $R = 0$ | $\cos\theta = 2/3$ |
| Outside, speed $u$ at top | $mg(3\cos\theta - 2) - mu^2/R$ | $R = 0$ | $\cos\theta = 2/3 + u^2/3gR$ |
| Inside, speed $u$ at bottom | $mu^2/R - 2mg + 3mg\cos\theta$ | $R = 0$ at top | $u \geq \sqrt{5gR}$ |
| Inside, oscillations | Same formula | — | $\cos\theta_{max} = 1 - u^2/2gR$ |
| Conical on sphere | $R = mg/\cos\alpha$ | $R > 0$ always | $\omega^2 = g/(R\cos\alpha)$ |

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Outside Sphere, Find Angle of Departure
$R = 0.5 \text{ m}$, $g = 9.8$. Particle released from rest at top.
**Answer:** $\theta = \cos^{-1}(2/3) \approx 48.2^\circ$ (independent of $R, g$)

### Example 2: Inside Sphere, Complete Circles
$R = 1.2 \text{ m}$. Find minimum speed at bottom for complete circles.
**Answer:** $u = \sqrt{5gR} = \sqrt{5 \times 9.8 \times 1.2} = \sqrt{58.8} \approx 7.67 \text{ m/s}$

### Example 3: Conical Motion on Sphere
$R = 2 \text{ m}$, particle moves in horizontal circle at $\alpha = 30^\circ$.
$\omega = \sqrt{g/(R\cos\alpha)} = \sqrt{9.8/(2 \times \cos 30^\circ)} = \sqrt{9.8/(2 \times 0.866)} = \sqrt{5.66} \approx 2.38 \text{ rad/s}$

### Example 4: Projectile After Leaving Sphere
Particle leaves at $\theta = \cos^{-1}(2/3)$ with $v = \sqrt{2gR/3}$.
Find horizontal distance from centre where it hits ground (ground at sphere centre level).

**Solution:**
Initial height: $y_0 = R\cos\theta = 2R/3$
Initial velocity: horizontal component $v_x = v = \sqrt{2gR/3}$, vertical $v_y = 0$
Time to fall: $y_0 = \frac{1}{2}gt^2 \implies t = \sqrt{4R/3g}$
Horizontal distance: $x = v_x t = \sqrt{2gR/3} \cdot \sqrt{4R/3g} = \sqrt{8R^2/9} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}R$

## Common Exam Questions

1. **Particle on outside of sphere** — show leaves at $\cos^{-1}(2/3)$
2. **Particle on inside of sphere** — minimum $u$ for complete circles ($\sqrt{5gR}$)
3. **Normal reaction as function of $\theta$** — derive $R(\theta)$
4. **Given $u$, find angle where contact lost** — solve $R(\theta) = 0$
5. **Conical motion on sphere** — find $\omega$, $R$, height
6. **Subsequent projectile motion** — after leaving sphere
7. **Compare with string/rod vertical circle** — same $u_{min} = \sqrt{5gR}$ for string/inside sphere
8. **Oscillations in bowl** — period for small oscillations ($T = 2\pi\sqrt{R/g}$)
