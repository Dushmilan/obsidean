# 4.4 Motion on Banked Tracks

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 4.4  
**Prerequisites:** Uniform Circular Motion (4.1), Friction (Statics 3.2), Force Resolution  
**Cross-refs:** Physics Mechanics Unit 2, Vehicle Dynamics, Conical Pendulum (4.2)

---

## Physical Setup

A vehicle of mass $m$ moves in a horizontal circle of radius $r$ on a banked track inclined at angle $\theta$ to the horizontal.

```
        ↗  Velocity v (into page)
       /|
      / | θ
     /  |
    /___|
   Banked track
```

**Forces:**
1. Weight $mg$ (vertically down)
2. Normal reaction $R$ (perpendicular to track surface)
3. Friction $F$ (parallel to track surface — direction depends on speed)

---

## Case 1: No Friction (Ideal Banking)

**Assumption:** Friction is negligible or speed is exactly the "design speed".

Resolve vertically (no vertical acceleration):
$$
R\cos\theta = mg \tag{1}
$$

Resolve horizontally (towards centre, centripetal acceleration $v^2/r$):
$$
R\sin\theta = \frac{mv^2}{r} \tag{2}
$$

Divide (2) by (1):
$$
\tan\theta = \frac{v^2}{rg}
$$

**Design speed (ideal speed):**
$$
\boxed{v_0 = \sqrt{rg\tan\theta}}
$$

**Corresponding angular velocity:**
$$
\omega_0 = \frac{v_0}{r} = \sqrt{\frac{g\tan\theta}{r}}
$$

**Key insight:** At this speed, no friction is needed. The normal reaction alone provides the centripetal force.

---

## Case 2: With Friction ($\mu > 0$)

Friction acts to oppose relative motion (sliding up/down the bank).

### Subcase 2a: Vehicle Tends to Slide **Up** the Bank ($v > v_0$)

Friction acts **down** the bank.

```
Resolve vertically:  R\cos\theta - F\sin\theta = mg
Resolve horizontally: R\sin\theta + F\cos\theta = mv^2/r
```
With $F = \mu R$ (limiting friction):
$$
\begin{aligned}
R(\cos\theta - \mu\sin\theta) &= mg \\
R(\sin\theta + \mu\cos\theta) &= \frac{mv^2}{r}
\end{aligned}
$$

Divide second by first:
$$
\frac{\sin\theta + \mu\cos\theta}{\cos\theta - \mu\sin\theta} = \frac{v^2}{rg}
$$
$$
\frac{\tan\theta + \mu}{1 - \mu\tan\theta} = \frac{v^2}{rg}
$$

**Maximum speed without skidding up:**
$$
\boxed{v_{max} = \sqrt{\frac{rg(\tan\theta + \mu)}{1 - \mu\tan\theta}}}
$$

### Subcase 2b: Vehicle Tends to Slide **Down** the Bank ($v < v_0$)

Friction acts **up** the bank.

```
Resolve vertically:  R\cos\theta + F\sin\theta = mg
Resolve horizontally: R\sin\theta - F\cos\theta = mv^2/r
```
With $F = \mu R$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R(\cos\theta + \mu\sin\theta) &= mg \\
R(\sin\theta - \mu\cos\theta) &= \frac{mv^2}{r}
\end{aligned}
$$

Divide:
$$
\frac{\sin\theta - \mu\cos\theta}{\cos\theta + \mu\sin\theta} = \frac{v^2}{rg}
$$
$$
\frac{\tan\theta - \mu}{1 + \mu\tan\theta} = \frac{v^2}{rg}
$$

**Minimum speed without slipping down:**
$$
\boxed{v_{min} = \sqrt{\frac{rg(\tan\theta - \mu)}{1 + \mu\tan\theta}}}
$$

---

## Speed Range Summary

For a given bank angle $\theta$ and coefficient of friction $\mu$:

| Condition | Speed Range | Friction Direction |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| No skidding | $v_{min} \leq v \leq v_{max}$ | Down if $v > v_0$, Up if $v < v_0$ |
| Ideal (no friction needed) | $v = v_0 = \sqrt{rg\tan\theta}$ | None |
| Too fast ($v > v_{max}$) | Skids up the bank | Down (maxed at $\mu R$) |
| Too slow ($v < v_{min}$) | Slips down the bank | Up (maxed at $\mu R$) |

**Condition for $v_{min}$ to be real (positive):**
$$
\tan\theta > \mu \quad \text{or} \quad \theta > \tan^{-1}\mu
$$
If $\tan\theta \leq \mu$, then $v_{min} = 0$ — vehicle won't slip down even at rest.

---

## Overturning vs Skidding

For a vehicle with centre of mass height $h$ above track, track width $2d$ (distance between wheels).

### Overturning Condition
Moments about outer wheel contact point:
- Overturning moment: $\frac{mv^2}{r} \cdot h$
- Restoring moment: $mg \cdot d$

Overturns when:
$$
\frac{mv^2}{r} h > mg d \implies v^2 > \frac{grd}{h}
$$

### Skidding Condition
Friction limit: $F \leq \mu R$. With banking:
$$
F = \frac{mv^2}{r}\cos\theta - mg\sin\theta \quad \text{(resolve along bank)}
$$
$$
R = mg\cos\theta + \frac{mv^2}{r}\sin\theta
$$
Skids when $|F| = \mu R$.

### Which Happens First?
Compare $v_{overturn} = \sqrt{grd/h}$ with $v_{max}$ from friction.

Typically:
- **Low centre of gravity, wide track** ($d/h$ large): Skids first
- **High centre of gravity, narrow track** ($d/h$ small): Overturns first

---

## Design of Banked Tracks

### Design Speed
Choose $\theta$ for a given design speed $v_d$ and radius $r$:
$$
\theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{v_d^2}{rg}\right)
$$

### Railway vs Road
| Aspect | Railway | Road |
|--------|---------|------|
| Friction | Very low (steel on steel) | Higher (rubber on asphalt) |
| Banking | Precisely engineered for design speed | Designed for range of speeds |
| Superelevation | $h = G\tan\theta$ ($G$ = gauge) | Crossfall + superelevation |
| Transition | Spiral curves (clothoid) | Superelevation runoff |

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Ideal Banking
$r = 200 \text{ m}$, $\theta = 15^\circ$, $g = 9.8 \text{ m/s}^2$. Find design speed.
$$
v_0 = \sqrt{200 \times 9.8 \times \tan 15^\circ} = \sqrt{1960 \times 0.2679} \approx \sqrt{525} \approx 22.9 \text{ m/s} \approx 82.5 \text{ km/h}
$$

### Example 2: With Friction
$r = 100 \text{ m}$, $\theta = 20^\circ$, $\mu = 0.4$, $g = 9.8$.

$v_0 = \sqrt{100 \times 9.8 \times \tan 20^\circ} = \sqrt{980 \times 0.364} \approx 18.9 \text{ m/s}$

$v_{max} = \sqrt{\frac{100 \times 9.8 (\tan 20^\circ + 0.4)}{1 - 0.4 \tan 20^\circ}} = \sqrt{\frac{980 (0.364 + 0.4)}{1 - 0.1456}} = \sqrt{\frac{980 \times 0.764}{0.8544}} \approx \sqrt{876} \approx 29.6 \text{ m/s}$

$v_{min} = \sqrt{\frac{100 \times 9.8 (\tan 20^\circ - 0.4)}{1 + 0.4 \tan 20^\circ}} = \sqrt{\frac{980 (-0.036)}{1.1456}}$ → imaginary!

Since $\tan 20^\circ \approx 0.364 < \mu = 0.4$, $v_{min} = 0$. Vehicle won't slip down even at rest.

### Example 3: Find $\mu$ Given Speed Range
$r = 150 \text{ m}$, $\theta = 10^\circ$, safe range $15 \text{ m/s} \leq v \leq 25 \text{ m/s}$.

Use $v_{max}$ formula with $v = 25$:
$$
\frac{25^2}{150 \times 9.8} = \frac{\tan 10^\circ + \mu}{1 - \mu\tan 10^\circ}
$$
$$
0.425 = \frac{0.176 + \mu}{1 - 0.176\mu} \implies 0.425 - 0.0748\mu = 0.176 + \mu
$$
$$
1.0748\mu = 0.249 \implies \mu \approx 0.23
$$

## Common Exam Questions

1. **Find design speed** given $r$, $\theta$ (no friction)
2. **Find max/min speed** given $r$, $\theta$, $\mu$
3. **Find $\mu$** given speed range and banking
4. **Skidding vs overturning** — which occurs first?
5. **Vehicle at rest on bank** — will it slip? ($\tan\theta \leq \mu$)
6. **Friction direction** — up or down the bank?
7. **Given $v$, find $F$ and $R$** — force magnitudes
8. **Railway design** — cant (superelevation) for given speed
