# Impulse and Momentum

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 2.4

---

## 1. Linear Momentum

**Definition:**
$$
\vec{p} = m\vec{v}
$$

**Newton's Second Law (momentum form):**
$$
\sum \vec{F} = \frac{d\vec{p}}{dt}
$$

For constant mass:
$$
\sum \vec{F} = m\frac{d\vec{v}}{dt} = m\vec{a}
$$

---

## 2. Impulse

**Definition:** Integral of force over time
$$
\vec{I} = \int_{t_1}^{t_2} \vec{F}(t)\,dt
$$

**Impulse-Momentum Theorem:**
$$
\vec{I} = \Delta \vec{p} = \vec{p}_f - \vec{p}_i
$$

**For constant force:**
$$
\vec{I} = \vec{F}\Delta t = m(\vec{v}_f - \vec{v}_i)
$$

**Graphical interpretation:** Area under $F$-$t$ graph = impulse.

---

## 3. Conservation of Linear Momentum

**Principle:** If net external force on a system is zero, total momentum is constant.
$$
\sum \vec{F}_{\text{ext}} = 0 \implies \frac{d\vec{P}_{\text{total}}}{dt} = 0 \implies \vec{P}_{\text{total}} = \text{constant}
$$

For two particles:
$$
m_1\vec{u}_1 + m_2\vec{u}_2 = m_1\vec{v}_1 + m_2\vec{v}_2
$$

**Key point:** Internal forces cancel in pairs (Newton's 3rd law).

---

## 4. Impulsive Forces

**Definition:** Large force acting for very short time $\Delta t \to 0$.

**Characteristics:**
- Finite impulse $I = \int F\,dt$
- Negligible displacement during impact
- External non-impulsive forces (weight, friction) can be ignored during impact
- Momentum changes abruptly

---

## 5. Oblique Impacts

Resolve momentum conservation into components:
- **Along line of centres (normal):** Momentum conserved, coefficient of restitution applies
- **Perpendicular to line of centres (tangential):** No impulse (smooth surfaces) → momentum conserved separately

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Normal:}&\quad m_1u_{1n} + m_2u_{2n} = m_1v_{1n} + m_2v_{2n} \\
\text{Tangential:}&\quad m_1u_{1t} = m_1v_{1t}, \quad m_2u_{2t} = m_2v_{2t}
\end{aligned}
$$

---

## 6. Coefficient of Restitution ($e$)

**Definition:** Ratio of speed of separation to speed of approach along line of centres.
$$
e = \frac{\text{speed of separation}}{\text{speed of approach}} = \frac{v_{2n} - v_{1n}}{u_{1n} - u_{2n}}
$$

**Range:** $0 \leq e \leq 1$

| $e$ | Collision Type | Energy |
|-----|----------------|--------|
| $e = 1$ | Perfectly elastic | KE conserved |
| $0 < e < 1$ | Partially elastic | Some KE lost |
| $e = 0$ | Perfectly inelastic | Max KE lost, bodies stick |

---

## 7. Direct (Head-on) Collisions

**Equations:**
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Momentum:}&\quad m_1u_1 + m_2u_2 = m_1v_1 + m_2v_2 \\
\text{Restitution:}&\quad v_2 - v_1 = e(u_1 - u_2)
\end{aligned}
$$

**Solutions:**
$$
\begin{aligned}
v_1 &= \frac{m_1 - em_2}{m_1 + m_2}u_1 + \frac{m_2(1+e)}{m_1 + m_2}u_2 \\
v_2 &= \frac{m_1(1+e)}{m_1 + m_2}u_1 + \frac{m_2 - em_1}{m_1 + m_2}u_2
\end{aligned}
$$

**Special cases:**
- Equal masses ($m_1 = m_2$): $v_1 = u_2$, $v_2 = u_1$ (exchange velocities if $e=1$)
- $m_2 \gg m_1$, $u_2 = 0$: $v_1 \approx -eu_1$ (rebound with fraction $e$ of speed)
- $e = 0$ (perfectly inelastic): $v = \frac{m_1u_1 + m_2u_2}{m_1 + m_2}$

---

## 8. Kinetic Energy Loss in Collisions

$$
\Delta K = K_f - K_i = \frac{1}{2}m_1v_1^2 + \frac{1}{2}m_2v_2^2 - \frac{1}{2}m_1u_1^2 - \frac{1}{2}m_2u_2^2
$$

**For direct collision:**
$$
\Delta K = -\frac{1}{2}\frac{m_1m_2}{m_1+m_2}(1-e^2)(u_1 - u_2)^2
$$

- $\Delta K \leq 0$ (KE never increases in closed system)
- Maximum loss when $e = 0$

---

## 9. Ballistic Pendulum

**Method to measure projectile speed:**
1. Projectile ($m$) embeds in block ($M$) — inelastic collision
2. Combined mass swings to height $h$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Collision:}&\quad mv = (M+m)V \\
\text{Swing:}&\quad \frac{1}{2}(M+m)V^2 = (M+m)gh \\
\text{Result:}&\quad v = \frac{M+m}{m}\sqrt{2gh}
\end{aligned}
$$

---

## 10. Variable Mass Systems (Rockets)

**Rocket equation (Tsiolkovsky):**
$$
M\frac{dv}{dt} = v_{\text{rel}}\frac{dM}{dt} + F_{\text{ext}}
$$

For rocket in gravity (no external force except gravity):
$$
\Delta v = v_{\text{rel}}\ln\left(\frac{M_0}{M}\right) - gt
$$

---

## 11. Summary of Key Equations

| Quantity | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| Momentum | $\vec{p} = m\vec{v}$ |
| Impulse | $\vec{I} = \int \vec{F}\,dt = \Delta\vec{p}$ |
| Conservation | $\sum \vec{p}_{\text{initial}} = \sum \vec{p}_{\text{final}}$ |
| Coefficient of restitution | $e = \frac{v_{\text{sep}}}{v_{\text{app}}}$ |
| Direct collision $v_1$ | $\frac{m_1 - em_2}{m_1+m_2}u_1 + \frac{m_2(1+e)}{m_1+m_2}u_2$ |
| KE loss | $-\frac{1}{2}\frac{m_1m_2}{m_1+m_2}(1-e^2)(u_1-u_2)^2$ |
| Rocket equation | $\Delta v = v_{\text{rel}}\ln(M_0/M)$ |
