# 2.1 Newton's Laws of Motion

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 2.1

---

## Newton's Three Laws

### First Law (Law of Inertia)
> A body remains at rest or in uniform motion in a straight line unless acted upon by a net external force.

$$
\sum \vec{F} = 0 \implies \vec{a} = 0 \implies \vec{v} = \text{constant}
$$

- Defines **inertial frames** of reference
- Mass $m$ measures inertia (resistance to acceleration)
- Net force = vector sum of all forces

---

### Second Law (Law of Acceleration)
> The rate of change of momentum of a body is proportional to the net force applied, and occurs in the direction of the force.

$$
\sum \vec{F} = \frac{d\vec{p}}{dt} = \frac{d(m\vec{v})}{dt}
$$

For constant mass:
$$
\sum \vec{F} = m\vec{a}
$$

**Component form:**
$$
\sum F_x = ma_x, \quad \sum F_y = ma_y, \quad \sum F_z = ma_z
$$

---

### Third Law (Action-Reaction)
> To every action there is an equal and opposite reaction.

$$
\vec{F}_{AB} = -\vec{F}_{BA}
$$

- Forces act on **different** bodies
- Same type (both gravitational, both contact, etc.)
- Simultaneous

---

## Common Forces in Dynamics

### Weight (Gravitational Force)
$$
\vec{W} = m\vec{g}, \quad g \approx 9.8\ \text{m/s}^2 \text{ downwards}
$$

### Normal Reaction
Perpendicular to contact surface:
$$
\vec{R} \perp \text{surface}, \quad R \geq 0
$$

### Friction
- **Static:** $F_s \leq \mu_s R$ (prevents motion)
- **Kinetic:** $F_k = \mu_k R$ (opposes sliding)
- $\mu_s > \mu_k$ typically

### Tension
In light, inextensible string:
- Same magnitude throughout
- Pulls away from body
- Direction along string

## Free-Body Diagrams (FBD)

**Procedure:**
1. Isolate the body
2. Draw all forces acting **on** it (not by it)
3. Label magnitudes and directions
4. Choose coordinate axes
5. Apply $\sum \vec{F} = m\vec{a}$

**Common setups:**

| Situation | Axes Choice |
|-----------|-------------|
| Horizontal surface | $x$ horizontal, $y$ vertical |
| Inclined plane | $x$ parallel to plane, $y$ perpendicular |
| Pulley systems | Along string direction for each mass |
| Circular motion | Radial ($r$) and tangential ($t$) |

## Applying Newton's Second Law

### Rectilinear Motion (1D)
$$
F_{\text{net}} = ma
$$

### Projectile/Curvilinear Motion (2D)
$$
\sum F_x = ma_x, \quad \sum F_y = ma_y
$$

### Variable Mass Systems (rocket, raindrop)
$$
\sum \vec{F}_{\text{ext}} = \frac{d}{dt}(m\vec{v}) = m\frac{d\vec{v}}{dt} + \vec{v}\frac{dm}{dt}
$$
Thrust force: $\vec{F}_{\text{thrust}} = \vec{u}\frac{dm}{dt}$ where $\vec{u}$ = relative exhaust velocity

---

## Inertial vs Non-Inertial Frames

| Frame | Newton's Laws | Fictitious Forces |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|
| Inertial (at rest or constant velocity) | Valid | None |
| Non-inertial (accelerating) | Invalid | Required |

In frame with acceleration $\vec{a}_0$:
$$
\vec{F}_{\text{real}} + \vec{F}_{\text{fict}} = m\vec{a}'
$$
where $\vec{F}_{\text{fict}} = -m\vec{a}_0$

## Applications

### 1. Block on Rough Horizontal Surface
$$
\begin{aligned}
x:&\quad F\cos\theta - \mu R = ma \\
y:&\quad R + F\sin\theta = mg
\end{aligned}
$$

### 2. Block on Rough Incline ($\theta$)
$$
\begin{aligned}
x\parallel:&\quad mg\sin\theta - F = ma \\
y\perp:&\quad R = mg\cos\theta
\end{aligned}
$$

### 3. Connected Particles (Atwood Machine)
$$
\begin{aligned}
m_1:&\quad T - m_1g = m_1a \\
m_2:&\quad m_2g - T = m_2a \\
\text{Result:}&\quad a = \frac{m_2 - m_1}{m_1 + m_2}g, \quad T = \frac{2m_1m_2}{m_1+m_2}g
\end{aligned}
$$

### 4. Lift/Scale Problems
Apparent weight = $m(g \pm a)$
- Lift accelerating up: $R = m(g+a)$
- Lift accelerating down: $R = m(g-a)$
- Free fall: $R = 0$ (weightlessness)

---

## Limitations of Newton's Laws

1. **High speeds** ($v \sim c$) → Special Relativity
2. **Small scales** (atomic) → Quantum Mechanics
3. **Non-inertial frames** → Add fictitious forces
4. **Variable mass** → Use $\vec{F} = \frac{d\vec{p}}{dt}$ form
