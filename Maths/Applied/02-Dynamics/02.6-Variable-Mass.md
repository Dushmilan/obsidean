# Variable Mass Systems

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 2.6

---

## 1. General Equation

For a system gaining/losing mass:
$$
M\frac{dv}{dt} = v_{\text{rel}}\frac{dM}{dt} + \sum F_{\text{ext}}
$$
where:
- $M(t)$ = mass of system at time $t$
- $v$ = velocity of system
- $v_{\text{rel}}$ = velocity of added/removed mass **relative to the system**
- $\sum F_{\text{ext}}$ = external forces on system

**Sign convention:** $dM/dt > 0$ for mass gain, $< 0$ for mass loss.

---

## 2. Rocket Equation (Mass Ejection)

**Setup:** Rocket mass $M$, ejects mass at rate $-\dot{M} = \alpha > 0$ with exhaust speed $u$ relative to rocket.

**Equation:**
$$
M\frac{dv}{dt} = u\alpha + F_{\text{ext}}
$$

### In Free Space ($F_{\text{ext}} = 0$)
$$
v = u\ln\frac{M_0}{M} + v_0
$$
**Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation:**
$$
\Delta v = u\ln\frac{M_0}{M_f}
$$

### With Gravity (Vertical Launch)
$$
M\frac{dv}{dt} = u\alpha - Mg
$$
Substitute $M = M_0 - \alpha t$:
$$
\frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{u\alpha}{M_0 - \alpha t} - g
$$
Integrate:
$$
v = u\ln\frac{M_0}{M} - gt
$$

### With Air Resistance ($F_{\text{drag}} = -kv$ or $-kv^2$)
Solve numerically or with specific drag models.

---

## 3. Rocket with Constant Thrust

Thrust $T = u\alpha = \text{constant}$:
$$
M\frac{dv}{dt} = T - Mg - kv \quad (\text{if linear drag})
$$
This is a linear ODE in $v(t)$ with time-varying $M(t)$.

## 4. Chains / Ropes (Mass Gain/Loss)

### Chain Falling onto Scale / Table
**Setup:** Chain length $L$, mass per unit length $\lambda$, released from rest.

**At time $t$:**
- Length on table: $x = \frac{1}{2}gt^2$
- Mass on table: $m = \lambda x$
- Momentum of falling part: $p = \lambda (L-x) v$ where $v = gt$

**Force on scale = Weight of resting part + Rate of momentum change:**
$$
F = \lambda x g + \lambda v^2 = \lambda x g + \lambda g^2 t^2 = 3\lambda g x
$$
**Reading = 3 × weight of chain on table**

---

### Chain Sliding off Table (Frictionless)
**Setup:** Chain length $L$, initially $x_0$ hanging, rest on smooth table.

**Equation of motion:**
Let $x$ = length hanging at time $t$.
Mass of chain: $M = \lambda L$
Hanging mass: $m = \lambda x$
Weight of hanging part: $\lambda x g$

**System momentum:** $p = \lambda L v$
**External force:** $F_{\text{ext}} = \lambda x g$
**Variable mass equation:**
$$
\lambda L \frac{dv}{dt} = \lambda x g
$$
Using $v = \dot{x}$, $v\frac{dv}{dx} = \frac{x g}{L}$:
$$
v^2 = \frac{g}{L}(x^2 - x_0^2)
$$

---

### Chain Lifted Vertically
**Setup:** Chain length $L$, mass per unit length $\lambda$, lifted at constant speed $v$.

**Force required:**
- Weight of lifted part: $\lambda x g$
- Rate of momentum gain: $\lambda v^2$
- Total: $F = \lambda g x + \lambda v^2$

**Power:** $P = F v = \lambda g x v + \lambda v^3$

---

### Rope Wound on Cylinder / Capstan
**Setup:** Rope unwinding from cylinder of radius $R$, moment of inertia $I$.

**Variables:** $x$ = length unwound, $M$ = total rope mass, $m = M - \lambda x$ = mass still wound.

**Equations:**
- Translation: $(M - \lambda x)\dot{v} = T - (M - \lambda x)g$
- Rotation: $I\alpha = TR$, $a = \alpha R$
- Constraint: $v = \dot{x}$

---

## 5. Raindrops / Hailstones (Mass Accretion)

**Setup:** Drop falls through cloud, gains mass at rate $\dot{m} = \alpha$.

**Assumption:** Accreted mass has zero vertical velocity relative to ground.

**Equation:**
$$
m\frac{dv}{dt} = v_{\text{rel}}\frac{dm}{dt} + mg
$$
Relative velocity of accreted mass: $v_{\text{rel}} = 0 - v = -v$ (drop catches stationary water)
$$
m\frac{dv}{dt} = -v\frac{dm}{dt} + mg
$$
With $m = m_0 + \alpha t$:
$$
\frac{d}{dt}(mv) = mg \implies mv = m_0 v_0 + \int mg\,dt
$$

---

## 6. Avalanches / Landslides

Similar to chain/rocket models but with:
- Entrainment of stationary material
- Complex friction models
- Often treated numerically

---

## 7. Control Volume Approach (Alternative)

For open systems, use Reynolds Transport Theorem:
$$
\sum \vec{F}_{\text{ext}} = \frac{d}{dt}\int_{\text{CV}} \rho \vec{v}\,dV + \int_{\text{CS}} \rho \vec{v}(\vec{v}_{\text{rel}} \cdot \vec{n})\,dA
$$

---

## 8. Summary of Key Equations

| System | Equation |
|--------|----------|
| General variable mass | $M\dot{v} = v_{\text{rel}}\dot{M} + \sum F_{\text{ext}}$ |
| Rocket (free space) | $v = u\ln(M_0/M) + v_0$ |
| Rocket (with gravity) | $v = u\ln(M_0/M) - gt$ |
| Chain falling on scale | $F = 3\lambda g x$ |
| Chain off table | $v^2 = \frac{g}{L}(x^2 - x_0^2)$ |
| Chain lifted at speed $v$ | $F = \lambda g x + \lambda v^2$ |
| Raindrop accretion | $\frac{d}{dt}(mv) = mg$ |

---

## 9. Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| Using $F = ma$ directly | Must use $F = \frac{dp}{dt} = v\frac{dM}{dt} + M\frac{dv}{dt}$ |
| Wrong sign for $v_{\text{rel}}$ | $v_{\text{rel}} = \vec{v}_{\text{added}} - \vec{v}_{\text{system}}$ |
| Ignoring external forces | Always include gravity, drag, etc. |
| Confusing system mass | System = rocket + remaining fuel, not ejected fuel |
