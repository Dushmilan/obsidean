# Work, Energy, Power

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 2.3

---

## 1. Work Done by a Force

### Constant Force
$$
W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{s} = F s \cos\theta
$$
where $\theta$ = angle between $\vec{F}$ and displacement $\vec{s}$.

### Variable Force
$$
W = \int_{\vec{r}_1}^{\vec{r}_2} \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r}
$$
In components: $W = \int F_x\,dx + \int F_y\,dy + \int F_z\,dz$.

### Work-Energy Theorem
$$
W_{\text{net}} = \Delta K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 - \frac{1}{2}mu^2
$$

---

## 2. Work by Specific Forces

| Force | Work Done |
|-------|-----------|
| Constant $\vec{F}$ | $W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{s}$ |
| Weight ($mg$ down) | $W = -mg\Delta h$ |
| Spring ($-kx$) | $W = \frac{1}{2}kx_1^2 - \frac{1}{2}kx_2^2$ |
| Friction ($\mu N$) | $W = -\mu N s$ (path dependent) |
| Normal reaction | $W = 0$ |
| Tension (inextensible string) | $W = 0$ (internal) |

---

## 3. Kinetic Energy
$$
K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{p^2}{2m}
$$

**Work-Energy Theorem (integral form):**
$$
\int \vec{F}_{\text{net}} \cdot d\vec{r} = \Delta K
$$

---

## 4. Potential Energy

### Gravitational (near Earth)
$$
U_g = mgh \quad (\text{reference: } h=0)
$$

### Elastic (Spring)
$$
U_s = \frac{1}{2}kx^2
$$

### General Conservative Force
$$
\vec{F} = -\nabla U = -\left( \frac{\partial U}{\partial x}\hat{i} + \frac{\partial U}{\partial y}\hat{j} + \frac{\partial U}{\partial z}\hat{k} \right)
$$

---

## 5. Conservation of Mechanical Energy

**For conservative forces only:**
$$
E = K + U = \text{constant}
$$
$$
\frac{1}{2}mv^2 + U(\vec{r}) = \frac{1}{2}mu^2 + U(\vec{r}_0)
$$

**With non-conservative forces (friction, air resistance):**
$$
K_f + U_f = K_i + U_i + W_{\text{nc}}
$$
where $W_{\text{nc}} < 0$ for dissipative forces.

---

## 6. Power

### Average Power
$$
P_{\text{avg}} = \frac{W}{\Delta t}
$$

### Instantaneous Power
$$
P = \frac{dW}{dt} = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{v}
$$

**Units:** Watt (W) = J/s. Also horsepower (1 hp = 746 W).

### Power for Constant Force
$$
P = Fv \cos\theta
$$

### Power to Overcome Gravity (Lifting)
$$
P = mgv
$$

### Power for Variable Force
$$
P(t) = \vec{F}(t) \cdot \vec{v}(t)
$$

---

## 7. Conservative vs Non-Conservative Forces

| Property | Conservative | Non-Conservative |
|----------|--------------|------------------|
| Work path | Independent | Dependent |
| Work closed loop | 0 | $\neq 0$ |
| Potential energy | Defined | Not defined |
| Mechanical energy | Conserved | Not conserved |

---

## 8. Key Applications

### Vertical Circular Motion
- String: $v_{\text{bottom}} \geq \sqrt{5gR}$
- Rod: $v_{\text{bottom}} \geq \sqrt{4gR}$

### Escape Velocity
$$
v_{\text{esc}} = \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{R}}
$$

### Terminal Velocity (Linear Drag)
$$
v_T = \frac{mg}{k} \quad \text{for } F_d = -kv
$$

### Terminal Velocity (Quadratic Drag)
$$
v_T = \sqrt{\frac{mg}{k}} \quad \text{for } F_d = -kv^2
$$

### Efficiency
$$
\eta = \frac{\text{Useful output power}}{\text{Input power}} \times 100\%
$$

## 9. Summary of Key Equations

| Quantity | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| Work (constant F) | $W = Fs\cos\theta$ |
| Work (variable F) | $W = \int \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r}$ |
| Work-Energy Theorem | $W_{\text{net}} = \Delta K$ |
| Gravitational PE | $U = mgh$ |
| Elastic PE | $U = \frac{1}{2}kx^2$ |
| Energy Conservation | $K + U = \text{const}$ (conservative) |
| Energy with friction | $K_f + U_f = K_i + U_i + W_{\text{fric}}$ |
| Power | $P = \frac{dW}{dt} = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{v}$ |
| Escape velocity | $v = \sqrt{2GM/R}$ |
| Terminal velocity (linear) | $v_T = mg/k$ |
