# Collisions

**Syllabus:** GCE A/L Combined Mathematics (021) — Paper II, Topic 2.5

---

## 1. Classification of Collisions

| Type | Coefficient of Restitution $e$ | Kinetic Energy |
|------|--------------------------------|----------------|
| Perfectly elastic | $e = 1$ | Conserved |
| Partially elastic | $0 < e < 1$ | Partially lost |
| Perfectly inelastic | $e = 0$ | Maximum loss (bodies coalesce) |
| Superelastic | $e > 1$ | Gains KE (explosions) |

---

## 2. Direct (Head-on) Collisions

**Setup:** Two spheres moving along same line

**Equations:**
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Momentum:}&\quad m_1u_1 + m_2u_2 = m_1v_1 + m_2v_2 \\
\text{Restitution:}&\quad v_2 - v_1 = e(u_1 - u_2)
\end{aligned}
$$

**Solutions:**
$$
\begin{aligned}
v_1 &= \frac{m_1 - em_2}{m_1 + m_2}u_1 + \frac{m_2(1+e)}{m_1 + m_2}u_2 \\
v_2 &= \frac{m_1(1+e)}{m_1 + m_2}u_1 + \frac{m_2 - em_1}{m_1 + m_2}u_2
\end{aligned}
$$

### Special Cases

1. **Equal masses ($m_1 = m_2$):**
   - $e = 1$: $v_1 = u_2$, $v_2 = u_1$ (velocities exchange)
   - $e = 0$: $v_1 = v_2 = \frac{u_1 + u_2}{2}$

2. **Heavy target ($m_2 \gg m_1$, $u_2 = 0$):**
   - $v_1 \approx -eu_1$, $v_2 \approx 0$

3. **Perfectly inelastic ($e = 0$):**
   - $v = \frac{m_1u_1 + m_2u_2}{m_1 + m_2}$ (common velocity)

---

## 3. Kinetic Energy Loss in Direct Collisions

$$
\Delta K = K_f - K_i = -\frac{1}{2}\frac{m_1m_2}{m_1+m_2}(1-e^2)(u_1 - u_2)^2
$$

- $\Delta K \leq 0$ always (KE never increases in closed system)
- Maximum loss when $e = 0$
- Zero loss when $e = 1$

## 4. Oblique Collisions

**Setup:** Smooth spheres, velocities at angles to line of centres.

**Strategy:** Resolve velocities into components:
- **Normal (n):** Along line of centres at impact
- **Tangential (t):** Perpendicular to line of centres

### Equations

**Momentum conserved in both directions:**
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Normal:}&\quad m_1u_{1n} + m_2u_{2n} = m_1v_{1n} + m_2v_{2n} \\
\text{Tangential:}&\quad m_1u_{1t} + m_2u_{2t} = m_1v_{1t} + m_2v_{2t}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Restitution only along normal:**
$$
v_{2n} - v_{1n} = e(u_{1n} - u_{2n})
$$

**Tangential components unchanged (smooth spheres):**
$$
v_{1t} = u_{1t}, \quad v_{2t} = u_{2t}
$$

### Deflection Angle

For sphere 1, angle of deflection $\phi$:
$$
\tan\phi = \frac{v_{1t}}{v_{1n}} = \frac{u_{1t}}{v_{1n}}
$$

## 5. Collision with a Fixed Plane (Wall)

**Setup:** Sphere hits smooth plane at angle $\theta$ to normal.

**Components:**
- Normal: $u_n = u\cos\theta$, $v_n = -eu\cos\theta$
- Tangential: $u_t = u\sin\theta$, $v_t = u\sin\theta$

**Rebound angle $\theta'$:**
$$
\tan\theta' = \frac{v_t}{|v_n|} = \frac{u\sin\theta}{eu\cos\theta} = \frac{\tan\theta}{e}
$$

**Speed after impact:**
$$
v = u\sqrt{\sin^2\theta + e^2\cos^2\theta}
$$

**Impulse on wall:** $I = m(u_n + v_n) = mu\cos\theta(1+e)$

---

## 6. Ballistic Pendulum

**Method:** Measure projectile speed by inelastic collision with pendulum.

1. Projectile ($m$) embeds in block ($M$) → inelastic collision
2. Combined mass swings to height $h$

**Equations:**
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Collision:}&\quad mv = (M+m)V \\
\text{Swing:}&\quad \frac{1}{2}(M+m)V^2 = (M+m)gh \\
\text{Result:}&\quad v = \frac{M+m}{m}\sqrt{2gh}
\end{aligned}
$$

## 7. Successive Collisions

**Scenario:** Three or more particles in line, multiple impacts.

**Method:**
1. Solve first collision (2 particles)
2. Use resulting velocities as initial for next collision
3. Check if particles catch up for further collisions

**Example (3 particles in line):**
- $m_1$ hits $m_2$ → get $v_1, v_2$
- If $v_2 > v_3$, $m_2$ hits $m_3$
- Continue until no more catch-ups

---

## 8. Coefficient of Restitution ($e$)

**Definition:**
$$
e = \frac{\text{relative speed of separation}}{\text{relative speed of approach}} = \frac{v_{\text{sep}}}{u_{\text{app}}}
$$

**Properties:**
- $0 \leq e \leq 1$ for physical collisions
- Depends on materials (e.g., steel on steel: $e \approx 0.9$, putty: $e \approx 0$)
- For smooth spheres, $e$ acts only along line of centres

**Newton's Experimental Law:**
$$
e = -\frac{v_2 - v_1}{u_2 - u_1} \quad (\text{direct collision})
$$

---

## 9. Impulse in Collisions

**Impulse on particle 1:**
$$
I_1 = \int F\,dt = m_1(v_1 - u_1) = \frac{m_1m_2(1+e)}{m_1+m_2}(u_1 - u_2)
$$

**Total impulse on system = 0** (internal forces)

---

## 10. Special Cases

### Small Mass Hits Large Mass ($m_1 \ll m_2$)
$$
v_1 \approx -eu_1 + (1+e)u_2, \quad v_2 \approx u_2
$$

### Large Mass Hits Small Mass ($m_1 \gg m_2$)
$$
v_1 \approx u_1, \quad v_2 \approx 2u_1 - u_2 \quad (\text{for } e=1)
$$

### Perfectly Elastic + Equal Masses
- Velocities exchange
- Directions reverse if one initially at rest

---

## 11. 2D Collision of Non-Spherical Bodies

For smooth bodies, same principles apply but:
- Line of centres = common normal at contact point
- Tangential = tangent plane
- No friction → no tangential impulse

---

## 12. Summary of Key Formulae

| Quantity | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| Momentum conservation | $m_1\vec{u}_1 + m_2\vec{u}_2 = m_1\vec{v}_1 + m_2\vec{v}_2$ |
| Restitution (direct) | $v_2 - v_1 = e(u_1 - u_2)$ |
| Restitution (oblique) | $v_{2n} - v_{1n} = e(u_{1n} - u_{2n})$ |
| Direct collision $v_1$ | $\frac{m_1 - em_2}{m_1+m_2}u_1 + \frac{m_2(1+e)}{m_1+m_2}u_2$ |
| KE loss (direct) | $-\frac{1}{2}\frac{m_1m_2}{m_1+m_2}(1-e^2)(u_1-u_2)^2$ |
| Wall collision rebound angle | $\tan\theta' = \frac{\tan\theta}{e}$ |
| Ballistic pendulum | $v = \frac{M+m}{m}\sqrt{2gh}$ |
| Impulse (direct) | $\frac{m_1m_2(1+e)}{m_1+m_2}(u_1 - u_2)$ |
