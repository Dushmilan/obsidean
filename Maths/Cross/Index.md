## 📝 Notation Glossary

| Symbol | Meaning | Context |
|--------|---------|---------|
| $\vec{r}, \vec{v}, \vec{a}$ | Position, velocity, acceleration vectors | Kinematics, Dynamics |
| $\hat{i}, \hat{j}, \hat{k}$ | Unit vectors (Cartesian) | Vectors, Physics |
| $\vec{r} \cdot \vec{s}$ | Scalar (dot) product | Vectors, Work |
| $\vec{r} \times \vec{s}$ | Vector (cross) product | Vectors, Torque, Angular momentum |
| $[\vec{a}\ \vec{b}\ \vec{c}]$ | Scalar triple product | Vectors, Volume |
| $(\vec{a}\ \vec{b}\ \vec{c})$ | Vector triple product | Vectors |
| $\ddot{x}$ | Second time derivative (acceleration) | Dynamics, SHM |
| $\omega$ | Angular frequency / velocity | Circular motion, SHM |
| $I$ | Moment of inertia | Rotational dynamics |
| $\vec{L}$ | Angular momentum | Rotational dynamics |
| $\tau$ | Torque | Rotational dynamics |
| $T$ | Period | SHM, Circular motion |
| $A$ | Amplitude | SHM, Waves |
| $e$ | Coefficient of restitution | Collisions |
| $\mu$ | Coefficient of friction | Statics, Dynamics |
| $\vec{G}$ | Centre of mass position | Statics |
| $W$ | Work done | Dynamics, Energy |
| $P$ | Power | Dynamics |
| $U$ | Potential energy | Energy conservation |
| $K$ | Kinetic energy | Energy conservation |
