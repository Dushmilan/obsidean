# 05.4 Triple Products

## Definition

### Scalar Triple Product
$$[\mathbf{a} \; \mathbf{b} \; \mathbf{c}] = \mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) = \begin{vmatrix} a_1 & a_2 & a_3 \\ b_1 & b_2 & b_3 \\ c_1 & c_2 & c_3 \end{vmatrix}$$

### Vector Triple Product
$$\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$$

## Formulae

| Formula | Expression |
|---------|------------|
| **Scalar triple** | $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$ |
| **Vector triple** | $\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$ |
| **BAC-CAB** | $\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) = (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c})\mathbf{b} - (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b})\mathbf{c}$ |
| **Volume parallelepiped** | $|\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})|$ |
| **Volume tetrahedron** | $\frac{1}{6}|(\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a}) \cdot ((\mathbf{c}-\mathbf{a}) \times (\mathbf{d}-\mathbf{a}))|$ |
| **Coplanarity** | $[\mathbf{a} \; \mathbf{b} \; \mathbf{c}] = 0$ |

## Properties / Key Concepts

### Scalar Triple Product

| Property | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| **Cyclic permutation** | $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) = \mathbf{b} \cdot (\mathbf{c} \times \mathbf{a}) = \mathbf{c} \cdot (\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b})$ |
| **Anti-cyclic** | $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) = -\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{c} \times \mathbf{b})$ |
| **Dot/cross interchange** | $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) = (\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}) \cdot \mathbf{c}$ |

- Geometric meaning: volume of parallelepiped with edges $\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}, \mathbf{c}$
- Coplanarity test: three vectors are coplanar iff $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) = 0$

### Vector Triple Product
- BAC-CAB identity: $\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) = (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c})\mathbf{b} - (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b})\mathbf{c}$
- Other form: $(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}) \times \mathbf{c} = (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c})\mathbf{b} - (\mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{c})\mathbf{a}$
- Not associative: $(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}) \times \mathbf{c} \neq \mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$

### Four-Vector Products
- $(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}) \cdot (\mathbf{c} \times \mathbf{d}) = (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c})(\mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{d}) - (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{d})(\mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{c})$
- $(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}) \times (\mathbf{c} \times \mathbf{d}) = [\mathbf{a} \; \mathbf{b} \; \mathbf{d}]\mathbf{c} - [\mathbf{a} \; \mathbf{b} \; \mathbf{c}]\mathbf{d}$

### Reciprocal Vectors
Given non-coplanar $\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}, \mathbf{c}$:
$$\mathbf{a}' = \frac{\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}}{\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})}, \quad \mathbf{b}' = \frac{\mathbf{c} \times \mathbf{a}}{\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})}, \quad \mathbf{c}' = \frac{\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}}{\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})}$$
Then $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{a}' = \mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{b}' = \mathbf{c} \cdot \mathbf{c}' = 1$ and $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}' = \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c}' = \dots = 0$.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Scalar Triple Product
$\mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{c} = 3\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k}$

$$[\mathbf{a} \; \mathbf{b} \; \mathbf{c}] = \begin{vmatrix} 2 & -1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & -1 \\ 3 & -1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 2(4-1) + 1(2+3) + 1(-1-6) = 6 + 5 - 7 = 4$$

Volume $= 4$ cubic units.

### Example 2: Coplanarity
$\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = 2\mathbf{i} + 3\mathbf{j} + 4\mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{c} = 3\mathbf{i} + 4\mathbf{j} + 5\mathbf{k}$

$$[\mathbf{a} \; \mathbf{b} \; \mathbf{c}] = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 3 & 4 \\ 3 & 4 & 5 \end{vmatrix} = 1(15-16) - 1(10-12) + 1(8-9) = -1 + 2 - 1 = 0$$

Vectors are coplanar.

### Example 3: BAC-CAB Identity
$\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{c} = \mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}$

$\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c} = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ 2 & -1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = \mathbf{i}(-1-1) - \mathbf{j}(2-1) + \mathbf{k}(2+1) = -2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}$

$\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ 1 & 2 & -1 \\ -2 & -1 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = \mathbf{i}(6-1) - \mathbf{j}(3-2) + \mathbf{k}(-1+4) = 5\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}$

Using identity:
$\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c} = 1+2-1 = 2$, $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 2-2-1 = -1$
$= 2\mathbf{b} - (-1)\mathbf{c} = 2(2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}) + (\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}) = 5\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}$ ✓

### Example 4: Tetrahedron Volume
$A(0,0,0)$, $B(1,0,0)$, $C(0,1,0)$, $D(0,0,1)$

$\overrightarrow{AB} = \mathbf{i}$, $\overrightarrow{AC} = \mathbf{j}$, $\overrightarrow{AD} = \mathbf{k}$
$\mathbf{i} \cdot (\mathbf{j} \times \mathbf{k}) = \mathbf{i} \cdot \mathbf{i} = 1$

Volume $= \frac{1}{6}|1| = \frac{1}{6}$

### Pattern 1: Scalar Triple Product
**Given:** $\mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{c} = 3\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k}$
**Find:** $[\mathbf{a} \; \mathbf{b} \; \mathbf{c}]$, volume of parallelepiped

### Pattern 2: Coplanarity
**Given:** $\mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i} + \lambda\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{c} = 3\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}$
**Find:** $\lambda$ such that vectors are coplanar

### Pattern 3: Vector Triple Product (BAC-CAB)
**Given:** $\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{c} = \mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}$
**Find:** $\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$

### Pattern 4: Volume of Tetrahedron
**Given:** Vertices $A(1,0,0)$, $B(0,1,0)$, $C(0,0,1)$, $D(1,1,1)$
**Find:** Volume

## Common Traps

| Trap | Wrong | Correct |
|------|-------|---------|
| Scalar triple product | $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}$ ambiguous | Always $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$ |
| Coplanarity | $[\mathbf{a} \; \mathbf{b} \; \mathbf{c}] \neq 0$ | $[\mathbf{a} \; \mathbf{b} \; \mathbf{c}] = 0$ |
| BAC-CAB | $(\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b})\mathbf{c} - (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c})\mathbf{b}$ | $(\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c})\mathbf{b} - (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b})\mathbf{c}$ |
| Associativity | $(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}) \times \mathbf{c} = \mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$ | Not associative! |
| Tetrahedron volume | Use parallelepiped volume | Divide by 6 |
| Volume sign | Forget absolute value | Volume = $\|[\dots]\|$ |
