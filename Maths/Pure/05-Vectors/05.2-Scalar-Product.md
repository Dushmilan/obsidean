# 05.2 Scalar Product (Dot Product)

## Definition

$$\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = \|\mathbf{a}\|\|\mathbf{b}\|\cos\theta$$
where $\theta$ is the angle between $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$ ($0 \le \theta \le \pi$).

### Component Form
$$\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = a_1b_1 + a_2b_2 + a_3b_3$$

## Formulae

| Formula | Expression |
|---------|------------|
| **Dot product** | $a_1b_1 + a_2b_2 + a_3b_3$ |
| **Angle** | $\cos\theta = \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{a}\|\|\mathbf{b}\|}$ |
| **Scalar projection** | $\frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{b}\|}$ |
| **Vector projection** | $\frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{b}\|^2}\mathbf{b}$ |
| **Perpendicular component** | $\mathbf{a}_\perp = \mathbf{a} - \text{proj}_{\mathbf{b}}\mathbf{a}$ |
| **Work** | $W = \mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{d}$ |
| **Direction cosines** | $\frac{a_i}{\|\mathbf{a}\|}$, sum of squares = 1 |

## Properties / Key Concepts

### Algebraic Properties

| Property | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| **Commutative** | $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{a}$ |
| **Distributive** | $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}) = \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c}$ |
| **Scalar multiplication** | $(k\mathbf{a}) \cdot \mathbf{b} = k(\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b})$ |
| **Self product** | $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{a} = \|\mathbf{a}\|^2$ |
| **Orthogonality** | $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 0 \iff \mathbf{a} \perp \mathbf{b}$ |

### Angle Between Vectors
$$\theta = \cos^{-1}\left(\frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{a}\|\|\mathbf{b}\|}\right)$$

### Projection
- Scalar projection: $\text{comp}_{\mathbf{b}}\mathbf{a} = \|\mathbf{a}\|\cos\theta = \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{b}\|}$
- Vector projection: $\text{proj}_{\mathbf{b}}\mathbf{a} = \left(\frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{b}\|^2}\right)\mathbf{b}$
- Perpendicular component: $\mathbf{a}_\perp = \mathbf{a} - \text{proj}_{\mathbf{b}}\mathbf{a}$

### Direction Cosines
For $\mathbf{a} = a_1\mathbf{i} + a_2\mathbf{j} + a_3\mathbf{k}$:
$$\cos\alpha = \frac{a_1}{\|\mathbf{a}\|}, \quad \cos\beta = \frac{a_2}{\|\mathbf{a}\|}, \quad \cos\gamma = \frac{a_3}{\|\mathbf{a}\|}$$
$$\cos^2\alpha + \cos^2\beta + \cos^2\gamma = 1$$

### Applications
- Work done: $W = \mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{d} = \|\mathbf{F}\|\|\mathbf{d}\|\cos\theta$
- Angle between lines with direction vectors $\mathbf{b}_1, \mathbf{b}_2$: $\cos\theta = \frac{|\mathbf{b}_1 \cdot \mathbf{b}_2|}{\|\mathbf{b}_1\|\|\mathbf{b}_2\|}$
- Orthogonality test: $\mathbf{a} \perp \mathbf{b} \iff \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 0$

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Angle
$\mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = 4\mathbf{i} + 4\mathbf{j} - 2\mathbf{k}$

$\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 8 - 4 - 4 = 0$
$\|\mathbf{a}\| = 3$, $\|\mathbf{b}\| = \sqrt{16+16+4} = 6$
$\cos\theta = 0 \Rightarrow \theta = 90^\circ$

### Example 2: Projection
$\mathbf{a} = 3\mathbf{i} + 4\mathbf{j} - 2\mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}$

$\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 6 - 4 - 2 = 0$
$\|\mathbf{b}\|^2 = 4 + 1 + 1 = 6$
$\text{proj}_{\mathbf{b}}\mathbf{a} = \frac{0}{6}\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{0}$
Vectors are perpendicular.

### Example 3: Orthogonal Condition
$\mathbf{a} = \lambda\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{i} - 4\mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k}$

$\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = \lambda - 8 + 6 = 0 \Rightarrow \lambda = 2$

### Pattern 1: Find Angle
**Given:** $\mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}$
**Find:** $\theta$ between $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$

### Pattern 2: Projection
**Given:** $\mathbf{a} = 3\mathbf{i} + 4\mathbf{j} - 2\mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}$
**Find:** $\text{proj}_{\mathbf{b}}\mathbf{a}$ and component perpendicular to $\mathbf{b}$

### Pattern 3: Orthogonal Condition
**Given:** $\mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i} + \lambda\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{i} - 2\mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}$
**Find:** $\lambda$ such that $\mathbf{a} \perp \mathbf{b}$

### Pattern 4: Direction Cosines
**Given:** $\mathbf{a} = 3\mathbf{i} - 4\mathbf{j} + 12\mathbf{k}$
**Find:** Direction cosines and angles with axes

## Common Traps

| Trap | Wrong | Correct |
|------|-------|---------|
| Dot product | Multiply magnitudes | Sum of products of components |
| Angle | Use $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = \|\mathbf{a}\|\|\mathbf{b}\|\sin\theta$ | Use $\cos\theta$ |
| Projection | $\frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{b}\|}$ (scalar) | Vector projection needs $\frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{b}\|^2}\mathbf{b}$ |
| Direction cosines | Forget to normalise | Divide components by magnitude |
| Orthogonal | $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 1$ | $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 0$ |
