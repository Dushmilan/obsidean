# 05.3 Vector Product (Cross Product)

## Definition

$$\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \|\mathbf{a}\|\|\mathbf{b}\|\sin\theta \, \hat{\mathbf{n}}$$
where $\theta$ is angle between $\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}$, and $\hat{\mathbf{n}}$ is unit vector perpendicular to both, following right-hand rule.

### Component Form
$$\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ a_1 & a_2 & a_3 \\ b_1 & b_2 & b_3 \end{vmatrix} = (a_2b_3 - a_3b_2)\mathbf{i} + (a_3b_1 - a_1b_3)\mathbf{j} + (a_1b_2 - a_2b_1)\mathbf{k}$$

## Formulae

| Formula | Expression |
|---------|------------|
| **Cross product** | $(a_2b_3-a_3b_2)\mathbf{i} + (a_3b_1-a_1b_3)\mathbf{j} + (a_1b_2-a_2b_1)\mathbf{k}$ |
| **Magnitude** | $\|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}\| = \|\mathbf{a}\|\|\mathbf{b}\|\sin\theta$ |
| **Area parallelogram** | $\|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}\|$ |
| **Area triangle** | $\frac{1}{2}\|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}\|$ |
| **Moment** | $\mathbf{M} = \mathbf{r} \times \mathbf{F}$ |
| **Perpendicular unit** | $\frac{\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}\|}$ |
| **Parallel condition** | $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{0} \iff \mathbf{a} \parallel \mathbf{b}$ |

## Properties / Key Concepts

### Algebraic Properties

| Property | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| **Anti-commutative** | $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = -(\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{a})$ |
| **Distributive** | $\mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}) = \mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{c}$ |
| **Scalar multiplication** | $(k\mathbf{a}) \times \mathbf{b} = k(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}) = \mathbf{a} \times (k\mathbf{b})$ |
| **Parallel vectors** | $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{0} \iff \mathbf{a} \parallel \mathbf{b}$ |
| **Self product** | $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{a} = \mathbf{0}$ |

### Geometric Interpretation
- $\|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}\|$ = Area of parallelogram formed by $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$
- $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}$ is perpendicular to both $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$
- Right-hand rule: fingers curl from $\mathbf{a}$ to $\mathbf{b}$, thumb gives direction

### Applications
- Area of triangle with vertices $\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}, \mathbf{c}$: $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}\|(\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}) \times (\mathbf{c} - \mathbf{a})\|$
- Moment of force $\mathbf{F}$ at point $\mathbf{r}$: $\mathbf{M} = \mathbf{r} \times \mathbf{F}$
- Unit vector perpendicular: $\hat{\mathbf{n}} = \frac{\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}\|}$

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Cross Product
$\mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}$

$$\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ 2 & -1 & 3 \\ 1 & 2 & -1 \end{vmatrix} = \mathbf{i}(1-6) - \mathbf{j}(-2-3) + \mathbf{k}(4+1) = -5\mathbf{i} + 5\mathbf{j} + 5\mathbf{k}$$

$\|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}\| = 5\sqrt{3}$

### Example 2: Area of Triangle
$A(1,0,0)$, $B(0,1,0)$, $C(0,0,1)$

$\overrightarrow{AB} = -\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j}$, $\overrightarrow{AC} = -\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{k}$
$\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC} = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ -1 & 1 & 0 \\ -1 & 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = \mathbf{i}(1) - \mathbf{j}(-1) + \mathbf{k}(1) = \mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}$

Area $= \frac{1}{2}\|\mathbf{i}+\mathbf{j}+\mathbf{k}\| = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$

### Example 3: Perpendicular Unit Vector
$\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}$

$\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ 1 & 2 & -1 \\ 2 & -1 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = \mathbf{i}(6-1) - \mathbf{j}(3+2) + \mathbf{k}(-1-4) = 5\mathbf{i} - 5\mathbf{j} - 5\mathbf{k}$

$\|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}\| = 5\sqrt{3}$

Unit vector: $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}(\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k})$

### Pattern 1: Compute Cross Product
**Given:** $\mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}$
**Find:** $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}$, $\|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}\|$

### Pattern 2: Area of Parallelogram/Triangle
**Given:** Points $A(1,2,3)$, $B(4,5,6)$, $C(7,8,9)$
**Find:** Area of $\triangle ABC$

### Pattern 3: Perpendicular Vector
**Given:** $\mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + \mathbf{k}$, $\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}$
**Find:** Unit vector $\perp$ to both $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$

### Pattern 4: Moment
**Given:** Force $\mathbf{F} = 3\mathbf{i} + 4\mathbf{j} - 2\mathbf{k}$ acts at point $(1,2,3)$
**Find:** Moment about origin

## Common Traps

| Trap | Wrong | Correct |
|------|-------|---------|
| Cross product | $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{a}$ | $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = -(\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{a})$ |
| Determinant | Forget alternating signs | $\mathbf{i}(a_2b_3-a_3b_2) - \mathbf{j}(a_1b_3-a_3b_1) + \mathbf{k}(a_1b_2-a_2b_1)$ |
| Area | Use $\|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}\|$ for triangle | Triangle = $\frac{1}{2}\|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}\|$ |
| Direction | Use wrong hand rule | Right-hand rule: $\mathbf{a} \to \mathbf{b}$ curl, thumb gives direction |
| Zero product | $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{0} \implies \mathbf{a} \perp \mathbf{b}$ | $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{0} \iff \mathbf{a} \parallel \mathbf{b}$ |
