A circle is the locus of all points at a fixed distance (radius) from a fixed point (centre). The standard equation $(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2$ encodes this directly -- it says "the distance from $(x,y)$ to $(a,b)$ is always $r$." The general form $x^2+y^2+2gx+2fy+c=0$ is just the expanded version, with centre $(-g,-f)$ and radius $\sqrt{g^2+f^2-c}$.

**The Intuition:** A circle is a dog on a leash. The leash length is the radius $r$. The point where you hold the leash is the centre $(a,b)$. The dog can reach every point exactly $r$ away, tracing out the circle. The equation $(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2$ is just the distance formula squared: no new geometry, just Pythagoras in disguise.

**The Math:** Standard form: $(x-a)^2 + (y-b)^2 = r^2$

General form: $x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$, centre $(-g, -f)$, radius $r = \sqrt{g^2 + f^2 - c}$

Parametric: $x = a + r\cos\theta,\; y = b + r\sin\theta$

Tangent at $(x_1,y_1)$ on $x^2+y^2=r^2$: $xx_1 + yy_1 = r^2$

Tangent with slope $m$: $y = mx \pm r\sqrt{1+m^2}$

Normal at $(x_1,y_1)$: passes through centre, perpendicular to tangent

Orthogonal circles: $2g_1g_2 + 2f_1f_2 = c_1 + c_2$

Radical axis: $S_1 - S_2 = 0$

**What does this mean for Pure Mathematics?** The tangent, normal, and chord equations follow a pattern that repeats for every conic section. Master the circle versions first, and the parabola, ellipse, and hyperbola versions will feel like variations on a theme.

### Worked Examples

**Setup:** $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 4y - 12 = 0$. Find centre and radius.

**Solution:**
1. Complete the square: $(x^2-6x+9) + (y^2+4y+4) = 12+9+4$
2. $(x-3)^2 + (y+2)^2 = 25$

**Key insight:** Centre $(3, -2)$, radius $5$. Group $x$ and $y$ terms separately, then complete the square.

---

**Setup:** Circle $x^2 + y^2 = 16$, slope $m = \frac{3}{4}$. Find tangent equations.

**Solution:**
1. $y = \frac{3}{4}x \pm 4\sqrt{1+\frac{9}{16}} = \frac{3}{4}x \pm 5$
2. $3x - 4y \pm 20 = 0$

**Key insight:** The $\pm$ gives two tangents, both with the same slope but touching opposite sides of the circle.
