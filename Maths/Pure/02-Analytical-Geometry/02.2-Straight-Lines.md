A straight line is the set of all points satisfying $Ax + By + C = 0$, described by its gradient and intercepts. Lines are the simplest curves and the basis for understanding gradients, angles, distances, and eventually tangents to all conics. The five standard forms are not different types of line -- they are different ways of specifying the same line depending on what information you are given.

**The Intuition:** A line is like a road. The gradient is the slope of the road. The y-intercept is where the road crosses the north-south highway. Parallel roads never meet; perpendicular roads cross at right angles. The gradient $m$ is the "rise over run" -- for every 1 unit you move right, you move $m$ units up.

**The Math:** Gradient: $m = \frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1} = \tan\theta$

Angle between lines: $\tan\theta = \left|\frac{m_1-m_2}{1+m_1m_2}\right|$

Point-to-line distance: $d = \frac{|Ax_1+By_1+C|}{\sqrt{A^2+B^2}}$

Parallel lines distance: $d = \frac{|C_1-C_2|}{\sqrt{A^2+B^2}}$

Parallel: $m_1 = m_2$ or $\frac{A_1}{A_2}=\frac{B_1}{B_2}$

Perpendicular: $m_1m_2 = -1$ or $A_1A_2+B_1B_2=0$

Family through intersection: $L_1 + \lambda L_2 = 0$

Standard forms: slope-intercept $y = mx + c$, point-slope $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$, intercept $\frac{x}{a} + \frac{y}{b} = 1$, normal $x\cos\alpha + y\sin\alpha = p$.

**What does this mean for Pure Mathematics?** Lines interact in structured ways: parallel lines share a gradient, perpendicular lines have gradients that multiply to $-1$, and families of lines through a point give parametric control over line bundles. The family $L_1+\lambda L_2=0$ misses one line ($L_2=0$ itself).

### Worked Examples

**Setup:** Through $(2, -3)$, gradient $4$. Find the equation.

**Solution:**
1. $y - (-3) = 4(x - 2)$
2. $y + 3 = 4x - 8 \Rightarrow 4x - y - 11 = 0$

**Key insight:** Point-slope form is the fastest when you have a point and gradient.

---

**Setup:** $P(1, -2)$ to $3x - 4y + 12 = 0$. Find the perpendicular distance.

**Solution:**
1. $d = \frac{|3(1) - 4(-2) + 12|}{\sqrt{9+16}} = \frac{|3+8+12|}{5} = \frac{23}{5}$

**Key insight:** Substitute carefully. The absolute value ensures a positive distance.
