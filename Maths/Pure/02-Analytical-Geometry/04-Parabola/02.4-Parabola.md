
## Definition

A parabola is the locus of points **equidistant from a fixed point (focus) and a fixed line (directrix)**, with eccentricity $e = 1$. Standard form (vertex at origin, opens right):

$$y^2 = 4ax$$

- Focus $(a, 0)$, directrix $x = -a$, latus rectum $4a$
- Parametric point: $(at^2, 2at)$, where $t$ is the tangent's gradient reciprocal

## The Intuition

The shape a water jet makes under gravity. The focus is a "magnetic point": parallel rays reflecting off the curve converge there (and conversely, rays from the focus reflect parallel to the axis — satellite dishes and headlights). The equidistant condition forces the curve to bend away from the directrix toward the focus.

## The Toolkit

For $y^2 = 4ax$:

| Result | Formula |
|--------|---------|
| Parametric point | $(at^2,\ 2at)$, $t = 1/m$ |
| Tangent at $t$ | $ty = x + at^2$ |
| Tangent at $(x_1,y_1)$ | $yy_1 = 2a(x+x_1)$ |
| Tangent, slope $m$ | $y = mx + \dfrac{a}{m}$ |
| Normal at $t$ | $y = -tx + 2at + at^3$ |
| Normal, slope $m$ | $y = mx - 2am - am^3$ |
| Focal chord condition | $t_1 t_2 = -1$ |
| Focal chord length | $a(t_1 - t_2)^2$ |
| Subnormal | $2a$ (constant at every point) |
| Latus rectum | $4a$ |

## Derivation

Equidistance: $(x-a)^2 + y^2 = (x+a)^2$ (distance to focus $=$ distance to directrix) expands to $y^2 = 4ax$. Tangents: differentiate implicitly $2y\frac{dy}{dx} = 4a$, giving slope $m = 2a/y$; at the parametric point this yields $ty = x + at^2$. Normal is the negative-reciprocal-slope line. [Full derivations: 02.4-Parabola-Proof]

## Method

1. **Identify $a$** — for $y^2 = 4ax$, the coefficient of $x$ is $4a$.
2. **Tangent/normal at a point:** use the parametric form $t$ — one formula per case.
3. **Tangent of given slope:** $y = mx + a/m$ — one tangent per slope (unlike circle's two).
4. **Focal chords:** points are paired by $t_1t_2 = -1$.
5. **Normals from a point:** set up the cubic in $t$; the number of real roots = number of normals.

## Worked Examples

**Setup:** $y^2 = 12x$ ($a = 3$). Tangent with $m = 2$.

**Solution:** $y = 2x + \frac{3}{2}$, i.e. $4x - 2y + 3 = 0$.

**Key insight:** $y = mx + \frac{a}{m}$ gives exactly one tangent per slope for the parabola.

---

**Setup:** $y^2 = 8x$ ($a = 2$). Normal at $t = 2$.

**Solution:** $y = -2x + 2(2)(2) + 2(8) = -2x + 24$.

**Key insight:** The normal slope is the negative reciprocal of the tangent slope at that point.

---

**Setup:** From $P(10, 4)$ to $y^2 = 4x$ ($a=1$), find the normals.

**Solution:** Cubic: $t^3 + (2-10)t - 4 = t^3 - 8t - 4 = 0$. Three real roots: $t = -2,\ 1+\sqrt{2},\ 1-\sqrt{2}$.

**Key insight:** The number of real roots of the cubic determines how many normals can be drawn.

## Common Traps

- Confusing focus/directrix signs: $y^2 = 4ax$ → focus $(a,0)$, directrix $x = -a$
- $y^2 = -4ax$ or $x^2 = 4ay$ variants — swap roles; re-identify $a$ before using formulas
- The tangent formula needs the point **on** the parabola
- Focal chord: $t_1t_2 = -1$, not $1$
- Normals give a cubic — don't expect one "the" normal
