A parabola is the set of all points equidistant from a fixed point (focus) and a fixed line (directrix), with eccentricity $e = 1$. It is the simplest non-circular conic -- one focus, one directrix, open to infinity in one direction. The parameter $t$ is the key to the parabola's elegant algebra, giving clean formulae for tangent, normal, and chord.

**The Intuition:** A parabola is the shape a water jet makes under gravity. The focus is like a "magnetic point" that all parallel rays converge to after bouncing off the curve. Every point on the curve is equidistant from the focus and the directrix. The equidistant condition forces the curve to bend away from the directrix toward the focus.

**The Math:** For $y^2 = 4ax$ (vertex at origin, opens right):

Parametric: $(at^2, 2at)$ where $t = 1/m$ ($m$ = tangent gradient)

Tangent at $t$: $ty = x + at^2$; at $(x_1,y_1)$: $yy_1 = 2a(x+x_1)$; with slope $m$: $y = mx + a/m$

Normal at $t$: $y = -tx + 2at + at^3$; with slope $m$: $y = mx - 2am - am^3$

Focal chord condition: $t_1t_2 = -1$; length $= a(t_1 - t_2)^2$

Subnormal: $2a$ (constant for any point on the parabola)

Latus rectum: length $4a$, through focus perpendicular to axis

**What does this mean for Pure Mathematics?** The reflection property (rays from the focus reflect parallel to the axis) is why parabolas appear in satellite dishes and headlights. The normal cubic for three normals from a point introduces a deeper structure connecting to the geometry of the curve.

### Worked Examples

**Setup:** $y^2 = 12x$ ($a = 3$). Tangent with $m = 2$.

**Solution:**
1. $y = 2x + \frac{3}{2}$ or $4x - 2y + 3 = 0$

**Key insight:** The formula $y=mx+\frac{a}{m}$ gives one tangent per slope for the parabola.

---

**Setup:** $y^2 = 8x$ ($a = 2$). Normal at $t = 2$.

**Solution:**
1. $y = -2x + 2(2)(2) + 2(8) = -2x + 24$

**Key insight:** The normal slope is the negative reciprocal of the tangent slope at that point.

---

**Setup:** From $P(10, 4)$ to $y^2 = 4x$ ($a=1$). Find the normals.

**Solution:**
1. Cubic: $t^3 + (2-10)t - 4 = t^3 - 8t - 4 = 0$
2. Three real roots: $t = -2, 1+\sqrt{2}, 1-\sqrt{2}$

**Key insight:** The number of real roots of the cubic determines how many normals can be drawn.
