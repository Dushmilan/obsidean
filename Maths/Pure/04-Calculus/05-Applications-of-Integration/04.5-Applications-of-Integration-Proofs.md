### Vertical Slices (Riemann Sum Derivation)
Partition $[a,b]$ into $n$ subintervals of width $\Delta x$. On $i$-th subinterval $[x_{i-1}, x_i]$, choose sample point $x_i^*$.

Area of approximating rectangle:
$$\Delta A_i \approx [f(x_i^*) - g(x_i^*)] \Delta x$$

Riemann sum:
$$A \approx \sum_{i=1}^n [f(x_i^*) - g(x_i^*)] \Delta x$$

Taking limit as $n \to \infty$:
$$A = \lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{i=1}^n [f(x_i^*) - g(x_i^*)] \Delta x = \int_a^b [f(x) - g(x)] \, dx$$

### Horizontal Slices
Similarly, for $x = f(y) \ge x = g(y)$ on $[c,d]$:
$$A = \int_c^d [f(y) - g(y)] \, dy$$

### Disc Method (Riemann Sum Derivation)
Revolve $y = f(x) \ge 0$ on $[a,b]$ about x-axis.

Slice into $n$ discs of thickness $\Delta x$. $i$-th disc radius $\approx f(x_i^*)$, volume:
$$\Delta V_i \approx \pi [f(x_i^*)]^2 \Delta x$$

Riemann sum $\to$ integral:
$$V = \lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{i=1}^n \pi [f(x_i^*)]^2 \Delta x = \pi \int_a^b [f(x)]^2 \, dx$$

### Washer Method
Region between $f(x) \ge g(x) \ge 0$, revolved about x-axis:
$$\Delta V_i \approx \pi [f(x_i^*)^2 - g(x_i^*)^2] \Delta x$$
$$V = \pi \int_a^b [f(x)^2 - g(x)^2] \, dx$$

### Cylindrical Shell Method
Revolve $y = f(x) \ge 0$ on $[a,b]$ about y-axis.

$i$-th shell: radius $x_i^*$, height $f(x_i^*)$, thickness $\Delta x$.
$$\Delta V_i \approx 2\pi x_i^* f(x_i^*) \Delta x$$

$$V = \lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{i=1}^n 2\pi x_i^* f(x_i^*) \Delta x = 2\pi \int_a^b x f(x) \, dx$$

**Derivation alternative:** $V = \int A(y) \, dy$ where $A(y)$ is cross-sectional area perpendicular to y-axis.

### Cartesian (y = f(x))
Partition $[a,b]$, $x_i^*$ sample point. Chord length on $[x_{i-1}, x_i]$:
$$\Delta L_i \approx \sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2} = \sqrt{1 + \left(\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}\right)^2} \Delta x$$

By MVT, $\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = f'(c_i)$ for some $c_i \in [x_{i-1}, x_i]$.
$$\Delta L_i \approx \sqrt{1 + [f'(c_i)]^2} \Delta x$$

Riemann sum $\to$ integral:
$$L = \lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{i=1}^n \sqrt{1 + [f'(c_i)]^2} \Delta x = \int_a^b \sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2} \, dx$$

### Parametric (x = x(t), y = y(t))
$$\Delta L_i \approx \sqrt{[x'(t_i^*)]^2 + [y'(t_i^*)]^2} \Delta t$$
$$L = \int_\alpha^\beta \sqrt{[x'(t)]^2 + [y'(t)]^2} \, dt$$

### Polar (r = f(Î¸))
Using $x = r\cos\theta, y = r\sin\theta$:
$$\frac{dx}{d\theta} = r'\cos\theta - r\sin\theta, \quad \frac{dy}{d\theta} = r'\sin\theta + r\cos\theta$$
$$\left(\frac{dx}{d\theta}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{dy}{d\theta}\right)^2 = r'^2 + r^2$$
$$L = \int_\alpha^\beta \sqrt{r^2 + \left(\frac{dr}{d\theta}\right)^2} \, d\theta$$

### About x-axis (y = f(x) â‰¥ 0)
Frustum of cone on $[x_{i-1}, x_i]$:
- Radius at ends: $f(x_{i-1}), f(x_i)$
- Slant height: $\Delta s_i = \sqrt{1 + [f'(c_i)]^2} \Delta x$

Surface area of frustum:
$$\Delta S_i \approx 2\pi f(x_i^*) \Delta s_i = 2\pi f(x_i^*) \sqrt{1 + [f'(c_i)]^2} \Delta x$$

Taking limit:
$$S = \int_a^b 2\pi f(x) \sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2} \, dx$$

### About y-axis
$$S = \int_a^b 2\pi x \sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2} \, dx$$

### Definition
Work done by force $F(x)$ moving object from $a$ to $b$:
$$W = \lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{i=1}^n F(x_i^*) \Delta x = \int_a^b F(x) \, dx$$

### Hooke's Law Derivation
Spring force: $F = kx$ (restoring force magnitude).
Work to stretch from 0 to $x$:
$$W = \int_0^x kx' \, dx' = \frac{1}{2} kx^2$$

### Centroid of Plane Region (Riemann Sum)
Region bounded by $y = f(x) \ge g(x)$ on $[a,b]$.

Partition into vertical strips. $i$-th strip:
- Area: $\Delta A_i \approx [f(x_i^*) - g(x_i^*)] \Delta x$
- Moment about y-axis: $\Delta M_{y,i} \approx x_i^* [f(x_i^*) - g(x_i^*)] \Delta x$
- Moment about x-axis: $\Delta M_{x,i} \approx \frac{f(x_i^*) + g(x_i^*)}{2} [f(x_i^*) - g(x_i^*)] \Delta x = \frac{1}{2}[f(x_i^*)^2 - g(x_i^*)^2] \Delta x$

Taking limits:
$$A = \int_a^b [f(x) - g(x)] \, dx$$
$$\bar{x} = \frac{M_y}{A} = \frac{1}{A} \int_a^b x [f(x) - g(x)] \, dx$$
$$\bar{y} = \frac{M_x}{A} = \frac{1}{2A} \int_a^b [f(x)^2 - g(x)^2] \, dx$$

### Pappus's Centroid Theorems

**Theorem 1 (Volume):** Volume generated by revolving plane region about external axis = Area Ã— Distance travelled by centroid.
*Proof:* $V = \int A(y) \, dy$. By shell method, $V = 2\pi \int \bar{x}(y) \, dA = 2\pi \bar{x} A$ where $\bar{x}$ = distance from centroid to axis.

**Theorem 2 (Surface Area):** Surface area generated by revolving curve about external axis = Arc length Ã— Distance travelled by centroid of curve.
*Proof:* $S = \int 2\pi r \, ds = 2\pi \bar{r} \int ds = 2\pi \bar{r} L$.

### Second Moment of Area
For lamina with density $\rho = 1$:
$$I_x = \iint y^2 \, dA = \int_a^b \int_{g(x)}^{f(x)} y^2 \, dy \, dx = \frac{1}{3} \int_a^b [f(x)^3 - g(x)^3] \, dx$$
$$I_y = \iint x^2 \, dA = \int_a^b x^2 [f(x) - g(x)] \, dx$$

### Parallel Axis Theorem
$$I = I_{cm} + A d^2$$
where $I_{cm}$ = moment about centroidal axis, $d$ = distance between axes.

**Proof:** $I = \iint (y - d)^2 dA = \iint (y^2 - 2dy + d^2) dA = I_{cm} - 2d \iint y dA + d^2 A$. But $\iint y dA = \bar{y} A$, and for centroidal axis $\bar{y} = 0$, so $I = I_{cm} + A d^2$.

### Force on Submerged Vertical Plate
Pressure at depth $h$: $P = \rho g h$.
Force on horizontal strip at depth $h$: $\Delta F \approx P(h) \cdot w(h) \Delta h = \rho g h w(h) \Delta h$
Total force:
$$F = \rho g \int_{h_1}^{h_2} h w(h) \, dh$$

## 9. Key Convergence Conditions

All integrals require integrability:
- **Area/Volume:** $f, g$ continuous on $[a,b]$ (or piecewise continuous)
- **Arc Length:** $f'$ continuous on $[a,b]$
- **Surface Area:** $f'$ continuous, $f(x) \ge 0$
- **Work:** $F(x)$ continuous
- **Centroid:** $f, g$ continuous
