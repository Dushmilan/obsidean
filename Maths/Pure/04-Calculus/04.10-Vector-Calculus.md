# 4.10 Vector Calculus

## Definition

$\mathbf{F}: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^n$ assigns a vector to each point.
- **Gradient field:** $\mathbf{F} = \nabla f$ for some scalar $f$
- **Conservative field:** Line integral path-independent
- **Solenoidal field:** $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F} = 0$ (divergence-free)
- **Irrotational field:** $\nabla \times \mathbf{F} = \mathbf{0}$

## Formulae

### Differential Operators

| Operator | Symbol | Formula | Maps |
|----------|--------|---------|------|
| **Gradient** | $\nabla f$ | $\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\mathbf{i} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\mathbf{j} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial z}\mathbf{k}$ | scalar $\to$ vector |
| **Divergence** | $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F}$ | $\frac{\partial P}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial Q}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial R}{\partial z}$ | vector $\to$ scalar |
| **Curl** | $\nabla \times \mathbf{F}$ | $\begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ \partial_x & \partial_y & \partial_z \\ P & Q & R \end{vmatrix}$ | vector $\to$ vector |
| **Laplacian** | $\nabla^2 f$ | $\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y^2} + \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial z^2}$ | scalar $\to$ scalar |

### Vector Identities

| Identity | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| $\nabla \cdot (\nabla \times \mathbf{F})$ | $0$ |
| $\nabla \times (\nabla f)$ | $\mathbf{0}$ |
| $\nabla(fg)$ | $f\nabla g + g\nabla f$ |
| $\nabla \cdot (f\mathbf{F})$ | $f(\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F}) + \mathbf{F} \cdot \nabla f$ |
| $\nabla \times (f\mathbf{F})$ | $f(\nabla \times \mathbf{F}) + \nabla f \times \mathbf{F}$ |
| $\nabla \cdot (\mathbf{F} \times \mathbf{G})$ | $\mathbf{G} \cdot (\nabla \times \mathbf{F}) - \mathbf{F} \cdot (\nabla \times \mathbf{G})$ |
| $\nabla \times (\nabla \times \mathbf{F})$ | $\nabla(\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F}) - \nabla^2 \mathbf{F}$ |

### Line Integrals

| Type | Formula |
|------|---------|
| **Scalar field** | $\int_C f \, ds = \int_a^b f(\mathbf{r}(t)) \|\mathbf{r}'(t)\| dt$ |
| **Vector field** | $\int_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r} = \int_a^b \mathbf{F}(\mathbf{r}(t)) \cdot \mathbf{r}'(t) dt$ |
| **Work done** | $W = \int_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r}$ |

### Surface Integrals

| Type | Formula |
|------|---------|
| **Scalar** | $\iint_S f \, dS = \iint_D f(\mathbf{r}(u,v)) \|\mathbf{r}_u \times \mathbf{r}_v\| du\,dv$ |
| **Vector (flux)** | $\iint_S \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{S} = \iint_D \mathbf{F}(\mathbf{r}(u,v)) \cdot (\mathbf{r}_u \times \mathbf{r}_v) du\,dv$ |
| **Explicit $z=g(x,y)$** | $\mathbf{n} dS = \langle -g_x, -g_y, 1 \rangle dx\,dy$ (upward) |

### Fundamental Theorems

| Theorem | Formula | Dimension |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| **Fundamental Theorem for Line Integrals** | $\int_C \nabla f \cdot d\mathbf{r} = f(B) - f(A)$ | 1D path |
| **Green's Theorem** | $\oint_C P dx + Q dy = \iint_D (Q_x - P_y) dA$ | 2D region |
| **Stokes' Theorem** | $\oint_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r} = \iint_S (\nabla \times \mathbf{F}) \cdot d\mathbf{S}$ | 3D surface |
| **Divergence Theorem** | $\iiint_V \nabla \cdot \mathbf{F} dV = \iint_{\partial V} \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{S}$ | 3D volume |

### Conservative Field Criteria
$\mathbf{F}$ conservative $\iff \nabla \times \mathbf{F} = \mathbf{0}$ (in simply connected domain)

For $\mathbf{F} = P\mathbf{i} + Q\mathbf{j} + R\mathbf{k}$:
$$\nabla \times \mathbf{F} = \mathbf{0} \iff \frac{\partial R}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial Q}{\partial z}, \quad \frac{\partial P}{\partial z} = \frac{\partial R}{\partial x}, \quad \frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial P}{\partial y}$$

### Finding Potential Function
For $\mathbf{F} = \nabla f$:
1. $\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = P \Rightarrow f = \int P dx + g(y,z)$
2. $\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} = Q \Rightarrow$ differentiate and solve for $g$
3. $\frac{\partial f}{\partial z} = R \Rightarrow$ solve for remaining

### Coordinate Systems

**Cylindrical:**

| Operator | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| $\nabla f$ | $\frac{\partial f}{\partial r}\hat{\mathbf{r}} + \frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial f}{\partial \theta}\hat{\boldsymbol{\theta}} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial z}\hat{\mathbf{z}}$ |
| $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F}$ | $\frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}(r F_r) + \frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial F_\theta}{\partial \theta} + \frac{\partial F_z}{\partial z}$ |

**Spherical:**

| Operator | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| $\nabla f$ | $\frac{\partial f}{\partial \rho}\hat{\boldsymbol{\rho}} + \frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial f}{\partial \theta}\hat{\boldsymbol{\theta}} + \frac{1}{\rho\sin\phi}\frac{\partial f}{\partial \phi}\hat{\boldsymbol{\phi}}$ |
| $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F}$ | $\frac{1}{\rho^2}\frac{\partial}{\partial \rho}(\rho^2 F_\rho) + \frac{1}{\rho\sin\phi}\frac{\partial}{\partial \phi}(\sin\phi F_\phi) + \frac{1}{\rho\sin\phi}\frac{\partial F_\theta}{\partial \theta}$ |

## Properties / Key Concepts

### Green's Theorem Conditions
- $P, Q$ have continuous partials on open region containing $D$
- $D$ is simply connected (no holes)
- $C$ is positively oriented (anticlockwise)

**Area:** $\text{Area}(D) = \oint_C x dy = -\oint_C y dx = \frac{1}{2}\oint_C (x dy - y dx)$

### Stokes' Theorem Conditions
- $S$ piecewise smooth oriented surface
- $\mathbf{F}$ has continuous partials on open region containing $S$
- $C = \partial S$ with orientation given by right-hand rule

### Divergence Theorem Conditions
- $V$ compact region with piecewise smooth boundary
- $\mathbf{F}$ continuously differentiable on open region containing $V$
- Total divergence inside = Net flux out through boundary

### Applications

**Fluid Dynamics:**
- $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{v}$ = source density (expansion rate)
- $\nabla \times \mathbf{v}$ = vorticity (local rotation)

**Maxwell's Equations:**
| Equation | Differential Form |
|----------|-------------------|
| Gauss's Law | $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} = \frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}$ |
| Gauss's Law for Magnetism | $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0$ |
| Faraday's Law | $\nabla \times \mathbf{E} = -\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t}$ |
| Ampère-Maxwell | $\nabla \times \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \mathbf{J} + \mu_0\varepsilon_0 \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t}$ |

**Wave Equation:** $\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} = c^2 \nabla^2 u$

**Heat Equation:** $\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = \alpha \nabla^2 u$

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Check if Conservative
$\mathbf{F} = (2xy + z^2)\mathbf{i} + (x^2 + 2yz)\mathbf{j} + (2xz + y^2)\mathbf{k}$
$Q_x - P_y = 2x - 2x = 0$
$R_y - Q_z = 2y - 2y = 0$
$P_z - R_x = 2z - 2z = 0$
$\nabla \times \mathbf{F} = \mathbf{0} \Rightarrow$ Conservative

Potential: $f = x^2y + xz^2 + y^2z + C$

### Example 2: Green's Theorem
$\oint_C (x^2 - y^2)dx + (2xy)dy$, $C$ boundary of $x^2+y^2 \le 1$
$P = x^2 - y^2$, $Q = 2xy$
$Q_x - P_y = 2y - (-2y) = 4y$
$\iint_D 4y dA = 0$ (odd in $y$, symmetric region)

### Example 3: Stokes' Theorem
$\mathbf{F} = yz\mathbf{i} + xz\mathbf{j} + xy\mathbf{k}$, $C$ boundary of $z = 1 - x^2 - y^2$, $z \ge 0$
$\nabla \times \mathbf{F} = \mathbf{0}$
$\oint_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r} = 0$

### Example 4: Divergence Theorem
$\mathbf{F} = x\mathbf{i} + y\mathbf{j} + z\mathbf{k}$, sphere $x^2+y^2+z^2 = a^2$
$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F} = 3$
$\iiint_V 3 dV = 3 \cdot \frac{4}{3}\pi a^3 = 4\pi a^3$

## Common Traps

| Trap | ❌ Wrong | Correct |
|------|-------|---------|
| Conservative check | Only 2D curl | Need all 3 components in 3D |
| Green's orientation | Clockwise | Anticlockwise (positive) |
| Stokes' orientation | Random | Right-hand rule |
| Divergence theorem | Open surface | Must be closed surface |
| Surface normal | Wrong direction | Outward for divergence, consistent with boundary for Stokes |
| Parametrisation | Wrong Jacobian | Compute $\|\mathbf{r}_u \times \mathbf{r}_v\|$ carefully |
| Potential function | Forget arbitrary function | Add $g(y,z)$ after integrating wrt $x$ |
